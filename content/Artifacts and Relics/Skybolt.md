# Skybolt


> [!side]
> :   ![[skybolt.png]]

*As recorded by Linzi, Minister of Communication of Thumping Waters*

Skybolt is quieter than people expect.

That surprises those who know its name, who imagine thunder and spectacle and arrows wrapped in lightning. In truth, Skybolt’s presence is felt most strongly in the pauses between motion—in the way the air seems to listen when it is lifted, in the subtle pressure that gathers when the string is drawn. The storm does not rush to it. The storm waits.

The bow is ancient, its curve shaped with the deliberate grace of centaur craftsmanship. It has passed through unworthy hands and been returned, and it remembers the difference. Skybolt does not forget who treats it as a trophy and who treats it as a trust.

In Ant’s hands, Skybolt behaves less like a weapon and more like a conversation with the wind.

When he draws, the air tightens, attentive. Arrows fly not with brute force, but with agreement, guided by unseen currents that correct their path without violence. There is no flash, no warning thunder. The wind simply decides to be helpful, and the shot completes itself.

Skybolt protects as readily as it strikes. When danger comes from afar, the air thickens around Ant, turning aside missiles with quiet offense, as though the wind resents being used against someone it has already chosen to favor. This protection feels less like armor and more like etiquette—the sky refusing discourtesy.

At times, Skybolt answers more fully. The wind gathers with purpose, lifting Ant from the ground and carrying him above the world with the same impartial strength that bears storms and migrating birds alike. From that height, problems shrink. Paths clarify. It becomes easy—too easy—to believe that elevation is wisdom rather than advantage.

Water listens to Skybolt as well. Rain gathers at the bow’s tips, drawn into long, snapping currents used to disarm, unbalance, and disrupt rather than kill. Ant favors these moments. He has learned that Skybolt responds better when force is applied with restraint, when victory does not require annihilation.

This is where Skybolt’s influence becomes apparent.

It does not whisper like Ovinrbaane, nor does it intrude upon thought like the Oculus of Abaddon. It teaches through repetition. Each use reinforces the same quiet lessons: that distance is authority, that height is safety, that storms resolve conflict quickly. None of these lessons are false. That is what makes them dangerous.

Ant is careful. He listens. He grounds himself before drawing the string, as if reminding both himself and the bow that control is not the same as entitlement. Skybolt answers him because he asks, not because he demands. The wind, I have learned, is very particular about that distinction.

Skybolt is not a relic of conquest.

It is a borrowed strength, offered once, and still watching to see how it will be used.

The storm does not belong to Ant.

But, for now, it answers him.

## Mechanics

### Skybolt

*An ancient longbow of the Rashalka, returned to the Nomen Centaurs and given in trust to the Tubthumpers.*

**Item Level** 15
**Rarity** Unique
**Traits** Magical, Evocation, Air, Water, Relic
**Usage** held in two hands
**Bulk** 1
**Base Weapon** Composite Longbow

Skybolt is an ancient centaur relic bound to wind, rain, and open sky. It does not dominate the elements—it requests their aid. When wielded with restraint, the air listens. When used recklessly, the storm grows distant.

#### Weapon Statistics

**Damage** 2d8 piercing
**Range Increment** 100 feet
**Reload** 0
**Group** Bow

#### Fundamental Runes

- +2 potency
- Striking
- Thundering

#### Relic Gifts

Skybolt has manifested the following relic gifts. These abilities function only while Skybolt is wielded.

##### Wind Barrier

**Activate** one action (concentrate)

An invisible barrier of air deflects ranged attacks until the beginning of your next turn.

- You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against ranged attacks.
- At 13th level, this bonus increases to +3.

##### Propelling Winds

**Activate** two actions (concentrate, manipulate)
**Frequency** once per hour

Skybolt calls upon the wind to lift its bearer. The relic casts **fly** targeting you.

The movement feels smooth and cooperative rather than forceful, as though the air is carrying you rather than being commanded.

##### Lashing Currents

**Activate** Interact

Water gathers at the tip of Skybolt, allowing you to adjust your grip and make Strikes with flowing currents instead of arrows.

While in this stance, you can make melee weapon Strikes using lashing currents with the following properties:

- Damage 1d4 bludgeoning
- Traits disarm, finesse, reach 10 feet, trip, versatile S
- Weapon Group flail

These Strikes gain the benefit of Skybolt’s fundamental and property runes. Property runes that would not logically apply do not function.

You can use another Interact action to return to Skybolt’s normal grip and make ranged Strikes.

#### Relic Nature

Skybolt responds best to deliberate use. It does not grant its full power to wielders who treat it as a blunt instrument of destruction. The GM may suppress or weaken relic gifts temporarily if Skybolt is used in a way that violates the spirit of its stewardship.
