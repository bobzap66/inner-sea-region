# Crafting

The authoritative Pathfinder 2e crafting rules are maintained on Archives of Nethys rather than duplicated here.

[View Item Crafting on Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2452)

## Campaign Notes

For this campaign, use the current Archives of Nethys rules when determining setup time, formulas, raw materials, Crafting DCs, batches, rune transfers, or other mechanical details. Older examples formerly kept on this page were written against an earlier version of the crafting rules and should not be treated as authoritative.

When a crafting question is specific to the campaign—such as whether a formula is available, whether a workshop exists in a settlement, or what unusual materials the party has recovered—that information belongs in the relevant character, settlement, treasure, or session article.
