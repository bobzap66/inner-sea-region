# Kingdom Turn Rules

This page is a **Kingmaker campaign quick reference**. The full published kingdom rules are maintained on Archives of Nethys rather than duplicated here.

[Open the Kingmaker kingdom rules on Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=1781)

## Turn Sequence

A Kingdom turn is resolved in four phases:

1. **Upkeep** — leadership, Unrest, resources, and Consumption.
2. **Commerce** — taxes, expenses, commodities, and trade agreements.
3. **Activity** — Leadership, Region, Civic, and—when relevant—Army activities.
4. **Event** — random or story events, kingdom XP, and level advancement.

[Open the Commerce Phase on Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=1800)

The navigation on the Archives of Nethys kingdom-rules pages links directly between the Upkeep, Commerce, Activity, and Event phases.
