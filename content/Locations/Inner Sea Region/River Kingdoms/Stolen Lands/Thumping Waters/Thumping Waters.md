The Republic of Thumping Waters is a young but expansive nation of the [[Stolen Lands]]. Founded by the adventurers known as the Tubthumpers, it grew from a wilderness charter and a handful of frightened settlements into a republic stretching from the [[Tuskwater]] basin to [[Varnhold]] and the western marches around [[Fort Drelev]].

Its history has been unusually eventful even by the standards of the [[River Kingdoms]]. Bandit lords, trolls, cyclopes, barbarian armies, hostile cults, supernatural Blooms, and the armies of [[Pitax]] have all attempted to end the Republic. None succeeded. The result is a country whose institutions were built during emergencies, whose ministers are frequently also its most dangerous adventurers, and whose citizens have learned to regard survival as a civic tradition.

## Government

Thumping Waters is governed by a council of eight ministries. Each minister is responsible for one part of the Republic’s civil administration, while the Prime Minister coordinates the council and acts as head of government. The offices were adapted from the positions recommended in the original kingdom-building charter, but the Tubthumpers gave them names that better reflected the nation they intended to build.

The title *Republic* is more than decoration. Leadership is treated as public service rather than hereditary property. Ministers may resign, be replaced, or move between offices as circumstances demand. The system has survived departures, deaths, prolonged expeditions, wartime emergencies, and at least one administrative crisis caused by a printing press.

## History

### A Charter in the Wilderness

Thumping Waters began with an Aldori exploration charter. The Tubthumpers were charged with mapping part of the Stolen Lands, protecting travelers, and ending the banditry that had made lasting settlement nearly impossible. Their early campaign carried them from Oleg’s Trading Post through the Narlmarches and Kamelands and culminated in the defeat of the [[Stag Lord]] at his fort beside the Tuskwater.

With the Stag Lord dead and the region’s roads beginning to reopen, the company received authority to establish a new realm. The Republic of Thumping Waters was founded on 1 Desnus, 4710 AR, with Thumpington as its capital.

### A Republic of Many Peoples

Expansion did not proceed solely through construction or conquest. Communities joined the Republic through rescue, negotiation, settlement, and pledged fealty. Oleg’s isolated trading post became the heart of [[Olegton]]. The [[Sootscale Kobolds]] entered the Republic and built [[Sootscale Valley]]. [[Greengripe]]’s goblins, the bog striders of [[Tok-Nikrat]], and the [[Nomen Centaurs]] all became part of the nation without surrendering the identities that made their communities distinct.

That principle has not always been easy to defend. The Republic has endured prejudice between neighboring peoples, disputes over public spending, religious agitation, and violence directed at newly incorporated citizens. Its history is not a story of effortless harmony. It is a record of repeatedly deciding that citizenship must mean the same thing when it becomes inconvenient.

### Wars, Vanishings, and Blooms

The first years of Thumping Waters were marked by a succession of threats. King [[Hargulka]]’s troll forces tested the young nation’s defenses. The disappearance of Varnhold led the Tubthumpers into the tomb-city of the cyclops lich Vordakai. The liberation of Fort Drelev drew the Republic into conflict with the [[Tiger Lords]] and [[Armag the Twice-Born]]. Repeated Blooms brought impossible creatures and fragments of the First World into settled lands.

Each crisis changed the country. Varnhold was incorporated into the Republic. Fort Drelev pledged fealty after the fall of Baron [[Hannis Drelev]] and began the long work of recovery. New roads, forts, farms, temples, theaters, schools, and workshops tied distant communities into a nation whose borders had once existed mostly as hopeful lines on a surveyor’s map.

### The War of the River Kings

King Castruccio Irovetti of Pitax eventually launched an undeclared war against Thumping Waters, striking through agents, monsters, sabotage, and invading armies. The conflict cost the Republic soldiers, settlements, and beloved public figures, including [[Nok-Nok]], Governor of Greengripe, who fell fighting to protect his troops.

The Tubthumpers carried the war into Pitax, exposed the forces behind Irovetti’s rule, and defeated him beneath his own palace. Thumping Waters did not simply annex the conquered city. A provisional administration was established while Pitax began disentangling its civic institutions from Irovetti’s court. The Republic entered 4715 AR victorious, exhausted, and increasingly aware that winning a war is much faster than repairing what it broke.

### The Years Between

The months following the war brought quieter changes of lasting importance. Mairi returned to Thumpington after freeing the spirit bound within Amiri’s sword and retired from public adventuring. Seamus and Linzi were married on 21 Rova, 4715 AR, before the people of the Republic. Research continued beneath Candlemere, where an ancient Pharasmian seal conceals chambers associated with figures named Foras and Korog.

The respite did not last. During a winter survey of the [[Glenebon]] Uplands, the ministers sighted Minognos-Ushad, the legendary Eater of Kings, and encountered a fey hunter preparing the land for the Wild Hunt. The encounter ended with the hunter warning that the fey would no longer offer Thumping Waters mercy. Soon afterward, a leshy named Lurk reported that the Wild Hunt was approaching and claimed that the seal beneath Candlemere had begun to fail.

As of 4 Abadius, 4716 AR, the Republic is strengthening its defenses and researching the Candlemere seal. Lurk’s claim has not been independently confirmed, nor has any connection between the Wild Hunt and whatever lies beneath the island.

## Territory and Communities

The Republic’s holdings cover a broad and geographically varied portion of the Stolen Lands. Its core lies around Thumpington and the Tuskwater, but its roads and obligations now extend north toward Olegton, east through Varnhold, and west into the lands administered from Fort Drelev.

**Principal settlements and holdings include:** [[Thumpington]], [[Olegton]], [[Tatzlford]], [[Sootscale Valley]], [[Greengripe]], [[Varnhold]], [[Fort Drelev]], [[Fort Tuskwater]], [[Fort Serenko]], [[Embeth Hall]], [[Willowfen]], [[Longtail Island]], and [[Tok-Nikrat]].

The Nomen centaurs have also pledged fealty to the Republic while retaining their own leadership and traditions. Across so large a territory, political membership does not always mean identical local government. Thumping Waters often relies upon existing chiefs, governors, councils, and community customs, provided they recognize the Republic’s laws and the rights of its citizens.

## National Character

Thumping Waters is defined less by a single ancestry or old noble lineage than by the decision to remain. Its people include settlers, refugees, traders, soldiers, former bandits, kobolds, goblins, bog striders, centaurs, and adventurers who discovered that clearing a monster from the wilderness occasionally leads to responsibility for the nearest bridge.

The Republic values practical achievement. A person who can defend a village, repair a road, negotiate a trade agreement, expose a corrupt official, or keep a printing press operating is likely to be treated with respect regardless of birth. This has produced a culture that can be energetic, irreverent, argumentative, and intensely proud of institutions that did not exist a few years earlier.

The completion of Thumpington’s printing press and the founding of *The Lantern and Ledger* marked an important change in national life. News, public notices, advertisements, obituaries, and official accounts could at last circulate beyond the council chamber and the capital’s taverns. For a country created by people who frequently vanish into the wilderness for weeks at a time, a reliable public record has proven particularly valuable.

## Foreign Relations

Thumping Waters maintains close historical ties with [[Restov]] and the Aldori interests that issued its founding charter, though the Republic has long since developed interests and responsibilities of its own. It has established diplomatic or commercial relations with distant states including Galt, Hymbria, and Outsea.

Its relationship with Pitax remains the defining foreign question of the present day. Irovetti is dead and the war is over, but the work of reconstruction, accountability, and stable local government continues. Relations with the Tiger Lords are equally complicated: Amiri now leads many of the surviving tribes, but shared enemies and old friendships have not erased every grievance.

To the north and east, Brevoy’s instability remains impossible to ignore. To the west, the scars of war remain visible. Beyond every border lie older powers that do not recognize charters, ministries, or carefully surveyed roads. The Republic has therefore learned to practice diplomacy with one hand while keeping the other near a weapon.

## Symbols and Public Life

The flag of Thumping Waters is bright blue with a broad yellow diagonal stripe edged in cyan. An eight-pointed yellow star occupies the lower field. The national anthem recounts the Republic’s unlikely beginnings and the dangers survived by its founders, transforming what began as a company of adventurers into part of the country’s shared civic memory.

Public life in Thumping Waters is rarely solemn for long. Festivals, theaters, unusual shops, competing taverns, religious processions, visiting performers, and political arguments all crowd together in its settlements. The same Republic that mobilizes armies against trolls may spend the following month debating municipal laundry magic or the proper preparation of a roc-egg omelette. Both matters will be reported in the newspaper.
