# Ministry of Justice

![](../../../../../../assets/images/shared-cover-48fb79abc6f1.png)

*An institutional profile maintained by the Office of Public Record*

The Ministry of Justice administers the laws of the Republic of Thumping Waters. It oversees national courts, legal procedure, warrants, appeals, mediation, and the enforcement of judgments while protecting citizens from the arbitrary exercise of government power.

The ministry is led by [Matteo](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/Characters/Matteo.md) Auditore. Its founding minister was [Ekundayo](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/NPCs/Ekundayo.md), who established the office when the Republic's law consisted of a charter, a council, and an alarming number of situations the charter had not anticipated.

### Mandate

The Ministry of Justice maintains the Republic's legal code, supervises national courts, trains magistrates and clerks, issues or reviews warrants, and establishes procedures for criminal and civil cases. It also mediates disputes among settlements, ministries, guilds, communities, and individuals when a negotiated resolution remains possible.

Justice is distinct from intelligence and defense. Intelligence may uncover a threat, and Defense may confront an armed enemy, but neither ministry can transform suspicion into criminal guilt. The Ministry of Justice exists in part to prevent necessity, fear, or official confidence from becoming substitutes for evidence and procedure.

### Offices and Services

#### Office of Courts and Magistrates

This office coordinates court schedules, appoints or recognizes magistrates, maintains clerks and bailiffs, and ensures that cases can be heard outside the capital. Local leaders and customary authorities continue to resolve many ordinary matters, particularly in communities that joined the Republic with established traditions of their own.

National courts intervene when a case crosses settlement boundaries, concerns national law, involves a ministry, or cannot be resolved fairly under local authority.

#### Office of Code and Precedent

The Office of Code and Precedent drafts statutes, records judicial decisions, reviews proposed council measures, and advises ministries about the legal limits of their plans. It is responsible for making the law consistent enough to govern distant communities without pretending that every dispute is identical.

Translations and explanatory editions are prepared with the [Ministry of Communication](Ministry%20of%20Communication.md) so that citizenship does not depend upon reading the preferred language of the capital.

#### Office of Warrants and Judicial Enforcement

This office works with local constables, marshals, bailiffs, and settlement authorities to serve warrants, secure prisoners, recover property, and carry out court orders. It also investigates allegations that officers have exceeded their lawful authority.

Military assistance may be requested when enforcement involves organized violence or an exceptional threat. Soldiers acting in support of a warrant remain subject to its terms and to review afterward.

#### Office of Mediation and Appeals

Mediators seek settlements in property disputes, commercial disagreements, boundary conflicts, and disagreements between local and national authorities. The appeals service allows a judgment to be reviewed when procedure was defective, law was applied incorrectly, or new evidence materially changes the case.

### Development of Republican Law

The Republic learned early that an enemy can be dangerous without being easily chargeable. When the agitator [Grigori](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/NPCs/Grigori.md) spread unrest through Thumpington, the government could not lawfully punish him merely for being persuasive and intolerable. His eventual arrest required provable conduct rather than official dislike. The case became an early lesson in the difference between protecting a government and protecting the law.

Expansion created further complications. Kobold, goblin, iruxi, bog strider, and centaur communities brought their own leaders, customs, obligations, and methods of resolving disputes. The ministry's task has not been to erase those systems. It establishes the national rights and procedures that no local custom may violate while recognizing local authority wherever possible.

### The Minister

Matteo Auditore succeeded Ekundayo as Minister of Justice. His public responsibilities include chairing major legal reviews, advising the council on procedure, supervising cases involving national officials, and determining when disputes belong before a court rather than another ministry.

This article does not presume to define Matteo's personal philosophy beyond his recorded decisions. Under his tenure, the ministry has emphasized written procedure, reviewable judgments, access to mediation, and clear boundaries between investigation and conviction.

### Relations with Other Ministries

Justice reviews council measures with the Prime Minister's office and publishes laws through Communication. It works with Intelligence on espionage, corruption, and covert networks while maintaining oversight of warrants and detention. Defense assists when civil enforcement faces military-scale resistance. Magic provides expertise in cases involving enchantment, curses, magical evidence, and supernatural contracts. Treasury supplies auditors in fraud and public-corruption cases. Interior coordinates local officials, prisons, and courts across distant settlements.

### Public Experience

Most citizens encounter the ministry through a local magistrate, property record, warrant, contract dispute, or petition for review. The ministry's reputation depends less upon dramatic trials than upon whether ordinary cases can be heard without wealth, ancestry, or proximity to the capital deciding the result in advance.

Petitions alleging an unjust local judgment may be submitted directly to the ministry. Filing a petition does not guarantee reversal. It does guarantee that “the mayor said so” is not treated as a complete statement of law.
