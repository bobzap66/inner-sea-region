# Ministry of the Treasury

![](../../../../../../assets/images/shared-cover-48fb79abc6f1.png)

*An institutional profile maintained by the Office of Public Record*

The Ministry of the Treasury manages the money, property, obligations, and financial records of the Republic of Thumping Waters. It prepares national budgets, collects revenue, supervises tariffs and public contracts, audits government accounts, and determines whether ambitious civic plans possess resources in addition to enthusiasm.

[Jubilost Narthropple](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/NPCs/Jubilost%20Narthropple.md) has led the ministry since the founding of the Republic.

### Mandate

The Treasury records public income and expenditure, prepares budgets for council approval, collects national taxes and customs duties, manages reserves, pays government obligations, supervises major contracts, and evaluates the financial consequences of proposed policy.

The ministry does not decide national priorities by itself. It tells the council what those priorities cost, identifies which revenues are actually available, and records which future obligations are being created when present enthusiasm exceeds present coin.

### Offices and Services

#### Office of the Exchequer and Budget

This office maintains the Republic's central accounts, prepares regular financial statements, and assembles the budget proposed to the council. It tracks commitments across multiple years so that a road, army, or public institution does not become inexplicably unaffordable halfway through construction.

#### Office of Revenue and Customs

Revenue officers administer national taxes, tariffs, tolls, and other lawful charges. Local governments may collect their own revenues under settlement charters, while the ministry establishes how national obligations are assessed and transferred.

Customs officers work at major roads, river ports, and trade centers. Their task is to collect what the law requires without turning every arriving merchant into a smuggler through delay and confusion.

#### Office of Accounts and Audit

Auditors inspect ministry accounts, local transfers, public inventories, and major contracts. They investigate missing funds, duplicate payments, false claims, and projects whose reported completion cannot be located upon the ground.

The office's most famous early irregularity involved [Linzi](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/NPCs/Linzi.md)'s unauthorized use of treasury funds to purchase a printing press. The expenditure produced a valuable national institution and nearly got her teacher sacrificed by a coven of hags. Treasury training materials cite the incident as proof that a useful result does not retroactively create lawful authorization.

#### Office of Trade and Public Contracts

This office negotiates and records trade agreements, manages major procurement, reviews bids, and advises merchants regarding national rules. It coordinates with foreign partners and settlement governments when a route, resource, or contract crosses several jurisdictions.

Public contracts are recorded so that materials, labor, deadlines, and payment remain identifiable after everyone involved has developed a different memory of the agreement.

### The Minister

Jubilost Narthropple brought experience as an explorer, scholar, and organizer to a treasury established before the Republic possessed a mature tax system. Under his tenure, the office developed standardized accounts capable of following the nation's expansion across distant settlements and through repeated emergencies.

His public record is one of exacting review, persistent questioning, and little patience for arithmetic presented as optimism. The ministry's institutional value does not depend upon every applicant enjoying the experience of being asked where the money will come from.

### War and Reconstruction

The War of the River Kings placed extraordinary demands upon the Treasury. Armies required wages, food, equipment, transport, fortifications, and replacement animals while settlements simultaneously lost trade, labor, buildings, and crops.

Postwar work now includes veteran payments, reconstruction grants, reopened trade routes, recovery of abandoned property, and financial coordination with [Pitax](../Settlements/Pitax/Pitax.md)'s provisional government. The Treasury has argued that victory cannot be considered paid for while widows, damaged settlements, and unpaid suppliers remain outside the ledger.

### Relations with Other Ministries

Every ministry submits budgets and accounts to the Treasury. Interior depends upon it for construction and settlement funding. Defense accounts for armies, forts, and military supply. Magic requests rare materials and secure facilities. Communication requires paper, ink, couriers, and a continuing explanation for why illustrations are not free. Justice assists with fraud, seizure, and contract enforcement. Intelligence tracks smuggling, hostile finance, and corruption. The Prime Minister coordinates disputes when several necessary projects cannot all be funded at once.

### Public Experience

Citizens most often encounter the Treasury through taxes, customs, licenses, public payments, contracts, or grants. Its legitimacy depends upon charges being understandable, predictable, and applied under law rather than improvised according to who is collecting them.

The ministry publishes summaries of national revenue and expenditure through the [Ministry of Communication](Ministry%20of%20Communication.md). Detailed security accounts and active military or intelligence spending may remain restricted, but they are still subject to internal audit.
