# Office of the Prime Minister

![](../../../../../../assets/images/shared-cover-48fb79abc6f1.png)

*An institutional profile maintained by the Office of Public Record*

The Office of the Prime Minister coordinates the civil government of the Republic of Thumping Waters. It sets priorities, convenes the Ruling Council, resolves disputes among ministries, and ensures that national decisions become assignments, budgets, orders, and completed work rather than a collection of strongly expressed preferences.

Ally Grainger currently serves as Prime Minister. [Mairi](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/Characters/Mairi.md) founded the office and led the Republic through its earliest years before retiring from public adventuring and government leadership.

### Mandate

The Prime Minister chairs the Ruling Council, receives reports from every ministry, coordinates policies that cross ministerial boundaries, and represents the Republic in major matters of state. The office may issue executive direction within the authority granted by law and the Republic's charters.

The Prime Minister is neither a monarch nor the personal owner of the government. The office leads a council-based republic and depends upon the ministries, courts, settlement governments, and public institutions that carry policy into practice.

### Offices and Services

#### Executive Secretariat

The secretariat schedules council meetings, circulates agendas, records decisions, tracks assignments, prepares executive correspondence, and follows unresolved disputes until the parties either reach agreement or admit that they require a decision.

#### Policy and Coordination Staff

This staff reviews proposals involving several ministries, identifies conflicts in authority or resources, and prepares options for the Prime Minister and council. Its work is most visible in settlement expansion, national construction, diplomacy, military mobilization, and large magical projects.

#### Crisis Coordination Office

During war, disaster, plague, magical emergency, or other national crisis, this office brings together information and personnel from across the government. It does not replace the responsible ministries; it keeps them working toward the same objective and prevents three urgent responses from competing for the same bridge, healer, or wagon.

### History of the Office

Mairi served as the Republic's founding Prime Minister, when the office's authority rested as much upon the confidence of the other founders as upon formal procedure. Her government established the first ministries, settlements, and institutions from which the modern Republic developed.

Ally moved from the [Ministry of Magic](Ministry%20of%20Magic.md) into the prime ministership. [Tristian](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/NPCs/Tristian.md) succeeded her as Minister of Magic, allowing the executive office to retain magical expertise without requiring its holder to direct two institutions at once.

### The Prime Minister and the Council

The Prime Minister leads the council but does not make every ministerial decision. Ministers remain responsible for their own departments and for the accuracy of the advice they give. The Prime Minister determines national priorities, resolves conflicts that cannot be settled below the council level, and accepts responsibility for the government's overall direction.

Emergency authority permits speed, not secrecy without record. Orders issued during a crisis are preserved by the Office of Public Record and remain subject to later legal, financial, and political review.

### Public Contact

Petitions to the Prime Minister may be submitted through the Executive Secretariat or a local government. Matters belonging to a single ministry are normally referred there, while issues affecting several settlements, multiple ministries, or the constitutional operation of the Republic remain with the executive office.
