# Ministry of Magic

![[shared-cover-48fb79abc6f1.png]]

*An institutional profile maintained by the Office of Public Record*

The Ministry of Magic advises the Republic of Thumping Waters on supernatural threats and coordinates the safe public use of arcane, divine, occult, and primal power. It maintains magical infrastructure, supervises dangerous rituals undertaken by the government, investigates unusual phenomena, and provides expert assistance to other ministries and settlements.

[[Tristian]] currently leads the ministry. [[Ally Grainger]] founded the office and served as Minister of Magic until she became Prime Minister.

### Mandate

The ministry studies magical threats, maintains government wards and teleportation facilities, inspects hazardous magical works, supervises large rituals, and advises the council whenever ordinary law or engineering encounters extraordinary reality.

It does not claim ownership of every spell cast within the Republic. Household magic, private worship, ordinary healing, personal study, and lawful magical trades are not treated as state property. Oversight becomes necessary when magic creates a public hazard, crosses property or settlement boundaries, alters unwilling minds, opens planar passages, affects essential infrastructure, or involves objects and entities capable of resisting ordinary control.

### Offices and Services

#### Office of Research and Phenomena

This office collects reports of curses, Blooms, planar disturbances, strange creatures, and unexplained magical effects. Scholars compare witness testimony with recovered records and field examinations before assigning a cause. When no cause can be established, the uncertainty is recorded rather than replaced with a more satisfying guess.

Current research interests include the First World, [[Wild Hunt]] activity, ancient cyclops magic, Pharasmian structures beneath Candlemere, and the interaction of powerful artifacts with their bearers.

The office exchanges records and expertise with the Free University's College of the Four Traditions. Ministry personnel may provide field access, containment, threat assessments, or restricted evidence, while university scholars conduct independent study and review. Neither institution may present the other's conclusions as its own without attribution.

#### Office of Rituals and Public Works

Government rituals affecting roads, bridges, defenses, agriculture, communications, or whole settlements require technical review and supervised execution. The office verifies components, identifies likely backlash, and ensures that someone other than the ritual's designer has considered what happens if it succeeds incorrectly.

The office also supports permanent teleportation circles, warded facilities, magical beacons, and other installations maintained for public use.

#### Office of Artifacts and Containment

The Republic has encountered intelligent weapons, cursed relics, mass-compulsion devices, and objects whose destruction is considerably more difficult than their discovery. This office records known properties, recommends storage or handling procedures, and coordinates secure transport when an artifact cannot safely remain where it was found.

Registration in the ministry's restricted artifact ledger does not imply government ownership. It records a danger the council may need to understand even when the object remains in private hands or has left the Republic.

#### Office of Magical Assistance

Settlements may request help with failed wards, magical contamination, unexplained curses, dangerous creatures, or disputes requiring expert examination. The office maintains lists of qualified practitioners and may arrange aid from temples, independent spellcasters, druids, alchemists, or specialists outside the Republic.

### History of the Ministry

The Ministry of Magic was created in a country where supernatural events were never theoretical. The [[Stolen Lands]] contained ancient wards, will-o'-wisps, cursed tombs, hostile fey, forgotten cyclops workings, and recurring Blooms before the Republic built its first road.

Under Ally Grainger, the ministry developed from a single minister's expertise into a network of consultants and records capable of supporting settlements across the Republic. Investigations surrounding Vordakai, Candlemere, the First World, and dangerous artifacts greatly expanded its responsibilities.

When [[Mairi]] left office, Ally became Prime Minister and Tristian assumed responsibility for magical affairs. The transfer separated the ministry's technical work from the expanding duties of head of government without severing the Prime Minister's long familiarity with magical policy.

### The Minister

Tristian directs research priorities, advises the council during supernatural emergencies, and determines when a proposed magical project requires formal supervision. His experience with divine magic is particularly important in a ministry whose duties cannot be divided neatly into arcane and religious categories.

Public records describe his administration through its work rather than through claims about his private beliefs: supervised rituals, maintained wards, coordinated specialists, and written assessments that distinguish verified danger from suspicion.

### Relations with Other Ministries

Magic supports Defense with battlefield wards and supernatural threat analysis, Interior with magical infrastructure and regional hazards, Intelligence with counter-divination and secure communications, Justice with expert evidence, Communication with verified public warnings, and Treasury with the cost and custody of rare materials. The Prime Minister's office coordinates responses when a magical event affects several ministries at once, which is most of them.

The ministry's principal academic relationship is with the College of the Four Traditions. Their joint concerns include research safety, magical ethics, dangerous artifacts, the First World, the Wild Hunt, and the Candlemere seal. The ministry retains authority over immediate public hazards and government operations; the college retains authority over its teaching, scholarly review, and publications. The Candlemere Studies project draws upon ministry records but is published through the university rather than issued as an official threat assessment.

### Public Experience

Citizens most often benefit from the ministry through wards that remain intact, hazards identified before construction begins, curses removed without spectacle, and specialists sent to problems no local official can reasonably solve.

The ministry encourages prompt reporting of unusual magical effects. Citizens are specifically advised not to carry an unidentified whispering object to the capital in an ordinary grain sack unless instructed to do so by someone prepared to explain why the grain sack is sufficient.

> Magic does not become safe because it is useful, and it does not become wicked because it is frightening. Our work begins where either assumption would be easier than examination.  
> —Ministry training text
