# Ruling Council

*An institutional profile maintained by the Office of Public Record.*

> **Seat of Government:** [Thumpington](../Settlements/Thumpington/Thumpington.md)
>
> **Membership:** The Prime Minister and seven ministers
>
> **Primary Function:** National policy, executive coordination, crisis response, and resolution of inter-ministerial matters

The Ruling Council is the executive and administrative forum of the Republic of [Thumping Waters](../Thumping%20Waters.md). It brings the Prime Minister together with the seven ministers responsible for Communication, Defense, Intelligence, Justice, Magic, the Interior, and the Treasury.

Each member directs a distinct public institution. The council exists because roads require budgets, wars require information, laws require publication, magical hazards ignore administrative boundaries, and no ministry can govern the Republic by itself.

## From Charter Offices to Ministries

The founders received a familiar set of charter offices but gave them names suited to the government they intended to build.

- **Ruler** became **Prime Minister**.
- **Counselor** became **Minister of Communication**.
- **General** became **Minister of Defense**.
- **Emissary** became **Minister of Intelligence**.
- **Warden** became **Minister of Justice**.
- **Magister** became **Minister of Magic**.
- **Viceroy** became **Minister of the Interior**.
- **Treasurer** became **Minister of the Treasury**.

## Purpose and Authority

The council establishes national priorities, coordinates work involving several ministries, receives reports, resolves jurisdictional disputes, and directs the government's response to major threats and opportunities. Ministers retain responsibility for ordinary decisions within their departments, while matters affecting the Republic as a whole come before the council.

The council is not a court of nobles. Membership follows public office rather than inheritance, household, or title. A change of minister changes the council's membership without dissolving the ministry or transferring its records into the departing officeholder's personal possession.

## Meetings and Records

The council meets in [Thumpington](../Settlements/Thumpington/Thumpington.md) and may be convened elsewhere when travel, diplomacy, war, or disaster makes the capital impractical. The Prime Minister chairs ordinary and emergency sessions. Ministries submit reports and proposals through the Executive Secretariat so that members may identify the decisions and resources required.

The [Ministry of Communication](Ministry%20of%20Communication.md) preserves minutes, orders, proclamations, and the public portion of council business. Sensitive military, intelligence, diplomatic, legal, or magical material may be placed in restricted annexes, but it remains part of the official record.

## Changes in Membership

The present council reflects several transitions since the Republic's founding. [Mairi](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/Characters/Mairi.md) was succeeded as Prime Minister by [Ally Grainger](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/Characters/Ally%20Grainger.md). [Tristian](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/NPCs/Tristian.md) succeeded Ally at Magic. [Ant](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/Characters/Ant.md) and [Seamus](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/Characters/Seamus.md) exchanged the Intelligence and Interior portfolios. [Matteo](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/Characters/Matteo.md) succeeded [Ekundayo](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/NPCs/Ekundayo.md) at Justice. Defense passed from [Amiri](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/NPCs/Amiri.md) to [Fitzroy](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/Characters/Fitzroy.md) Fulgrim III and then to Ekundayo after Fitzroy resigned and returned to [Brevoy](../../../../Brevoy/Brevoy.md).

[Linzi](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/NPCs/Linzi.md) and [Jubilost Narthropple](../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/NPCs/Jubilost%20Narthropple.md) remain in the offices they established at the Republic's founding.

## Public Accountability

Council decisions are carried out under law, through appropriated resources, and by institutions whose records survive their current leaders. Citizens may petition individual ministries, the Prime Minister, local governments, or the courts according to the nature of the issue.

Public records are available through the Ministry of Communication except where lawful restriction protects an active operation, a vulnerable person, a confidential source, or the security of the Republic. Financial secrecy does not prevent Treasury audit, and operational secrecy does not remove judicial or executive oversight.
