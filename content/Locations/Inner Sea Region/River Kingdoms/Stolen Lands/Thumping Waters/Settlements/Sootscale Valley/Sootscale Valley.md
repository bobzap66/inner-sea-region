# Sootscale Valley


### Sootscale Valley

Carved into the cliffs of the southern [[Locations/Inner Sea Region/River Kingdoms/Stolen Lands/Greenbelt|Greenbelt]], **Sootscale Valley** is the proud heart of kobold life within the Republic of Thumping Waters. Once a network of crude tunnels and smoky warrens, it now stands as a bustling town of iron, innovation, and redemption. Under the leadership of **Governor [[Kereek]]**, a former spy turned statesman, Sootscale Valley has become both a symbol of reconciliation and a forge of progress—its forges blazing day and night, lighting the hills with the glow of new beginnings.

#### Origins

Kereek’s rise to prominence is one of the more unusual stories in the republic’s history. Only a few years ago, he and his companion **[[Prazil]]** were agents of [[Hargulka]]’s monstrous horde, sent north to spy on the newly formed kingdom of Thumping Waters. The Tubthumpers captured them easily—but instead of treachery, they found desperation. The pair revealed that they spied only to protect their kin, held hostage by Hargulka’s forces deep in the hills. With their guidance, the heroes infiltrated Hargulka’s keep and ended his reign of tyranny. When the smoke cleared, Kereek and Prazil stood beside the victors, not as prisoners, but as allies.

After the battle, Kereek and Prazil led the surviving kobolds of Hargulka’s domain to the protection of the Sootscale tribe, whose mines had already been liberated by the Tubthumpers in earlier years. What began as a cautious merger of two wary groups quickly became a brotherhood. Kereek’s intellect and sense of duty won the respect of [[Chief Sootscale]], who recognized in him the rare combination of cunning and conscience that could guide their people into the future. In time, when the aging chief retired, Kereek was named his successor and appointed **Governor of Sootscale Valley** by the republic’s council.

#### The Town Today

The valley itself is a place of industry and wonder. A fortified **wooden wall** guards the entrance, and a sturdy **bridge** crosses the stream that once fed the tribe’s mines. Inside, **paved streets** twist between stone homes and glowing lampposts enchanted by the valley’s arcanists. **Magical streetlamps** flicker on at dusk, illuminating carved murals of dragons, pickaxes, and the republic’s star emblem. The air smells of coal, metal, and the rich malt of the town’s **brewery**. Steam hisses from vents along the cliffs, while the rhythmic pounding of hammers from the **foundry** and the whir of the **stonemason’s yard** echo through the vale like a heartbeat.

At the town’s center stands the **town hall**, a modest but well-built structure of dark stone where the kobold council meets weekly. Beside it rises the **Festival Hall**, once a natural cavern, now an amphitheater strung with colored lanterns. It hosts markets by day and song contests by night, its acoustics perfect for the high, chittering melodies of kobold music. A tall **Arcanist’s Tower** looms above the valley floor, a gift from the Ministry of Magic, where kobold mages and human engineers collaborate on experiments in artifice, illumination, and “minorly volatile metallurgy.”

#### Leadership

**Governor Kereek** is no traditional kobold leader. Cunning, calm, and unusually articulate, he governs through logic and cooperation rather than fear. His policies focus on integration, education, and trade—ensuring the kobolds’ skills serve the republic rather than isolate them. Though some older kobolds still mutter that he was once a spy, most see him as proof that loyalty can be earned by choice, not blood. He maintains a close friendship with **Chief Sootscale**, now retired but still active as an adviser and unofficial mentor. Prazil serves as Kereek’s captain of the watch, keeping order with enthusiasm, firebombs, and absolutely no subtlety whatsoever.

Kereek’s personal residence, a cliffside **mansion** with workshops and observatories built into the rock, doubles as his office. There, he holds council meetings, drafts treaties, and, on occasion, hosts visiting officials from [[Thumpington]] who leave marveling at the efficiency of kobold governance—and the taste of Sootscale ale.

#### Industry and Life

Sootscale Valley thrives on craft and invention. The **foundry** and **stonemason** produce tools, weapons, and construction materials for the entire republic. The **lumberyard** feeds the valley’s ever-growing demand for scaffolding and carts, while the **brewery** exports its distinctive smoked amber ale, *Firetongue Draft*, to taverns across Thumping Waters. Beneath the surface, an intricate **sewer system** channels heat and water from the forges to warm homes and power machinery—one of Kereek’s earliest and most celebrated innovations.

The kobolds’ mastery of both craftsmanship and magic has made the valley one of the most efficient industrial centers in the republic. The Ministry of Infrastructure often sends engineers here for training, and the Ministry of Magic proudly credits the valley’s mages with several breakthroughs in safe enchantment storage (a subject on which “safe” is still under active debate). The town’s motto, carved into the gate, reads: *“From shadows, light.”*

#### Culture

Life in the valley is defined by work, laughter, and invention. Every building hums with activity, every street corner boasts a story. The kobolds celebrate two major festivals each year: the *Festival of Ash*, commemorating their liberation from Hargulka’s tyranny, and the *Festival of Sparks*, honoring the republic’s founding and the lights that now burn above their heads. Their humor is sharp and their hospitality generous—though visitors are advised not to stand too close during fireworks tests.

The **Festival Hall** hosts nightly entertainment: music, dance, and mechanical exhibitions. Its walls are decorated with murals showing kobolds standing shoulder to shoulder with humans and centaurs, forging tools beneath the light of the republic’s blue star. The younger generation of kobolds speak Common as fluently as Draconic, and their pride in their new identity—both as Sootscales and as citizens of Thumping Waters—is unmistakable.

#### Modern Role

Today, Sootscale Valley stands as the republic’s industrial heart and its most shining example of redemption through unity. Its foundries and workshops power the realm’s expansion, and its mages light the path forward—literally and figuratively. Governor Kereek has plans to expand the **Arcanist’s Tower** into a full academy, training both kobolds and humans in engineering and magical design. The valley’s goods are exported throughout Thumping Waters and beyond, carried by caravans that now speak proudly of their origins “in the forges of Sootscale.”

At dusk, as the streetlamps ignite and the valley glows in gold and orange, the forges cool for the night. Kereek stands on his balcony, looking down over the town that once hid from the world and now helps to build it. “We were spies once,” he says, “but now we make what the world spies upon.” Few could argue with that.

## General Details

##### From the Journal of Linzi, Minister of Communication of Thumping Waters

*The Spies Who Came in from the Swamp*

When we first met **Kereek** and **Prazil**, they weren’t governors or heroes or anything close to respectable. They were spies. Bad ones. We caught them skulking around the streets of Thumping Waters, whispering to each other in Draconic and pretending to be very small bushes. Prazil sneezed and gave them away. Kereek immediately blamed him. It was, in hindsight, a very accurate preview of their relationship.

Kereek was all swagger and scales—bright red, sharp-eyed, and dressed like a festival banner someone had set on fire. He talked faster than most people think, and every sentence sounded like a plan. Prazil, on the other hand, was… simpler. A green-scaled mountain with a club, a grin, and the kind of loyalty that makes you forgive the occasional property damage. They were supposed to be working for Hargulka, the self-proclaimed Troll King, but the truth was much sadder. Their tribe was being held hostage, and spying was the only way to keep them alive. That, I think, was the first time I realized that not every villain’s shadow hides evil—sometimes it hides fear.

Once they confessed, Kereek started making suggestions—schemes, really. Big ones. Dangerous ones. The kind that start with “technically this isn’t treason” and end with fire. But he was clever, and more importantly, right. His knowledge of Hargulka’s forces let the Tubthumpers sneak into the troll’s fortress and end the war before it could spread. I still remember Kereek’s grin afterward, as if saving the kingdom was something he’d planned all along. Maybe it was. Or maybe he just didn’t like being on the losing side.

After the battle, the two kobolds were invited to stay. Most would have slunk away, but Kereek saw opportunity—and Prazil saw lunch. Together, they led their kin to the safety of Sootscale Valley, where clever hands and strong backs were finally welcome. Chief Sootscale saw potential in them both, though it was Kereek who caught his eye. The “clever tongue,” the “red-scaled schemer”—he had a spark the chief said belonged in a forge, not a dungeon. And for once, Kereek agreed.

Now, years later, I’ve watched that same kobold—once a liar and a spy—stand before his people as **Governor Kereek**. His voice carries confidence, his plans carry the republic, and his robe still looks like a bonfire in progress. Prazil stands beside him, smiling wide and proud, ready to smash anyone who threatens his friend. It’s strange to think that two misfits from a swamp changed the course of a kingdom. But that’s the beauty of Thumping Waters: we don’t throw away our rogues. We give them titles, jobs, and hopefully a few safety inspections.

*—Linzi*

Editor’s note: The Ministry of Communication wishes to clarify that espionage, while occasionally forgiven, is not an officially recognized path to public office.
