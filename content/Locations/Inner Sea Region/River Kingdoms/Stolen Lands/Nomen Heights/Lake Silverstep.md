# Lake Silverstep


### Lake Silverstep

Lake Silverstep lies along the southeastern edge of the Greenbelt, its glassy surface mirroring both the sky and the mountains that cradle it. From this deep and ancient basin flows the Gudrin River, which winds westward through the heart of the Stolen Lands before emptying into the Tuskwater. The lake is often called “the eye of the east,” for it seems to watch the land with calm, unblinking patience—a symbol of quiet power in a realm once ruled by chaos.

#### Geography and Features

The lake is fed by countless streams and cascades tumbling down from the steep tors to the east. These slopes are lush and wild, wrapped in mist and the sound of falling water. Moss-covered cliffs give way to hanging gardens of willow and lily, while the air hums with birdsong and the quiet music of unseen fey. Numerous caverns wind beneath the waterfalls, their entrances veiled in spray and shadow. Miners whisper that the walls glitter faintly with veins of silver and gemstone, though few who go seeking them return with more than stories.

The western shore, gentler and more settled, now supports a handful of fishing communities under the governance of Thumping Waters. Wooden piers reach out into the clear blue depths, and narrow trails snake upward to watchposts overlooking the lake. Travelers speak of the lake’s strange stillness, as though the water listens to every word spoken near it.

#### Inhabitants

Lake Silverstep teems with life. Its waters are home to a wealth of fish, from silver-scaled trout to the small, thick-bodied eels that gave the lake its name. These “silversteps” shimmer when cooked and are considered a delicacy throughout the republic, often reserved for feasts and festivals. Otters, herons, and water voles make their homes among the reeds, and the forests beyond are alive with songbirds and fireflies.

Yet the lake’s most famous denizens are not mortal. Nixies, grigs, and faerie dragons dwell among the reed beds and floating lilies, forming a secretive and mercurial court. They are generally friendly to the people of Thumping Waters—so long as their groves and pools are respected. Offenses against the lake are said to draw its fey guardians’ wrath, and even the most skeptical fishers leave small offerings of flowers or coins before each voyage.

#### Legends and Lore

Many tales surround the caverns beneath the eastern falls. Some speak of glittering treasure chambers guarded by spirits of water and stone; others of ancient ruins from before the fall of the cyclops empire. A few claim the lake’s depths are bottomless, connecting by some forgotten channel to the Inner Sea itself. Scholars from Thumping Waters have begun modest surveys of the area, but the fey are protective of their domain, and strange lights have been seen moving through the mist when explorers linger too long.

Among the people of the Greenbelt, Lake Silverstep is regarded with reverence. Its waters sustain the republic’s farmlands, and its beauty draws both poets and pilgrims. Some say that to gaze into the lake at dawn is to glimpse one’s truest reflection—not as the world sees you, but as the land itself remembers you. Whether truth or superstition, few leave Silverstep unchanged.
