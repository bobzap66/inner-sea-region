# Nomen Heights


The Nomen Heights region of the [[Stolen Lands]], within the [[River Kingdoms]], is a wilderness of steppes and mountains dotted with camps of a centaur tribe and the small settlement of Varnhold. It encompasses much of the [[Locations/Inner Sea Region/River Kingdoms/Stolen Lands/Nomen Heights/Dunsward|Dunsward]] frontier, the northern [[Locations/Inner Sea Region/River Kingdoms/Stolen Lands/Nomen Heights/Tors of Levenies|Tors of Levenies]] mountain range and the several rivers flowing from its peaks, and other wildlands south of [[Restov]]. The Thicketfell, a region of foothills on the western side of the [[Locations/Inner Sea Region/River Kingdoms/Stolen Lands/Nomen Heights/Tors of Levenies|Tors of Levenies]], is south of the Nomen Heights.
