# Nomen Heights


The Nomen Heights region of the [Stolen Lands](../Stolen%20Lands.md), within the [River Kingdoms](../../River%20Kingdoms.md), is a wilderness of steppes and mountains dotted with camps of a centaur tribe and the small settlement of Varnhold. It encompasses much of the [Dunsward](Dunsward.md) frontier, the northern [Tors of Levenies](Tors%20of%20Levenies.md) mountain range and the several rivers flowing from its peaks, and other wildlands south of [Restov](../../../Brevoy/Restov.md). The Thicketfell, a region of foothills on the western side of the [Tors of Levenies](Tors%20of%20Levenies.md), is south of the Nomen Heights.
