The Stolen Lands are a vast, untamed region in the northeastern reaches of the [[River Kingdoms]], bordering the southern frontier of [[Brevoy]]. Marshes, forests, and wild plains stretch for leagues, broken only by the ruins of past empires and the bones of failed colonies. Though often dismissed as a lawless backwater, the Stolen Lands have become the stage for one of the River Kingdoms’ most remarkable stories: the rise of the Republic of Thumping Waters.

#### History

The first major effort to tame these wilds came from Taldor, whose Fifth Army of Exploration sought to chart and civilize the region. Their campaign left behind a network of roads, waystations, and ruins — all of which have long since been reclaimed by the land. Later attempts by Brevoy and the River Kingdoms met the same fate, undone by distance, monsters, or their own ambition.

In 4710 AR, a Brevic charter sent a band of adventurers south to bring law to the northernmost stretch of the [[Locations/Inner Sea Region/River Kingdoms/Stolen Lands/Greenbelt|Greenbelt]]. Their success was swift and decisive: the fall of the bandit lord known as the [[Stag Lord]] gave rise to a new nation, the Republic of Thumping Waters. Founded on ideals of cooperation rather than conquest, the Republic soon became a rare example of lasting order in the Stolen Lands.

Not long after its founding, the young Republic faced its first true war — the Invasion of [[Hargulka]]. The self-proclaimed troll king united giant-kin, trolls, and beasts beneath his banner and sought to crush the fledgling realm. Through cunning leadership and relentless courage, the Tubthumpers shattered Hargulka’s forces and ended his dream of a troll dominion.

Peace did not last. That same year, the land was struck by the Bloom of Monsters — a horrifying curse spread by a cult of [[Lamashtu]] that caused beasts and citizens alike to erupt into monstrous forms. The Tubthumpers traced the source to a corrupted breach scar between the First World and the Abyss, sealing it and ending the plague, though at great cost.

In 4711 AR came the Vanishing of Varnhold, when an entire frontier settlement disappeared overnight. The Tubthumpers’ investigation revealed the hand of Vordakai, an ancient cyclopean lich. By confronting and defeating him, they restored Varnhold and secured the Republic’s eastern border.

Since then, the Republic has strengthened through diplomacy and trade, forging ties with Outsea, Hymbria, and Galt. Yet dangers persist: the bogs of [[Locations/Inner Sea Region/River Kingdoms/Stolen Lands/Hooktongue Slough|Hooktongue Slough]] still whisper with fey voices, and the ambitions of [[Fort Drelev]] threaten war. Still, the people of Thumping Waters endure, proving that even in the Stolen Lands — where nations have risen and fallen like morning mist — something lasting can take root.
