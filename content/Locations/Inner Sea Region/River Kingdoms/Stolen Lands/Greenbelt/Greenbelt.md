# Greenbelt


### The Greenbelt

The region known as the Greenbelt lies between the deep forests of the [[Locations/Inner Sea Region/River Kingdoms/Stolen Lands/Greenbelt/Narlmarches|Narlmarches]] to the west and the rolling plains of [[Brevoy]] to the east. Once a wilderness of lawless glades, hidden bandit camps, and untamed hills, the Greenbelt has since become the heartland of the Republic of Thumping Waters. What began as a dangerous frontier has grown into a thriving land of farms, trade routes, and proud frontier towns—though the memory of its chaotic beginnings still lingers.

#### Early History

Before unification, the Greenbelt was a patchwork of danger and opportunity. The western Narlmarches concealed forgotten ruins and the lairs of bandits, while the open hills to the east were home to scattered fey and restless kobold tribes. The greatest threat of the age was the bandit lord known as the [[Stag Lord]], whose fortress on the northern shore of the [[Locations/Inner Sea Region/River Kingdoms/Stolen Lands/Greenbelt/Kamelands/Tuskwater/Tuskwater|Tuskwater]] dominated the region through terror and extortion. Travelers and traders feared to enter his domain, and the few who did seldom returned.

That reign of fear ended when the chartered explorers who would become known as the Tubthumpers marched on the Stag Lord’s fort and brought him down. His fall broke the back of organized banditry in the Greenbelt and marked the birth of what would become Thumping Waters.

#### Unification and Growth

After the Stag Lord’s defeat, the Tubthumpers claimed his fortress as the foundation of their capital, [[Thumpington]]. From there, they began the immense work of taming and governing the surrounding lands. New roads connected remote holdings, farmland spread across the Tuskwater basin, and trade began to flow along safe routes once again. The [[Groups/Sootscale Kobolds|Sootscale Kobolds]] of the southern hills, long treated as raiders, were approached with diplomacy rather than steel. Under [[Chief Sootscale]], they became steadfast allies and a symbol of the republic’s ideal of coexistence between the peoples of the [[Stolen Lands]].

The troublesome gremlins of the southern reaches were eventually driven off, and the wilderness grew calmer under the watch of Thumping Waters’ militias. To the west, the [[Locations/Inner Sea Region/River Kingdoms/Stolen Lands/Hooktongue Slough/Hooktongue Slough|Hooktongue Slough]] remains wild and treacherous—its boggard tribes and ancient horrors beyond the reach of easy governance—but the Greenbelt itself has found a fragile peace. Roads now link Thumpington to [[Olegton]] in the north, [[Sootscale Valley]] in the south, and the borderlands of [[Fort Drelev]] beyond the western swamps.

#### Present Day

Today, the Greenbelt serves as the stable heart of Thumping Waters—a thriving landscape of forests, fields, and small frontier towns bound by common purpose. Its people are proud, practical, and unshaken by the hardships of their past. Yet even here, the wilderness whispers. The Narlmarches still guard their ancient mysteries, and the fog of Hooktongue sometimes drifts eastward, carrying strange lights and stranger stories. The republic’s banners may fly proudly, but every road still leads into the unknown.

Still, for the first time in living memory, travelers can walk the Greenbelt’s roads without fear of bandits or beasts, and children grow up beneath the promise of a free and unified land. The Stolen Lands are not yet tamed—but they are no longer lost. The Greenbelt endures as their beating heart, steadfast between the forest and the swamp.
