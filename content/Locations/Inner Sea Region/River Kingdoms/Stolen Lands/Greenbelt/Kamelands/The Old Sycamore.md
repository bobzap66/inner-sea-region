# The Old Sycamore


Looming over the hills in the southern Greenbelt, a graying hulk of a sycamore tree clings precariously to its last years of life. The 100-foot-tall tree stands as a known landmark and is visible for miles around.

Beneath the tree is a series of caverns, until recently occupied by a tribe of chaotic mites, sworn enemies of the [Sootscale Kobolds](../../../../../../Groups/Sootscale%20Kobolds.md).
