# Tuskwater


> [!side]
> :   **Points of Interest**
>     [Stag Lord's Fort](Stag%20Lord%27s%20Fort.md)

The Tuskwater is one of the largest lakes in the [Stolen Lands](../../../Stolen%20Lands.md). The [Stag Lord](../../../../../../../Campaigns/Kingmaker/NPCs/Stag%20Lord.md)’s fort sits atop a hill on the lake’s hill-crested northeastern shore, and while his bandits are more active in this area, wildlife remains a dangerous concern.
