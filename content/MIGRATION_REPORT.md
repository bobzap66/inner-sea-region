# Archive conversion status

The initial archive conversion is complete. Obsidian is now the canonical editing environment, and GitHub provides version history and publication updates.

## Current conventions

- Articles use YAML frontmatter for titles, types, hierarchy, and local artwork.
- Internal references use Obsidian wikilinks.
- Article images are stored locally under `assets/`.
- Campaign-specific material belongs under its campaign folder.
- Geographic material belongs under `Locations/`.
- New or revised articles should be reviewed with `git diff` before being committed and pushed.
