# Six Bears


## Six Bears Following

  

### Overview

A Mammoth Lords following from the Realm of the Mammoth Lords, the Six Bears are a hard-traveled Kellid tribe whose traditions prize physical might, stoicism, and survival in brutal tundra. Their name evokes endurance: six bears circling a fire, guarding clan and hearth.
  

### Culture & Traditions

• Gendered Roles: The Six Bears maintain strongly male-dominated roles in hunting, war, and leadership. Women are expected to tend hearth, medicine, and craft—exceptions are discouraged and often punished.
• Rites of Trial: Coming-of-age initiations are perilous wilderness hunts meant to prove ferocity and obedience. Failure brings disgrace; success earns a warrior’s place.
• Law of the Blood: Kin-slaying is taboo. Feuds must be resolved through sanctioned duels, exile, or “hunts of atonement.” Circumventing this law is itself a grave crime.
• Trophies & Legend: Warriors carry oversized or storied weapons as proof of personal legend. Mighty kills are displayed and sung in winter moots.
  

### Amiri & the Broken Oath

Amiri, a Six Bears youth who rejected the tribe’s gender rules, was sent on a final initiation many expected would end in her death. She returned alive with a frost giant’s blade and overheard her kinsfolk celebrating her assumed demise. In a rage she slew the conspirators, violating the Law of the Blood, and fled south. The tribe’s elders marked her name from the rolls—an exile whose legend would nonetheless follow them.
  

### Beliefs

The Six Bears respect ancestral spirits, winter totems, and the strength of giants—admired as foes and emulated as ideals. Shamans bind oaths with bone fetishes and keep bans against fey bargains that would weaken the will.
  

### Recent History (Campaign)

• Scattered & Hunted: In the wake of hard seasons and wandering far from their homeland, a remnant of the Six Bears—only seven young warriors and their shaman-chieftain [Nilak](../Campaigns/Kingmaker/NPCs/Nilak.md)—were discovered in Hooktongue Slough. Exhausted and wounded, they held a ring of ice against waves of undead.
• The Ghost’s Claim: The dead pursued them at the behest of a vengeful First World spirit seeking the soul trapped within Amiri’s frost giant blade. The tribe believes yielding that spirit would doom them; fighting earns the ghost’s wrath.
• Diplomacy Failed: A tense parley with envoys of Thumping Waters collapsed; harsh words were traded, and Nilak led her survivors away to seek safety on their own terms.
• Status: Near-extinct remnant. Location intentionally obscured. The clan prioritizes survival, burial of the honored dead, and finding a way to placate or banish the hunting spirit.
  

### Customs & Taboos

• Honor the Trial: Deeds speak; boasts without scars draw mockery.
• No Blood of Kin: Even traitors among one’s own must be judged by rite, not rage.
• Winter Hospitality: Share fire and broth in white-months—even with rivals—until first thaw.
• Shaman’s Word: In times of wandering, the shaman may act as chieftain; refusal to heed ritual command is exile.
  

### Relationships

• Realm of the Mammoth Lords: Distant kin and rivals. The Six Bears’ orthodoxy has strained ties with more progressive followings.
• Thumping Waters: Currently strained after the Hooktongue standoff; potential reconciliation hinges on mutual respect and addressing the ghost.
• Fey & First World: Avoided. The tribe teaches that beautiful bargains hide hungry teeth.
  

### Adventure Hooks

• Call Spirit: Learn or commission the Call Spirit ritual to free the soul bound in Amiri’s blade and end the ghost’s hunt.
• Winter Moot: Escort Nilak’s remnant north to rejoin kin—or help them found a new hearth under different laws.
• Law of the Blood: Prove before witnesses that Amiri’s kin-slaying met true necessity, opening the door to formal reconciliation.
  

### Notable Figures

• Nilak, Shaman-Chieftain: Holds the tribe together with iron will and winter magic; fiercely protective of her youths.
• The Vengeful Brother (Ghost): A First World spirit who names the blade-bound soul “brother” and pursues it relentlessly across the swamps.
• Amiri, the Exile: Once Six Bears—now a legend who walks beside southern kings, her past still casting a long shadow.
  

### Symbols & Regalia

A ring of six bear-claw pendants around a fire-mark; white-fur cloaks trimmed with bone plates; oath-fetishes of carved antler bound in red sinew.
