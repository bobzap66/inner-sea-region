### Prefatory Statement

This document is not history. It is an attempt to approach it.

The following dossier is compiled from a wide array of sources, none of which can be considered neutral.

Primary materials include grimoires, marginal notes, correspondence, ritual schematics, and architectural plans recovered from **Gauntlight** and the **Abomination Vaults** themselves. These sources are inherently suspect. The Vaults were not merely a lair, but a functioning thaumaturgical system designed to manipulate fear, memory, and interpretation. Any knowledge recovered from within them must therefore be treated as contaminated.

Secondary sources include civic and genealogical records from **Absalom**, port and tax documents from **Otari**, artifacts and journals preserved in the private museum at **Mayor Oseph Menhemes’s estate**, and theological and occult treatises consulted at the **Dawnflower Library**.

  

Where these external records corroborate one another, I have treated the information as provisionally reliable. Where Gauntlight stands alone, I have marked conjecture as such.

*If this dossier errs, it will err in one direction only: toward assuming intention where coincidence might suffice. I would rather be embarrassed than surprised.*
*— Morlibint*
