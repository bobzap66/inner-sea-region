# When the Dark Teaches Hunger


…thirst. Not water. Not bread. Something else. Warm. Thick. Sliding behind the teeth.

Heartbeat—mine? No. Too sharp. Too fast. Not mine. Borrowed. Stolen.

Dark walls breathe with me.
I see them now. Stone sweating. Rats moving. Their blood hums in my ears. Too loud. Too near.

Names. Hold on to names.
Theron. August. Talia. Comet. Malaroth.
The names drip like water through cracks. I drink them but they do not fill me.

Eyes. My eyes. Wrong. The dark is less dark.
I shouldn’t see, but I do. Silver on stone.
Shadows crawl where there should be none.

Teeth ache. Gums burn. Jaw tight.
Smile feels like a snarl.
Am I smiling?

Dreams wake before sleep.
Pale faces lean close, whispering with no mouths.
Ours. Ours. You are ours.
I scream, but the sound comes out sweet. Hungry.

Rations rot in the corner. I kick them away.
I want—no. I don’t.
I want.

I am Kiri. I am.
But the dark laughs.
You are becoming.

When the door opens…
I will be waiting.
With teeth.
