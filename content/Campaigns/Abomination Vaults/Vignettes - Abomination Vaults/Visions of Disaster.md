# Visions of Disaster


The visions won’t stop.

Every night, the same shadow looms over Otari. The lighthouse—Gauntlight. That cursed place. It calls to me, whispering through the veil of my dreams. I see the light flicker to life in the darkness, an unnatural, sickly glow. The town below shivers, its people oblivious to the storm gathering on the horizon. But I see it. I feel it.

It’s not just a nightmare. It’s a warning.

The others don’t believe me, of course. Why would they? I’m the strange tiefling seer at the edge of town, the one with the cryptic visions and the eyes watching the stars. But I can’t ignore this. Something is stirring in the Gauntlight, something old and hungry. I don’t know what it is, but it won’t stay dormant for long. I can feel its cold breath on the back of my neck even now.

I need help. Someone brave enough—or foolish enough—to face what lies in those ruins. Who would answer such a call? The guards won’t touch it. The townsfolk avoid it like the plague. I need heroes. Real ones. Those willing to face the unknown with fire in their hearts and steel in their hands.

But what if they fail? What if this is already too late?

No. I can’t think like that. I have to believe.

There are heroes out there. Somewhere. And I’ll find them before it’s too late.
