# All Dressed Up and Nowhere to Go


Razor was gone. Two days now. Not a sound since they dragged him off. Gorul didn’t like Razor much—loud, arrogant, always pushing his luck—but he didn’t deserve whatever those pale freaks had done to him.

Shad was curled up, barely moving, muttering to himself. Ziskilly sat with her back to the wall, staring at the dim, sickly glow of the fungi on the ceiling, tapping her fingers against her knee. Nobody spoke. What was left to say? They’d been stupid. Thought they were slick, sneaking into the ruins for easy loot. Nobody comes back from the Gauntlight, they’d been told. But Razor laughed, and Shad said rumors were just that—rumors. Ziskilly agreed, and Gorul… Gorul had followed. Idiot.

No one knew where they were. The Osprey Club didn't exactly keep close tabs on its members. If they'd gone missing in Absalom? Maybe someone would notice. Maybe. But out here, sneaking around like fools? They might as well have walked off the edge of the world.

But Yinyasmera... she was smart. Too smart to trust half the stories they’d fed her over the years. Maybe she’d figure it out. Maybe she’d put the pieces together. If anyone was coming for them, it’d be her. If she even cared.

Gorul shifted his weight, wincing as his bruises pressed against the cold, stone floor. What was worse—waiting to be dragged off like Razor, or rotting in this hole until they all starved?

Didn’t matter.

The freaks would decide soon enough.
