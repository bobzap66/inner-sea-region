# Kiri's Captivity


The stone walls drip with damp, each drop echoing like the toll of a bell. Kiri hangs against them, shackled high enough that her shoulders ache. The chains burn where iron bites into her flesh, but the true fire comes from within. It whispers in her veins, a hunger she doesn’t want, a thrum of necrotic life that does not belong to her. The Gauntlight has rewritten her blood, and she knows it is not finished.

She stares across the chamber at the bubbling vats, each one frothing with half-seen shapes writhing in murky fluid. Limbs press against the glass from within, twisting and twitching as though desperate to be born. They remind her too much of herself: unfinished, reshaped, waiting to be remade into something grotesque. She fears the vats most of all, because she suspects they are her future.

The thing that guards her doesn’t speak. It never has. A hulking silhouette of warped flesh, with folds and bulges and joints where none should be. It doesn’t need chains—it is the weight of inevitability. Its eyes linger on her as though measuring whether she is ready for the next stage.

Then the door shatters open. Steel and fire and fury spill into the chamber. A skeletal bard with glowing eyes clutches his pan flute, his presence both absurd and terrifying. Talia, her once-feline companion, scarred and finned, steps into the light with a haunted gaze. Behind them comes Malaroth, his monstrous form wrapped in black iron and bone-bound magic. August, blue-skinned and stern, raises a wand in hand. Captain Longsaddle charges in heavy armor, his ranseur gleaming like a banner of defiance.

And then—Kai. Her brother. A shoony with the familiar pug-faced muzzle, his eyes wide and horrified as they lock on hers. His presence cuts deeper than the chains, because his face is still whole, still untouched. He has come to save her—but what if what he finds isn’t her anymore?

The creature turns. The vats boil, their glass trembling. Kiri strains against her bonds, and the hunger in her blood stirs. She knows she is moments away from being forced into those same vats, her body warped until it resembles her captor. Worse still, she feels her fangs press against her lip, and in the pit of her stomach the new hunger answers. She doesn’t know if she will be rescued, or if she will be reborn into something too monstrous to save.
