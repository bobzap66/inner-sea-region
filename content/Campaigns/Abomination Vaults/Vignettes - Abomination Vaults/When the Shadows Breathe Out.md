# When the Shadows Breathe Out


> [!side]
> :   ![[when-the-shadows-breathe-out-dorianna.png]]

The gaslight guttered, throwing long, frantic shadows across Doriana's delicate face. Oseph sat beside her, heart sinking like a stone, the air thick with fear and stale dreams. Her breath came shallow and uneven, broken by soft whimpers that cut straight through him. He reached out, fingers trembling, tracing the faint, twisted lines on her withered hand—the cruel mark the Motley Man had left on his little girl. All he could do was sit in this waking nightmare and pray, desperate for any god to hear him.

  

Then the room shivered. Not a sound—just a strange, silent tremor that rippled through the air like reality holding its breath. The shadows deepened for a single heartbeat, then peeled back as if pushed away. Doriana gasped, her body going still.

  

Oseph froze. Color—real, living color—crept back into her cheeks. Her small fingers twitched. Before his eyes, the withered skin softened, smoothed, the terrible lines fading until her hand was whole again. Untouched. Innocent.

  

A deep, peaceful sigh slipped from her lips as sleep finally claimed her—real sleep, the first she’d had in weeks. The oppressive weight in the room lifted, leaving behind a thin, fragile hope.

  

For the first time in what felt like forever, Oseph let himself believe his daughter might truly be safe.
