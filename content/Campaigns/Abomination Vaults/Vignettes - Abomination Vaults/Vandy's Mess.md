# Vandy's Mess


Where is it? I know I read it.

Vandy Banderdash pulled another bundle of parchment from the cluttered shelves, her fingertips smudged with dust and ink. The candle beside her flickered, barely illuminating the sprawl of reports, maps, and hastily scrawled notes before her. The air in the archive room smelled of old paper and binding glue, tinged with the ever-present scent of lamp oil.

"Thida," she murmured under her breath. "I remember your name. You wrote to us... months ago?"

She frowned, tapping her quill against her lip. Yes, it had been some time back—long enough that she had filed it away as concerning but not urgent. A wandering champion of the Dawnflower, she recalled, keeping tabs on strange happenings in the area. Something about missing livestock? A collapsed ruin? No, no... it was worse than that. There had been monsters. But which kind?

Flipping through another sheaf of reports, she skimmed over complaints about bandits, sightings of enormous bats near the cliffs, and even a report about a ghostly figure seen by a fisherman near the old mill. All troubling, but none from Thida.

Could she have misfiled it?

A sense of unease coiled in her stomach. She had dismissed the report too quickly at the time, overwhelmed with other matters—the needs of the townsfolk, the endless cycle of tending to the sick and hungry, the push and pull of the faithful seeking guidance. Had she failed to recognize the urgency?

She exhaled sharply, pushing a lock of hair behind her ear. No. Sarenrae teaches that redemption lies in action, in lighting the path forward.

"Come on, Thida," she whispered. "Tell me what you saw."

She reached for another stack, her pulse quickening. If Thida's warnings were part of what was happening now—if they connected to Wrin Sivinxi’s dire visions and the growing sense of dread hanging over Otari—then she needed to find that report. And fast.
