# Pathfinder Society Chronicle: Steadfast - Appendix A


## Appendix: On the Nature of the Self Beyond Death

**Compiled for the Grand Lodge Archives:** Venture-Captain Evni Zongnoss, with commentaries from the Radiant Oath and the Esoteric Order of the Palatine Eye

---

### Thesis

The Steadfast case compels ethical review of **non-evil, non-mindless undead**. Where personhood evidently persists, duty and doctrine clash: are such beings allies to be protected, victims to be released, or abominations to be opposed?

### I. Theological Disquiet

- **Pharasma’s Doctrine.** Undeath interrupts the River of Souls; even benevolent undead constitute a corruption of cosmic order.
- **Iomedaean Perspective.** Valor and justice oppose evil, yet slaying an undead defender of innocents risks injustice; toleration risks normalization of corruption.
- **Other Traditions.** Sarenites, adherents of Milani, and the Radiant Oath weigh mercy to victims against enabling a cursed state; consensus remains unsettled.

### II. Ethical Considerations

- **Consent.** Steadfast’s undead did not choose their condition; moral censure for existence alone misapplies blame to victims of necromantic blight.
- **Persistence of Personhood.** Memory, skill, and character (e.g., Tomas’s masonry) indicate continuity of self warranting moral regard and protections.
- **Alliance Precedent.** Years of cooperative survival show coexistence is possible, if fragile and context-bound.

### III. Practical Consequences

- **Strategic Utility.** Sentient undead resist fatigue, hunger, and disease—assets in logistics and defense—yet an unstable foundation for long-term policy.
- **Civic Integration.** Ustalavic communities may respond with fear or violence; mediation, education, and clear legal status are required.
- **Allied Relations.** Formal recognition risks friction with Pharasmin clergy and Lastwall remnants; diplomacy and narrowly scoped pilot arrangements are advised.

### IV. Questions for Deliberation

- Should intelligent undead be accorded rights equal to the living (property, due process, oaths)?
- Is the destruction of such beings mercy, murder, or sanctioned exorcism? Under whose authority?
- Does alliance with them erode moral authority, or uphold truth above dogma?
- Can undeath be “redeemed,” or is it invariably a snare of the Tyrant regardless of temperament?

> **Closing Note.** To ignore these anomalies is willful blindness; to embrace them uncritically is reckless. The Society must navigate between zeal and cynicism, led by evidence, compassion, and prudence.
