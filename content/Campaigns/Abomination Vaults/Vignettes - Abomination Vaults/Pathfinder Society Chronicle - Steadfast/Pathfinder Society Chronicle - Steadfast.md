# Pathfinder Society Chronicle - Steadfast


**Filed by:** Venture-Captain Evni Zongnoss, Vodavani Lodge (Caliphas, Ustalav)
**Subject:** Reconnaissance and Evacuation of the Gravelands Settlement “Steadfast”
**Contributing Agents:** *Names Redacted for Security*

---

### Background

At the urging of Pathfinder Claudiette Dralston of the Radiant Oath, Society agents entered the Gravelands to locate and aid the hidden settlement of **Steadfast**, founded in the ruins of a Lastwall hamlet. The community’s defining anomaly was the coexistence of living refugees with **non-evil, sentient undead**—skeletons who retained memory, skill, and personality.

### Discoveries

#### 1) The Living–Undead Compact

Steadfast’s labor and defense were shared by living citizens and skeletal townsfolk who remembered their former lives. A notable example, *Tomas of Vigil*, a mason in life, continued his craft in undeath, strengthening fortifications and civic works. These undead arose away from the strongest necrotic loci and displayed intact faculties and pro-social intent. See [Appendix A](Appendix%20A.md) for further philosophical speculation.

#### 2) The Keystone of Elios

Agents recovered a consecrated keystone imbued by the late Pharasmin priest *Elios*. Once reset into the great hall, the stone projected a ward preventing freshly fallen bodies from rising, denying the Whispering Tyrant’s forces their customary battlefield recursion.

#### 3) Assault by Seldrick Dralston

*Seldrick Dralston*—once a Pathfinder, now an *Ivory Reaper*—led a disciplined strike on Steadfast. With the keystone active and the agents’ intervention, the attack was repelled and Seldrick destroyed. Given the nature of Tar-Baphon’s chosen, the permanence of this defeat remains uncertain.

#### 4) Evacuation and Resettlement

Despite the tactical victory, Steadfast’s position proved untenable beneath the pall of a nearby undead-held watchtower. The Society coordinated a full evacuation across the Path River into Ustalav. Refugees—living and those undead who retained autonomy—were escorted for resettlement and vetting. Claudiette Dralston now assists with training recruits at Vodavani Lodge.

#### Operational Implications

- Document and study conditions under which undead retain personhood (non-invasive protocols).
- Provide humanitarian support while mitigating doctrinal conflicts with allied faiths.
- Task reconnaissance to validate the western watchtower’s influence and garrison strength.

### Conclusion

Steadfast now stands empty; its people live on. For the Society, the settlement is proof that even in Tar-Baphon’s domain, identity and virtue can survive—sometimes even past death.
