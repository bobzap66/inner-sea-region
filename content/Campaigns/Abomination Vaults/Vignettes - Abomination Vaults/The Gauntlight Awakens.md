# The Gauntlight Awakens


Vandy Banderdash, the ever-cheerful yet keen-eyed high priestess of Sarenrae, stands in the sunlit gardens outside her temple in Otari, watching the dawn’s golden light spill over the treetops. Normally, this hour fills her heart with warmth, but today, something gnaws at the edges of her peace. A shiver runs down her spine, though the air is warm.

She closes her eyes and prays, pressing a hand over the sunburst holy symbol at her chest. Sarenrae, Dawnflower, guide me. What is this shadow upon my heart?

Then, like a whisper on the wind, a vision flickers through her mind’s eye—Gauntlight. The ruined lighthouse stands stark against a storm-dark sky, its crumbling stones cold and lifeless… yet something within pulses. A sickly blue glow, like a heartbeat, throbs from the top of the ruined tower. Shadows writhe within the stone halls, unseen eyes peering from the darkness.

Vandy gasps and clutches her chest as the vision fades. She opens her eyes, her breath quickened. The feeling lingers—a warning. A threat.
