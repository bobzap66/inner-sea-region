# Leif's Leaving


As Leif let his attention be distracted for a split second, the creature struck. He collapsed in a spray of blood. After several agonizing moments fighting for his existence on this plane, he surrendered to the inevitable.
He was shocked to find himself being pulled not onward, but down. He screamed in silence as he found himself trapped with hundreds of other souls, twisting and churning in a despairing mass. With growing horror, he realized that his soul would slowly be consumed to power the Gauntlight. Not only would he not pass on, but his very essence would be used to fuel the engine of destruction. Too late, he had a perfect understanding of the Gauntlight. But no one could hear his screams.
