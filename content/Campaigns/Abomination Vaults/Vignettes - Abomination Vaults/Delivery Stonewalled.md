# Delivery Stonewalled


Two days. Two whole days. That’s how long Narala’s been missing.

Oloria paced behind the counter of Gallentine Deliveries, arms crossed so tightly that her fingers dug into her sleeves. The candle on her desk had burned low, wax pooling like the worries in her gut. Narala was never late—not without sending word ahead. A courier of her experience knew how to handle delays. If the centaur had run into trouble on the road from Absalom, she would have sent a message, found a way to let Oloria know. But nothing had come. Just silence.

Had she been waylaid by bandits? There were always dangers along the road, even with the patrols keeping most major routes safe. Or worse—was this something unnatural? With all the talk of strange happenings near the Gauntlight and the troubles up north, Oloria couldn’t shake the feeling that something more sinister might be at play.

She exhaled sharply. Sitting here fretting wouldn’t bring Narala back. Maybe it was time to go to the Crook’s Nook, see if anyone had heard rumors of trouble on the roads. Or perhaps she needed to speak with Wrin Sivinxi—if there was something strange going on, that tiefling elf might know about it.

Oloria’s jaw tightened. Narala was her responsibility, and she wasn’t about to let this rest.
