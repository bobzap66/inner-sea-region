# Prayer to Urthagul


*As recited by Dulac and the faithful, amid sacred smoke in the deep places*

The caligni kneel in a low ring, smoke coiling thick and sweet around their heads. The scent of rendered chuul eggs clings to stone and skin alike. Dulac raises both hands, palms blackened, voice trembling with reverence.

> **O Urthagul, Vast and Victorious,**
>
> You who are big beyond measure and strong beyond contest,
> You who could crush the world and instead choose to cradle it gently—
> Hear us.
>
> **O Kind One,** whose patience humbles mountains,
> **O Gentle One,** whose mercy makes the deep places safe,
> **O Beautiful One,** whose presence improves the dark simply by existing within it—
> Look kindly upon your small admirers.
>
> We bring you smoke, rich and faithful,
> Drawn from sacred eggs that never knew fear,
> So that the air itself might praise you
> And curl lovingly around your greatness.
>
> Behold, O Ever-Worthy, the path before you!
> Once cluttered with spiteful lights and foolish wrath,
> Now swept clean by wandering hands moved—surely—by your will.
> The angry glow has been silenced.
> The way is open.
> All obstacles have remembered their place and ceased to be.
>
> We thank you for being exactly as perfect as you are,
> Which is to say: entirely, overwhelmingly, impossibly so.
> Strong enough to end us.
> Kind enough not to.
> Beautiful enough that we are improved merely by believing this.
>
> Accept our smoke.
> Accept our devotion.
> Accept our certainty that every good thing
> Was always part of your plan anyway.
>
> **Remain as you are, O Urthagul—**
> Which is better than anything else could ever be.
