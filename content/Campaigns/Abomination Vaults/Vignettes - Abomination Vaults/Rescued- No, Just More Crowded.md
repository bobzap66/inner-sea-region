# Rescued? No, Just More Crowded


Fear twisted Gorul's belly as he saw these newcomers taken as easily as had been he and his companions. They looked competent enough, based on the sheer volume of empty scabbards and pouches, but he shook his head. They had given up. More sacrifices to the freaks. He knew in his heart Razor wasn't coming back. And with more potential sacrifices, he and his remaining crew might not be as scarce a resource as they had been. Not worth preserving.

"So you just gave up? Let them take you?" he asked the big one, spotting several empty sword sheathes across his back. "You didn't kill any of them?"

"Well, we need information, and you can't get information from a corpse."

"You can't use information as a corpse, either! Cayden's hangover, if you'd just killed the freaks and let us out, I'd've told you anything you wanted to know! Pharasma's icy nipple, I'd've told you my mother's secret oxtail recipe!"

Gorul wondered, however, why the freaks had been so eager to offer the newcomers surrender. Could it be there was something they needed besides fresh meat? Did they have some kind of leverage after all?
