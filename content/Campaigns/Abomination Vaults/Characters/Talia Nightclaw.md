# Talia Nightclaw

![](../../../assets/images/talia-nightclaw-portrait.png)

Talia Nightclaw is a catfolk investigator whose instinct is to understand a danger before deciding how to survive it. Raised in Valash Raj, she learned early that powerful and dangerous forces do not need to be worshipped to deserve careful study. That habit served her well in the Abomination Vaults, where lore, ritual practice, medicine, and occult research often mattered as much as a weapon.

The Vaults also made that pursuit painfully personal. After a disastrous fall and capture by the fleshwarper Jafaki, Talia survived torture and extensive experimentation that permanently altered her body. The experience left her with aberrant grafts, unusual adaptations, and a lasting fear of losing control of her own flesh. Rather than abandon scholarship, she turned it into a form of atonement: if knowledge had failed to save people once, she would acquire enough of it to save the next person.

That philosophy shaped nearly everything she did afterward. Talia became one of the party's most capable physicians, learned arcane magic under Morlibint, studied forbidden texts despite their risks, and treated even grotesque magical problems as questions that could be tested. Her bedside manner remained famously clinical; she could lecture an injured companion about the monetary value of a foolish decision, read romance novels aloud as "auditory stimulation," and voluntarily alter her own physiology simply because the experiment was worth conducting.

Her research eventually uncovered the mechanism by which Belcorra could be destroyed permanently. During Nhimbaloth's trials, Talia refused the offer to erase her pain because pain was information: evidence of what had happened and what could be learned from it. She carried that same attitude into the final battle, testing assumptions, identifying weaknesses, and adjusting whenever the evidence contradicted her expectations. When Belcorra finally vanished and the Gauntlight collapsed, Talia's first instinct afterward was entirely predictable—there was still more to document.

## Personality

Talia is analytical, introverted, self-critical, dryly funny, and relentlessly curious. She prefers evidence to instinct and tends to process trauma by studying it. Her compassion often appears as competence rather than warmth: she shows that she cares by finding the answer, stopping the bleeding, and making sure the same mistake is not repeated.

## Notable Moments

- Survived Jafaki's fleshwarping experiments and turned the study of her own altered body into part of her life's work.
- Saved companions through advanced medical treatment while maintaining an almost absurdly clinical bedside manner.
- Uncovered Belcorra's research on the Fulcrum Lenses and the method needed to destroy her permanently.
- Refused Nhimbaloth's attempt to take away her pain, declaring that pain is an indicator that helps people learn.
- Critically struck a creature's internal organs while trapped inside its stomach and treated the result as useful empirical data.
