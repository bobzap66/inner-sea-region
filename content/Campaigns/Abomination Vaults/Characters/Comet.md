# Comet

Comet is a bard whose life, death, undeath, and eventual return to living form were all accompanied by the same irrepressible instinct: if the world insists on becoming grim, make noise anyway. Originally a monkey goblin performer, Comet drowned during the party's adventures and returned as a skeleton, continuing on with panpipes, jokes, and a deliberately casual attitude toward the metaphysical problem of being dead.

For much of the campaign he concealed his skeletal form beneath magic while wrestling with questions he rarely allowed to become solemn in public. He feared not death—he had already done that—but rejection, irrelevance, and the possibility that the people he entertained would see only an abomination if the disguise failed. His experiences with sentient undead and the strange communities beneath Otari gave him evidence that identity could survive death, but Nhimbaloth's trials exposed how deeply the fear of being forgotten still ran.

Comet answered those trials in the most Comet way possible. When the Empty Death offered to take away his pain in exchange for surrender, his response was simply, "No thank you." He took the pain and kept walking. That mixture of mischief and stubborn resilience defined his entire descent through the Vaults, whether he was soothing mindless undead with music, navigating dangerous negotiations, replacing broken bones with "newer models," or recovering from being temporarily transformed into a turtle with a triumphant "Bones, baby!"

After Belcorra's defeat, Comet woke changed again. His skeletal body was gone, replaced by a duskwalker form marked by Pharasma's judgment. For someone who had spent so long hiding what he had become, the transformation mattered less because it made him normal than because it let him stop pretending. Strange, indigo-skinned, marked by death, and unmistakably himself, Comet began looking toward music again—not as a defense against the world, but simply because he wanted an audience.

## Personality

Comet is mischievous, warm, irreverent, theatrical, and far more emotionally resilient than his jokes initially suggest. Music is both his craft and his answer to silence. He thrives in crowds, dislikes imposed solemnity, and has a habit of meeting cosmic horror with the social energy of someone heckling a bad performance.

## Notable Moments

- Drowned and returned as a skeleton, continuing his career with remarkably little respect for the expected gravity of undeath.
- Learned that music could calm mindless undead, turning his own strange condition into a bridge rather than merely a curse.
- Was transformed into a turtle by a ghost and celebrated his return to skeletal form with "Bones, baby!"
- Refused Nhimbaloth's offer of relief with a polite "No thank you."
- Awoke after Belcorra's defeat as a duskwalker, finally able to stand openly before the world without hiding behind an illusion.
