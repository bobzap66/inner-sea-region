# Kiri Steelpaws

![[kiri-steelpaws-portrait.jpeg]]

Kiri Steelpaws is a shoony thaumaturge whose curiosity carried her from a close-knit village in Isger into the depths beneath Otari. Cheerful, stubborn, and intensely observant, she approached the strange with an investigator's fascination even when common sense suggested leaving it alone. The Abomination Vaults rewarded that curiosity with scars, repeated brushes with death, and eventually a transformation into a dhampir, forcing Kiri to reconcile the warm, communal instincts of a shoony with a body increasingly associated with monsters.

Rather than allow that transformation to define her, Kiri made it another problem to understand. The existence of communities where living people and benevolent undead coexisted gave her hope that personhood mattered more than physiology. Her blood-filled thaumaturgic chalice became an ordinary part of the party's survival, and the companions around her gradually treated her altered nature as simply another fact about Kiri. That acceptance mattered, because the deepest fears exposed during Nhimbaloth's trials were not of pain or death, but of isolation and of losing control badly enough to harm the people she loved.

Kiri survived being swallowed whole more than once, prolonged paralysis, cursed wounds that resisted healing, and even being dragged bodily into lava by a magma scorpion. She nevertheless remained one of the party's most dependable front-line combatants. During the final descent she shattered Lady's Whisper with her battle axe, destroying an ancient servant of death shortly before the party confronted Belcorra Haruvex herself.

When the Gauntlight finally collapsed and Otari celebrated, Kiri emerged changed but unmistakably herself. She decided that the frightening things she now carried—red eyes, fangs, scars, and all—did not have to make her frightening. With her winged sandals still in hand, she resolved to use the impossible life she had survived to make children laugh rather than recoil.

## Personality

Kiri is curious, loyal, analytical, stubborn, and increasingly pragmatic. She tends to treat failure as information and danger as something that can be understood if examined closely enough. Her strongest instinct is communal: she needs her companions, protects them fiercely, and fears isolation more than almost anything else.

## Notable Moments

- Survived being swallowed whole by a Duth Rag and fought her way through its stomach.
- Endured paralysis, destroyed armor, and a Grave Knight curse that repeatedly tore healed wounds back open.
- Refused Nhimbaloth's offer to remove her pain, insisting that what she had endured remained hers.
- Landed the killing blow on Lady's Whisper during the final approach to Belcorra.
- Walked out of the fallen Gauntlight determined that becoming frightening would never mean surrendering joy.
