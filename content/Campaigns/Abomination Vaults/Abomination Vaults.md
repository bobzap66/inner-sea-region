# Abomination Vaults

The **Abomination Vaults** campaign followed a group of adventurers drawn into the threat beneath [Otari](../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/Otari.md), where the reawakened Gauntlight lighthouse marked the return of the ghostly sorcerer **Belcorra Haruvex**. What began as an investigation of the ruins beneath Gauntlight became a long campaign through the sprawling dungeon below, as the party uncovered Belcorra's history, confronted the creatures and factions inhabiting the Vaults, and searched for a way to destroy her permanently.

The expedition exacted a heavy price. [Leif Darkwood](Characters/Leif%20Darkwood.md) was killed in the Vaults and his soul became trapped within Gauntlight, while [Theron](Characters/Theron.md) died and was restored through primal reincarnation in the body of a tengu. The surviving adventurers endured repeated encounters with Belcorra, gathered the Fulcrum Lenses needed to complete the Fulcrum Lattice, and eventually descended into the deepest reaches of the complex to confront the power of Nhimbaloth that sustained their enemy.

The campaign ended with Belcorra's final destruction and the collapse of Gauntlight itself. The survivors escaped as the lighthouse and the Abomination Vaults fell into ruin, ending the centuries-old threat hanging over Otari.
