# Octavia

![[octavia-portrait.webp]]

> [!side]
> :   Influence Level: 2
>     Discovery Skills: Arcana, Perception, Society
>     Lowest DC: Performance

*Arcane Idealist · Escaped Slave · Lightning in Her Eyes*

Octavia is a half-elf with striking golden eyes, a cascade of tawny curls, and the sharp mind of a scholar tempered by the hard-earned instincts of a survivor. Once a slave in the [[River Kingdoms]], she escaped alongside her companion [[Regongar]]—her magic finally awakened under pressure, granting her the power to break their chains.

The Tubthumpers encountered her and Regongar in the wilderness: ragged, hungry, and wary of every movement. Rather than turn them away, the party offered food, shelter, and safe passage to [[Olegton]]. Octavia never forgot that kindness. She and Regongar spoke little of their past, but gratitude was clear in her quiet expressions and rare, gentle smiles.

Behind her reserved demeanor lies a fierce belief in freedom—for herself, for others, and for any soul ground under heel by tyranny. Her magic, wild and precise, is a tool she uses to protect those who cannot protect themselves. She's no stranger to violence, but unlike Regongar, she wields it reluctantly, preferring cleverness, stealth, and diplomacy whenever possible.

Since parting ways with the Tubthumpers, she hasn’t been seen. Rumors say a half-elf matching her description has been spotted in the borderlands near [[Pitax]], moving quietly among the discontent and the displaced. Whether she walks alone, or still travels with Regongar, is unknown—but those who have met her speak of a young woman who sees the world as it could be, not merely as it is.

And if she ever returns, it will be as an ally—or a reckoning.
