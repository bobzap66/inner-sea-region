# Stag Lord

![[stag-lord-portrait.webp]]

*Mad Tyrant · Masked Marauder · Broken Son of a Broken Land*

Once a name whispered in dread throughout the Greenbelt, the Stag Lord was a warlord, bandit king, and symbol of unchecked cruelty. Towering, masked, and seemingly inhuman beneath his antlered helm, he ruled the northern wilds from a crumbling fort on the northern shore of the Tuskwater. His reign was one of extortion, ambush, and fear—settlements and travelers alike lived at the mercy of his unpredictable wrath and the greed of his lieutenants.

But the truth behind the terror was far more tragic. Born the abused son of a mad druid, the Stag Lord grew up brutalized and twisted by his father’s fanatical rants and punishments. After murdering the druid in a fit of long-buried rage, he fled into the wilds and carved out a kingdom of violence, drawing other broken men to his cause. Though clever and charismatic when sober, he quickly spiraled into paranoia and drink, isolating himself in his fortress and letting lieutenants like Akiros, Dovan, and Auchs act with near-total impunity.

His downfall came at the hands of the Tubthumpers, the adventurers sent by Restov to tame the Stolen Lands. After months of scouting and skirmishes, the party launched a daring raid on the Stag Lord’s fort. With his forces scattered and his defenses broken, the tyrant fell—cut down in the very throne room where he once dreamed of dominion.

His body was unceremoniously tossed into the Shrike River, left to drift like so many victims of his cruelty. His legacy endures only in cautionary tales and the haunted eyes of those he once commanded.
