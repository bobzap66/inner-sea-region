*Bardic Historian · Ink-Stained Fingers · Heart of the Kingdom*

Linzi is a spirited halfling with a quill always in hand and a song never far from her lips. Slight of frame but brimming with energy, she’s rarely seen without her oversized journal, where she feverishly records the deeds and dramas of the Republic of Thumping Waters. Whether she’s drafting epic verses, sketching portraits, or jotting down council gossip, Linzi is determined to capture history as it happens—warts and all.

She began her journey at the “Feast of Heroes” in [[Restov]], dreaming of chronicling the birth of a new kingdom. Since then, she’s become more than an observer. As Minister of Communication, Linzi acts as the voice of the people and the public face of the Tubthumpers, delivering speeches, managing information, and shaping the narrative of the republic’s rise.

Linzi is friendly, infectiously enthusiastic, and talkative to a fault. While she often comes across as naïve, her optimism hides a growing political savvy—especially after surviving assassination attempts, bureaucratic sabotage, and the burdens of power. She believes stories shape reality, and she’s determined that the story of Thumping Waters will be one worth telling.

Her passion for accessible information recently led her to a bold (and somewhat reckless) move—borrowing funds from the treasury to secretly commission a state-of-the-art printing press. When the caravan carrying it—and her old mentor, [[Eobald]]—went missing, Linzi feared she had jeopardized not only her position but the ideals she stood for. Fortunately, the Tubthumpers recovered both Eobald and the press before tragedy struck, though Linzi’s reputation still bears the inkstains of that gamble.

Still, she keeps writing. Because history doesn’t wait, and neither does Linzi.
