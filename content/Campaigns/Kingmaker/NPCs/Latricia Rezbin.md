# Latricia Rezbin


*Warm Smile · Steely Resolve · Heart of the People*

Latricia Rezbin may not be the loudest voice in a room, but when she speaks, people listen. Her gentle manner and warm, reassuring smile conceal a spine of steel and a heart honed by hardship. A former priestess of Erastil and devoted partner to Loy Rezbin, Latricia has always believed in the promise of community — in building something lasting from the wild soil of the Stolen Lands.

When Loy set his sights on founding a new settlement, it was Latricia who turned his dream into something real. She organized the early settlers, treated the sick, calmed disputes, and led prayers when tempers flared. Though Loy often spoke on behalf of the community, it was Latricia’s quiet strength and competence that earned their trust.

In the wake of threats both monstrous and political, Latricia has emerged as a pillar of the Republic of Thumping Waters — not through force of arms, but through boundless empathy, practical wisdom, and unwavering faith. She sees the kingdom not just as territory, but as a tapestry of lives bound together by shared hopes and burdens. She champions the values of family, tradition, and neighborly care, all while remaining unafraid to challenge decisions that threaten the heart of what they’ve built.

Those who underestimate Latricia do so at their peril — for behind her kindness lies a fierce protector of her people and ideals.
