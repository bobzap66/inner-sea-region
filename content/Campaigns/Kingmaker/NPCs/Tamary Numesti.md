# Tamary Numesti


Tamary Numesti is one of those names that travels ahead of itself—spoken in the same hushed tones as “hostage,” “tribute,” and “Tiger Lords.” I’ve never met her, and truth be told, I’ve only heard her story in fragments, carried on the wind from **[Tatzlford](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Tatzlford/Tatzlford.md)** and **[Fort Drelev](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Fort%20Drelev/Fort%20Drelev.md)** and whispered by travelers who claim to know someone who knows her sister. But even half a story can tell you a lot about a person.

  

From what I’ve gathered, Tamary is the eldest daughter of a knight named ****[Terrion Numesti]****, once one of Drelev’s loyal commanders before the baron traded honor for survival. When **Baron Drelev** bent the knee to **King Irovetti** and the **Tiger Lords**, the barbarians demanded hostages to ensure his obedience. Terrion refused to hand over his daughter. Drelev, showing the kind of courage for which he’s become locally famous, gave her up anyway. And so Tamary was taken west—alive, yes, but not free.

  

That’s all anyone really knows for certain. Some say she was sent to the Tiger Lords’ encampments, kept as a bargaining chip. Others claim she was given as a “gift” to one of their war chiefs. A few swear she’s still alive, biding her time and waiting for her chance to escape. I prefer to believe that one. It sounds like the sort of story that ends with a rescue and a reckoning, and I’ve always liked those kinds best.

  

I’ve heard more of her sister, **[Kisandra](Kisandra%20Numesti.md)**—how she braved the swamps of [Hooktongue Slough](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Hooktongue%20Slough/Hooktongue%20Slough.md) to warn Tatzlford of Drelev’s attack, and how she begged the Tubthumpers to help free her family. If that’s the kind of courage the Numestis are made of, then I imagine Tamary’s not sitting quietly in her cage. She’s waiting. Planning. Enduring. The kind of strength that doesn’t make noise until it’s too late for anyone standing in its way.

  

Someday, when the Republic’s banners fly over Fort Drelev, I hope we find her alive. Because the songs we could write about the woman who survived the Tiger Lords will make even the boggards stop croaking long enough to listen.
