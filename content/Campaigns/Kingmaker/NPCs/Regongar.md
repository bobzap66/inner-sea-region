# Regongar

![[regongar-portrait.webp]]

> [!side]
> :   Influence Level: 0

*Bloodied Past · Flashing Blade · Grin Like a Thunderstorm*

Regongar is a muscular half-orc with sharp tusks, sharper eyes, and the kind of grin that makes people check their weapons. His storm-scarred skin carries the lingering scent of ozone, a side effect of the raw electrical energy that crackles through him when battle calls. Where [[Octavia]] is the mind, Regongar is the storm: direct, intense, and always one insult away from drawing blood.

The Tubthumpers first met him alongside Octavia, both freshly escaped from brutal captivity. While Octavia held her emotions close, Regongar made no effort to hide his fury or his mistrust. Still, he accepted the party’s food and protection, albeit with a wary glare and a hand on his weapon. Though no promises were made, his parting words carried weight: “I don’t forget debts. Not the good ones… or the bad.”

Regongar sees strength as survival. He has little patience for hesitation, and even less for authority he hasn’t personally bloodied. But beneath the scars and bravado lies something more: an unspoken bond with Octavia, a deep loyalty that has kept him alive and on something resembling a path. For her, he will walk away from a fight. For her, he’ll also burn an empire to the ground.

Since leaving the Tubthumpers’ protection, Regongar has vanished into the [[River Kingdoms]]’ chaos. Some say he fights in the pit brawls of [[Pitax]]. Others say he’s killed a dozen slavers by himself. All agree: if Regongar returns to Thumping Waters, it’ll be with lightning in his veins and blood on his hands.
