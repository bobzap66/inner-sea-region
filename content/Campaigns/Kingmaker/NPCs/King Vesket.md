# King Vesket

![[king-vesket-portrait.png]]

*Old Scales · Wary Eyes · Chieftain Turned Mayor*

Vesket is a grizzled iruxi warrior with scarred scales and a chiseled sense of pride. Once the unquestioned leader of the Longtail Tribe, he ruled by strength and reverence for ancestral spirits—until one such “spirit” led his people astray. Duped by a will-o’-wisp posing as an ancient king, Vesket nearly lost everything before outside help shattered the illusion and spared his tribe from destruction.

Now, Vesket leads not just as a chieftain, but as the appointed mayor of Longtail Island, navigating the strange currents of diplomacy, agriculture, and bureaucracy. It’s not a role he sought, but one he shoulders with grim resolve. He is still every bit the war-leader—his claws sharp, his orders swift—but he tempers his rule with caution born from past humiliation.

He speaks rarely and wastes no breath on flattery. To Vesket, strength lies in deeds, not words. He mistrusts softness and scorns indulgence, but honors promises and respects those who earn their place. Though still wary of the Tubthumpers’ ways, he has come to see their value—and the survival of his people now depends on that uneasy alliance.

Longtail Island stands because Vesket chose change over pride. But he will never forget the price of weakness. And should danger ever return, the king behind the mayor is ready.
