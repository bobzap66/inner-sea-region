# Stas

![[stas-portrait.jpeg]]

*Simple Logger · Persistent Storyteller · Hodag (Almost) Slayer*

Stas is a plainspoken, broad-shouldered logger working the woods of the Republic of Thumping Waters. With calloused hands and a quiet demeanor, he’s easy to overlook—until he starts talking about the time he claims to have stuck a spear in the back of a rampaging hodag. For months, locals laughed off his tale as the ramblings of a woodsman with too much moonshine and too little company.

But Stas had the last laugh.

When the Tubthumpers brought down the very same beast, unmistakable and scarred in the exact place Stas described, the tale turned from tall to true. He’s been walking a little taller ever since—though he still splits logs like everyone else and never lets his “close call with glory” get in the way of a good day’s work.

These days, folks buy Stas drinks at the tavern. Just don’t ask him to tell the story twice in one sitting—he’s still a logger, not a bard.
