# Harrim

![[harrim-portrait.jpg]]

> [!side]
> :   Influence Level: 2

*Cleric of [[Groetus]] · Doomsayer with a Heart of Stone (and Gold)*

Harrim is a middle-aged dwarf with a bald pate, a thick salt-and-pepper beard, and the weariness of one who’s spent a lifetime waiting for the end of the world. Once a traditional dwarf with a traditional life ahead of him, Harrim turned his back on the worship of [[Torag]] after witnessing the senselessness of suffering in the world. He found a strange comfort in the teachings of Groetus, god of the end times — not in the chaos or destruction, but in the inevitability of it all.

With a calm demeanor and a surprisingly affable manner, Harrim often puts people at ease… right up until he starts muttering about the looming moon of Groetus, the doom that awaits all empires, and the futility of mortal toil. Despite his bleak philosophy, he is not cruel. In fact, Harrim is compassionate in his own fatalistic way, believing that kindness is all the more important in a world that is ultimately doomed.

He is practical, level-headed, and often serves as a much-needed anchor in tense situations — provided his companions can stomach his grim sermons. Though his god promises oblivion, Harrim remains fiercely loyal to those he fights beside. After all, if the end is coming, one might as well face it in good company.
