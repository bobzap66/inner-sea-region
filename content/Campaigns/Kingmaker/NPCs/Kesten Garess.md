# Kesten Garess

![[kesten-garess-portrait.webp]]

Kesten Garess bears the name of a noble house in Brevoy, but little of its privilege. A quiet scandal—one whispered in the drawing rooms of Restov and hushed in the halls of House Garess—led to his quiet exile from noble society. Rather than accept a life of shame among the idle elite, Kesten took up sword and shield, turning to a mercenary’s life with grim determination.

Now stationed at Oleg’s Trading Post and loyal to the charter of Thumping Waters, Kesten serves as a stalwart guard captain and field commander. His armor is well-maintained, his posture disciplined, and his gaze watchful. Though he rarely speaks of his past, those with knowledge of Brevic politics may recall the scandal: a forbidden romance or dishonorable duel—no two rumors agree.

Kesten is moody, often brooding in silence, but not cruel. He has a strong sense of duty and respects those who work to uphold order, even if his tone remains blunt and his friendships few. To those who earn his trust, he is a steadfast ally and a shield against the darkness pressing at the edges of civilization.
