# Hargulka

![[hargulka-portrait.webp]]

*Self-Styled Troll King · Tyrant of Fire and Stone · Devoured by Ambition*

Once nothing more than a particularly brutal troll warlord, Hargulka rose to power by terrorizing rival monsters into submission and demanding tribute in flesh and gold. His ambitions expanded after a prophetic dream showed him not merely as a chief, but as a king—enthroned atop a mound of skulls, worshiped by monsters of all kinds: hobgoblins, harpies, basilisks, even ogres and leucrottas. From that moment, the dream consumed him. He would not rest until the Stolen Lands were his to rule.

Hargulka saw the Republic of Thumping Waters not as a fledgling nation to negotiate with, but as a usurper squatting on what should be his domain. Their order, their walls, their festivals — they were insults carved into his rightful kingdom. Yet Hargulka was no fool. He reined in his more violent impulses and set about building an empire of monsters, recruiting like-minded beasts and tyrants from the swamps, hills, and wildlands. Under his brutal reign, trolls were organized for the first time in living memory, forming the backbone of a monstrous coalition.

His kingdom, however, was built on fear and fire — and the Tubthumpers brought both in greater measure. Tracking the growing threat to its source, they infiltrated Hargulka’s fortress through ancient ruins and stormed his throne room. In a titanic battle, they faced Hargulka and his spell-slinging consort — a monstrous hag of great power. Despite the odds, the Tubthumpers slew them both, shattering his short-lived reign and scattering the remnants of his monstrous army.

Hargulka’s dream of dominion died with him, his scorched corpse a warning to any creature that might dare raise a crown against the Republic of Thumping Waters. His fortress was left in ruin, and his name now lingers only in whispered warnings passed among trolls who dare not dream of kingship again.
