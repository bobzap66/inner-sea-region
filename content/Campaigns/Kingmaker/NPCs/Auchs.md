# Auchs

![[auchs-portrait.jpeg]]

A lumbering lummox of a man, Auchs is simpleminded in everything but cruelty. He delights in the sounds of sobbing and screaming—so long as they're not coming from him—and gleefully crushes or pummels bandits and prisoners alike at the command of his fellow lieutenants or the Stag Lord himself.

Illiterate and largely incapable of complex thought, the only word Auchs claims to spell is his own name—and even that is suspect, as Dovan took a perverse pleasure in telling him it was spelled “ox.” He believed it, of course, and sometimes scrawls it into the dirt with a stick, beaming with pride.

After the fall of the Stag Lord, Auchs vanished into the wilderness alongside Akiros Insmort, who—perhaps out of guilt, obligation, or quiet compassion—promised to keep the brute under control.
