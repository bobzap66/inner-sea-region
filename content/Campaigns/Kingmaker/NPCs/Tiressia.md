# Tiressia

![[tiressia-portrait.jpeg]]

*Forest Spirit · Graceful Guardian · Wary Ally of Mortals*

Tiressia is a dryad bound to one of the oldest trees in the Narlmarches, her presence a balm to the wilderness she calls home. Tall and willowy, with bark-brown skin and hair like trailing leaves, she embodies the beauty and fragility of the forest. Though cautious of outsiders, she understands that even the wilds sometimes need mortal aid.

When corruption stirred in her domain—a monstrous scythe tree rooted in blight—Tiressia turned to the Tubthumpers for help. She offered them a gift of rare feather tokens in exchange for the creature’s destruction, asking that they also deliver them to Melianse, another woodland spirit in need.

Though she remains distant from civilization, Tiressia watches the Republic of Thumping Waters from afar, hopeful that the new rulers may yet prove to be protectors of the natural world rather than its next defilers.
