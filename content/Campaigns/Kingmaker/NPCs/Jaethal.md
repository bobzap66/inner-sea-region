# Jaethal

![[jaethal-portrait.jpg]]

> [!side]
> :   Influence Level: 2

*Undying Enigma · Inquisitor of Urgathoa*

Jaethal is a vision of quiet menace — deathly pale with raven-black hair and eyes that seem to judge more than observe. Clad in somber blacks and muted greys, she wears no jewelry, no ornamentation, and no expression that suggests comfort in the land of the living. Always near at hand is her weapon of choice: a massive, wickedly sharp scythe, its edge gleaming with promise.

She does not speak unless spoken to, and even then, her words are often clipped, precise, and unsettling. At feasts or gatherings, Jaethal watches others like a vulture watching a battlefield — not with hunger, but with morbid curiosity. She measures people not by their charm or wit, but by their will to survive and their relationship with death.

What most don’t realize at first glance is that Jaethal is already dead — an elf brought back as an inquisitor of Urgathoa, goddess of undeath and gluttony. She does not hunger for flesh, but for truth, vengeance, and the unraveling of mortal hypocrisy. Unlike many undead, she is no mindless servant; she chose her path, and walks it with purpose.

Those who fight alongside her find her a loyal, if eerie, companion. She respects strength, hates weakness of will, and speaks with the conviction of someone who knows how the story ends — and doesn’t fear it.
