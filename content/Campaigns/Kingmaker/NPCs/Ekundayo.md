# Ekundayo

![[ekundayo-portrait.webp]]

> [!side]
> :   Influence Level: 1

he Watchful Hand · Archer of Vengeance · Carpenter in Peace

Ekundayo is a man forged by loss and tempered by purpose. Dark-skinned and broad-shouldered, with close-cropped hair and a gaze as steady as a drawn bowstring, he speaks rarely—but when he does, his words strike true. Behind his quiet demeanor lies a mind honed by tragedy and survival.

Years ago, Ekundayo lost his family to troll marauders. That grief hardened into a single mission: vengeance. With his loyal hound by his side, he hunted the beasts across the [[Stolen Lands]], each arrow loosed in memory of the life he could never reclaim. His path eventually crossed with the Tubthumpers, and together they struck down the monsters that haunted him—including Gragglebeard, the giant who had escaped his justice for far too long.

In the early days of the Republic of Thumping Waters, Ekundayo served as Minister of Defense, a title he bore with grim precision. He trained the militia, led hunts, and helped defend the fledgling realm from trolls, beasts, and worse. But the longer he served, the more he was haunted not just by what he had lost—but by what he feared he might become. When his old flame, Ntavi, reappeared and tried to lure him away with a chance to live in vengeance once more, he nearly went. But in the end, Ekundayo chose peace over blood. Ntavi left. He stayed.

Now retired from government, Ekundayo spends most days shaping wood rather than drawing steel. He builds cribs and shingles, doors and docks—finding meaning in the quiet act of creation. Yet his bow still hangs near the door, and his faithful dog still sleeps beside the hearth.

He doesn’t speak of his grief often. He doesn’t have to. It lives in every arrow, every scar, and every life he quietly protects.
