# Chief Sootscale

![[chief-sootscale-portrait.png]]

## Chief Sootscale

*Former Governor of [[Sootscale Valley]] • Liberator of the [[Groups/Sootscale Kobolds|Sootscale Kobolds]]*

> Chief Sootscale ensured that his people entered the republic on their own feet rather than upon their knees.

#### Titles and Roles

Former Governor of Sootscale Valley  
Chief of the Sootscale Kobolds  
Liberator of the Sootscale Tribe

#### Known For

Overthrowing [[Tartuk]]  
Destroying the idol of Old Sharptooth  
Defending kobold independence  
Establishing Sootscale Valley

---

### Appearance

Chief Sootscale is a dark-scaled kobold with sharp, disciplined eyes and the controlled bearing of a commander accustomed to being obeyed. He wears a ceremonial skull helm adorned with green plumage, along with simple armor and ornaments whose value lies in tradition rather than wealth.

Every part of his appearance reflects hard-won authority, tribal memory, and his determination that the Sootscales should never again be ruled through deception or fear.

---

### History

Chief Sootscale rose to lead the kobolds beneath [[Locations/Inner Sea Region/River Kingdoms/Stolen Lands/Greenbelt/Kamelands/The Old Sycamore|The Old Sycamore]], but his authority was stolen by the gnome [[Tartuccio]], who disguised himself as a purple-scaled kobold prophet named Tartuk. Through threats, manipulation, and false miracles, Tartuk drove the tribe into fearful devotion to a supposed demon called Old Sharptooth and reduced Sootscale to a leader in name only.

The adventurers who would later found the Republic of Thumping Waters exposed Tartuk’s deception and helped Chief Sootscale reclaim control of his people. During the conflict, the Tubthumpers rescued the kobold Mikmek from mite captivity, destroyed the mite enclave beneath the Old Sycamore, and returned the tribe’s stolen idol of Old Sharptooth.

Once his authority had been restored, Chief Sootscale smashed the idol himself. The act publicly ended Tartuk’s hold over the tribe and broke the Sootscales’ formal ties to the cruel worship imposed upon them. Tartuk was slain soon afterward and reverted to his true form as Tartuccio, finally revealing the full extent of the fraud.

**The destruction of the idol marked both the end of Tartuk’s rule and the beginning of the Sootscales’ liberation.**

This victory became one of the earliest foundations of the relationship between the [[Groups/Sootscale Kobolds|Sootscale Kobolds]] and the future Republic of Thumping Waters. Chief Sootscale had good reason to distrust outsiders, but the actions of the Tubthumpers convinced him that cooperation did not always mean submission.

---

### Leader of Sootscale Valley

Under Chief Sootscale’s leadership, the kobolds left behind the fear and fanaticism of Tartuk’s rule and began the difficult process of rebuilding their society. He encouraged discipline, stability, and traditions that strengthened the tribe without returning it to cruelty or demon worship.

As Thumping Waters expanded, the Sootscales became full citizens of the republic, and their caverns and surrounding lands developed into Sootscale Valley. The settlement became an important center of mining, providing stone, ore, and other raw materials essential to the republic’s growth.

Chief Sootscale served as both leader and guardian during this transition. He supported integration with the republic but remained fiercely protective of the kobolds’ independence, customs, and political rights. He had already seen one outsider seize control of his people through deception and refused to allow gratitude toward the Tubthumpers to become unquestioning obedience.

> Cooperation did not mean submission.

His rule helped establish the principle that the kobolds of Sootscale Valley were not dependents of Thumping Waters, but equal citizens and partners within it.

---

### Personality

Chief Sootscale is disciplined, pragmatic, proud, and deeply protective of his people. He values order, stability, and respect, regarding disorder as a threat to everything the Sootscales fought to reclaim after Tartuk’s defeat.

He is capable of gratitude, but never meekness. Sootscale remembers betrayal too clearly to surrender his judgment to outsiders, even trusted ones. He prefers clear agreements, defined responsibilities, and consequences that are understood in advance.

His seriousness can make him appear humorless, especially beside leaders such as [[Nok-Nok]], but it comes from responsibility rather than cruelty. Chief Sootscale spent much of his rule ensuring that his people would never again be frightened into following someone who claimed authority through spectacle and lies.

---

### Relationship with [[Greengripe]]

Chief Sootscale’s relationship with Greengripe was frequently strained during Nok-Nok’s governorship.

The goblins of Greengripe often treated pranks as a form of friendship and diplomacy. Chief Sootscale regarded frightened livestock, disrupted patrols, damaged property, and misplaced alchemical devices as threats to discipline and public safety rather than affectionate gestures.

He lodged repeated complaints with the government of Thumping Waters and pressed the Tubthumpers to restrain Greengripe before the disputes escalated into open hostility. Nok-Nok, for his part, struggled to understand why the kobolds refused to appreciate what he considered generous gifts of laughter.

#### Nok-Nok’s View

Chaos could strengthen a community by teaching its people to laugh at fear.

#### Sootscale’s View

Order protected a community from falling back into fear in the first place.

Despite their mutual frustration, the dispute never erased Sootscale’s loyalty to the republic or Nok-Nok’s contributions to it. Relations between Sootscale Valley and Greengripe improved after [[Kereek]] succeeded Chief Sootscale as governor, as Kereek proved more willing to tolerate Nok-Nok’s methods and negotiate around the resulting problems.

---

### End of His Governorship

Chief Sootscale no longer serves as governor of Sootscale Valley. He was succeeded by Kereek, whose more flexible and politically ambitious approach marked a new period in the settlement’s development.

The change did not erase Chief Sootscale’s achievements. By the time he left office, the Sootscales had been freed from Tartuk’s influence, integrated into the Republic of Thumping Waters, established as respected citizens, and secured control over one of the republic’s most productive mining regions.

**Kereek inherited a stable community because Chief Sootscale had spent years making stability possible.**

---

### Legacy

Chief Sootscale is remembered as the leader who reclaimed his tribe from Tartuk, destroyed the idol of Old Sharptooth, and guided his people from an isolated and frightened tribe into full membership within the Republic of Thumping Waters.

His leadership was not colorful or theatrical. It was cautious, demanding, and sometimes inflexible. It was also exactly what the Sootscales needed after years of manipulation and fear.

Chief Sootscale ensured that his people survived their liberation, preserved their dignity, and entered the republic on their own feet rather than upon their knees.
