## Kereek

*Governor of [[Sootscale Valley]] • Clever Tongue • Red Scales • Schemer with a Soft Spot*

> A larger enemy is not necessarily a stronger enemy. It is merely a larger target with more ways to become tired.
>
> — A principle frequently attributed to Governor Kereek

#### Titles and Roles

Governor of Sootscale Valley  
Representative of the Republic’s kobold population  
Former servant of the Tubthumpers

#### Known For

Political maneuvering  
Careful bargaining  
Ruthless tactics  
Unacknowledged loyalty to [[Prazil]]

---

### Overview

Kereek is a sharp-minded, red-scaled kobold whose tongue is as quick as his wit. Draped in a patchwork orange robe and armed with blades, bravado, and a seemingly endless supply of opinions, he has always preferred manipulation to muscle and strategy to strength. He thrives on outsmarting others through clever bargains, misleading flattery, carefully chosen threats, and tactics ruthless enough to work even when the plan itself does not.

In Kereek’s estimation, he is a genius tactician, gifted statesman, and natural leader. In truth, he is clever, resourceful, and considerably more capable than many initially assume, though not always quite as brilliant as he believes. His oversized ego can lead him to underestimate threats, overplay his hand, or mistake good fortune for proof of flawless planning. Even so, few can match his survival instincts, and fewer still possess his nerve.

---

### History

Kereek first came to the attention of the Republic of Thumping Waters after he and his companion Prazil escaped enslavement under the Troll King. Their flight required equal parts cunning, desperation, and luck, though Kereek’s account places considerably less emphasis on the latter two. After making contact with the Tubthumpers, the pair offered their knowledge and assistance against the trolls, beginning a cautious relationship with the young republic.

Kereek initially served the Tubthumpers with calculated loyalty. He was eager to prove his usefulness, secure protection for himself and Prazil, and ensure that no one dismissed him as merely another frightened kobold refugee. His service was rarely selfless, but it was dependable when it mattered.

Over time, ambition gave way to investment. Kereek came to see the republic not only as a source of opportunity, but as a place in which kobolds could build something lasting.

---

### Governor of Sootscale Valley

Kereek eventually succeeded [[Chief Sootscale]] as the leader of Sootscale Valley and now serves as its governor within the Republic of Thumping Waters.

His rise brought a marked change in the settlement’s leadership. Chief Sootscale had ruled through suspicion, pride, and a rigid concern for authority. Kereek is no less proud, but he is far more willing to negotiate, compromise, and exploit an opportunity when one presents itself. Where his predecessor often saw neighboring settlements as potential threats, Kereek is more inclined to see possible trading partners, political allies, or useful sources of leverage.

As governor, Kereek oversees Sootscale Valley’s civic affairs, trade, defense, and relationship with the wider republic. His leadership is practical and frequently calculating, but he is genuinely committed to the settlement’s survival and prosperity. He understands that the kobolds of Sootscale Valley are stronger as citizens of Thumping Waters than they would be as an isolated tribe, and he works to ensure they are neither ignored nor taken for granted.

Kereek’s administration favors careful bargaining and preparation over displays of force. He would rather make an enemy dependent upon Sootscale goods than defeat them in battle, though he is perfectly willing to arrange the latter when the former proves impossible.

> Allow larger enemies to exhaust themselves before striking.
>
> — A recurring theme in Kereek’s speeches

### Relationship with [[Greengripe]]

Relations between Sootscale Valley and Greengripe improved after Kereek replaced Chief Sootscale.

The old chief had little patience for [[Nok-Nok]]’s conviction that pranks were expressions of friendship, particularly when they involved frightened livestock, damaged property, or alchemical residue. Kereek did not necessarily find Greengripe’s antics amusing, but he understood Nok-Nok well enough to distinguish mischief from hostility.

The two governors developed a workable relationship based on mutual respect, practical cooperation, and a shared commitment to their people. Nok-Nok’s chaos occasionally tested Kereek’s patience, while Kereek’s habit of treating every conversation as a negotiation occasionally tested Nok-Nok’s attention span. Even so, disputes between their settlements became manageable rather than dangerous.

Kereek respected what Nok-Nok had achieved in Greengripe, even if he considered the goblin’s methods needlessly loud. Nok-Nok, in turn, appears to have regarded Kereek as one of the few kobolds who understood that a prank could be both irritating and affectionate.

---

### Prazil

Kereek’s constant companion is Prazil, a powerful kobold whom Kereek habitually treats as a dull-witted subordinate. He gives the orders, takes the credit, corrects Prazil publicly, and explains even the simplest plans as though addressing someone of very limited intelligence.

Beneath the bluster lies a genuine and deeply rooted affection that Kereek refuses to acknowledge. Prazil protected him during their enslavement and escape, and Kereek has never forgotten it. He may insult Prazil, exploit his loyalty, or threaten to abandon him when danger approaches, but anyone who seriously harms the larger kobold risks becoming the object of Kereek’s patient and intensely personal revenge.

> Their relationship is less that of a master and servant than two [[Survivors]] who have spent too long depending on one another to admit how much that dependence matters.

---

### Personality

Kereek is ambitious, vain, suspicious, and relentlessly opportunistic. He dislikes being overlooked and responds poorly to anyone who mistakes his size for weakness. Praise is the quickest way to earn his attention, though not necessarily his trust, while public embarrassment is rarely forgotten.

He prefers plans that place danger at a safe distance from himself, but he is not a coward. Kereek survived slavery, escaped the Troll King, and built a place for himself within the republic through persistence and nerve. When forced into danger, he fights viciously and without concern for fairness.

His loyalty is cautious rather than unconditional. Kereek does not give trust freely, but once he decides that a person, settlement, or cause belongs to him, he becomes surprisingly protective. He may describe that protectiveness as strategic necessity, political foresight, or the defense of valuable assets. Few who know him are fooled.

---

### Status within Thumping Waters

Kereek is now an established governor and an important representative of the republic’s kobold population. His relationship with the Tubthumpers has developed from wary cooperation into genuine allegiance, though he would likely object to any description suggesting sentimentality.

He remains ambitious and has no intention of allowing Sootscale Valley to become a minor or forgotten corner of the republic. Under his leadership, the settlement seeks greater influence, stronger trade, and a louder voice in national affairs.

Kereek once looked upon the young Republic of Thumping Waters and saw room at the top for someone clever enough to seize it.

**He was not entirely wrong.**
