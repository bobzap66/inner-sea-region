# Imeckas Stroon

![](../../../assets/images/imeckas-stroon-portrait.webp)

**Imeckas Stroon** is the court wizard of [Fort Drelev](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Fort%20Drelev/Fort%20Drelev.md) and brother to [Pavetta Stroon-Drelev](Pavetta%20Stroon-Drelev.md). Originally from Galt, he cultivated his magical talents among minor noble circles where arcane ability was as much a status symbol as a discipline. Imeckas possesses keen intellect, extensive training, and the temperament of a man who considers himself the cleverest person in any room—usually to his detriment.

  

Under Baron Hannis Drelev’s rule, Imeckas served as an adviser, arcane researcher, and political shield. Though not directly responsible for the baron’s cruelties, he enabled them through calculated inaction and the comfortable belief that his work was “above” the needs of the common folk. Imeckas prefers theory to practice, manipulation to leadership, and the sound of his own voice over nearly any alternative.

  

When the Tubthumpers breached the keep, Imeckas was confronted by a shaken Baroness Pavetta and a party of invisible revolutionaries who had just slaughtered half the foyer guards. His first instinct was to negotiate from a position of presumed superiority; his second was to reassess once it became clear the Tubthumpers were not impressed. Imeckas attempted to bargain for Pavetta’s continued rule in exchange for betraying Drelev, but the party refused any deal that preserved the old regime’s power unchecked.

  

Ultimately, Imeckas provided valuable intelligence on the baron’s location, partly out of self-preservation and partly out of the belated realization that the people of Fort Drelev might deserve better than fear and hunger. He remains within the keep, his political future uncertain. Though still arrogant and self-satisfied, he appears willing—for now—to accept the new limits imposed by Thumping Waters, if only because resistance would be profoundly inconvenient.

  

Whether Imeckas adapts into a functional state wizard, flees to more comfortable pastures, or becomes a lingering problem for the new administration is still an open question. His talents are undeniable. His humility, less so.
