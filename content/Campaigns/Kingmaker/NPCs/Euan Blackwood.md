# Euan Blackwood

![](../../../assets/images/euan-blackwood-portrait.jpeg)

*Herbalist · Healer · Husband of Mairi*

Soft-spoken in manner and silent by nature, Euan Blackwood is the calm to [Mairi Blackwood](../Characters/Mairi.md)’s storm. A skilled herbalist with a deep connection to the wilds of the [Stolen Lands](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Stolen%20Lands.md), Euan tends to his craft with patience, care, and quiet devotion. His deafness is not a hindrance, but simply another way in which he sees the world differently — he communicates primarily through sign language and subtle expressions, which those close to him have learned to read like poetry.

Though unassuming in appearance and demeanor, Euan is a steady and grounding presence. His knowledge of regional plants, salves, and tonics has helped save lives throughout Thumping Waters, and his quiet wisdom often carries more weight than shouted commands. He rarely seeks recognition — content to let others take the spotlight — but those who spend time with him know his strength lies in resilience, kindness, and a clear-eyed understanding of the land and its people.

He is fiercely loyal to his wife, Mairi, whom he loves without reservation. Their bond is unshakable, forged in shared hardship and profound mutual respect. Where she is a tempest, he is the still water beneath it — steady, strong, and quietly vital.
