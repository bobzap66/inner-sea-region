# Loy Rezbin

![[loy-rezbin-portrait.png]]

*Village-Born Dreamer · Civic Heart · Builder of Better Tomorrows*

Loy Rezbin is a broad-shouldered, bright-eyed idealist with calloused hands and a head full of blueprints. Born in a village that withered from neglect, Loy swore he’d one day build a better home — not just for himself and his wife, Latricia, but for any soul willing to work for a better life. When he heard that the Republic of Thumping Waters was carving civilization from wilderness, he saw his chance.

The Tubthumpers granted him a charter to found a new settlement, and so [[Tatzlford]] was born — a riverside village raised plank by plank with grit, sweat, and joy. Loy led the effort not with pomp, but with presence: lifting beams, organizing volunteers, and offering speeches that stirred tired workers into proud settlers. Under his guidance, Tatzlford grew quickly — a place of trade, shelter, and hard-earned hope.

Now, Tatzlford stands strong as one of the Republic’s most successful settlements — a testament to what can be built with courage, community, and a little idealism. Loy continues to serve as its mayor, though he still insists he’s just “the guy with the hammer and the clipboard.” His hands are still calloused. His dream is still growing.
