*Silk-Clad Schemer · Gnome in Name Only · Master of Delusion*

Impeccably dressed in rich robes and adorned in silks more fitting a prince than a pathfinder, Tartuccio never quite looked the part of an adventurer. From the start, he radiated arrogance—brash, self-important, and utterly convinced of his own superiority. He belittled his fellow expeditioners, dismissing them as unworthy of the charter to rule the [[Stolen Lands]] and declaring himself the only rightful candidate.

But behind the bombast was something darker.

The party later discovered Tartuccio alive and in disguise, masquerading as the kobold shaman [[Tartuk]] to manipulate the Sootscale tribe for his own ends. What his true intentions were—whether political sabotage, magical experimentation, or something more insidious—remain unclear. His charade came to a violent end on 21 Pharast, 4710, when the Tubthumpers exposed and defeated him beneath the Sootscale caverns.

His schemes, like his ego, crumbled in the dark.
