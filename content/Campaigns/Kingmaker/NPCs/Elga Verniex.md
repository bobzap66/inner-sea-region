# Elga Verniex

![[elga-verniex-portrait.png]]

Elga Verniex, known to locals and wary travelers alike as the Old Beldame, is a reclusive figure who dwells in a crooked mud-brick hut along the misty shores of the Tuskwater. Rumors cling to her like swamp moss—some call her a hag, others a wise woman, but few dare to test the truth. A sorcerer touched by fey blood and steeped in uncanny lore, Elga commands a deep knowledge of the region’s rarest herbs and deadliest fungi. Her brews are said to cure what medicine cannot—or kill with equal ease. Most speak of her black-cap deathrattle tea in hushed tones: a bitter, potent concoction she sips with unnerving calm, claiming it sharpens the mind and quiets the spirit. She keeps her own company, tolerates few, and trusts fewer still—but those who earn her favor may find in her a powerful, if unsettling, ally.
