# Nilak

![[nilak-portrait.png]]

## Nilak, Chieftain of the [[Groups/Six Bears|Six Bears]]

### Titles

The Ice-Warden · Daughter of Winter’s Breath · Last Chieftain of the Six Bears

### Overview

Nilak is the proud and formidable chieftain of the last surviving members of the Six Bears following — a Kellid tribe once native to the Realm of the Mammoth Lords. A gifted spirit-talker and wielder of primal frost magic, she embodies both the fierce independence and the spiritual depth of her people. Once a healer and shaman, Nilak has become a leader hardened by loss, carrying the weight of her tribe’s extinction on her shoulders.

When the Tubthumpers first found her, she and seven young survivors were besieged by undead in the [[Locations/Inner Sea Region/River Kingdoms/Stolen Lands/Hooktongue Slough|Hooktongue Slough]], trapped behind a wall of ice that Nilak held together through sheer force of will. Exhausted and wounded, she had been fending off wave after wave of undead while protecting her kin — a testament to her resolve and power.

### Appearance

Nilak is tall and broad-shouldered, with the sculpted build of a hunter and the poise of a spiritual leader. Her dark hair is streaked with gray despite her youth, bound in braids wrapped with carved bone and tiny shards of ice. Her furs are patched and worn from years of travel, but she wears them with pride. Her gaze is steady and cold — the eyes of someone who has seen death too many times and lived to endure it.

### Personality

Nilak is proud, guarded, and utterly devoted to the remnants of her tribe. She speaks bluntly and without deference, especially to outsiders. Her temper is slow to rise, but once provoked, her anger burns cold and merciless. Though she once revered the spirits of her ancestors, the horrors that have befallen her people have tested her faith nearly to breaking.

To her, strength is survival, and mercy is a luxury the dead cannot afford. Yet beneath the ice of her demeanor lies a heart still capable of grief, compassion, and sacrifice — though few are allowed to see it.

### History

Nilak was born among the Six Bears, a following in the far north known for their mastery of the hunt and their devotion to ancient spirits of winter and strength. When [[Amiri]] — once one of their own — was exiled for the crime of kinslaying, Nilak was only a girl, but the scandal shaped her youth. She grew up hearing both sides of the tale: that Amiri was a murderer who broke sacred law, and that the elders had sent her to die out of jealousy and fear.

Years later, when the Six Bears journeyed south, disaster followed. Undead began hunting them, driven by an unseen will — a powerful ghost seeking a spirit trapped within Amiri’s greatsword. One by one, the Six Bears fell until only Nilak and seven young warriors remained, besieged and desperate when the Tubthumpers found them.

### Recent Events

After the Tubthumpers helped defeat the undead siege, a fragile peace was shattered by words rather than weapons. During the tense diplomacy that followed, [[Fitzroy|Fitzroy Fulgrim III]], champion of [[Erastil]] and leader of Thumping Waters, questioned Nilak’s right to act in the [[Stolen Lands]] and accused her people of pursuing vengeance instead of justice.

Nilak’s response was cold fury. She denounced him for his arrogance, declaring that he dishonored the god whose symbol he bore. The confrontation ended in bitterness — any hope of reconciliation or alliance was destroyed.

With her people weary and broken, Nilak ordered the remnants of the Six Bears to pack their things and head north. Before departing, she cast one final, damning declaration:

“If your kingdom covets the cursed blade so dearly, then let it be your burden. We’ve carried enough ghosts.”

### Role in the Campaign

Nilak represents the last flicker of the Six Bears’ legacy — proud, scarred, and unwilling to kneel to anyone. Her exit from the Stolen Lands leaves the Tubthumpers with both guilt and responsibility. The ghost still hunts the sword, and Nilak’s refusal to aid in resolving the curse means the burden now rests on Thumping Waters alone.

### Personality Notes & Dialogue Flavor

• “You wear your god’s mark, knight, but not his patience.”
• “The cold does not care for excuses. Only strength endures.”
• “We are the last of the Six Bears. Our dead are enough company for one lifetime.”

### Legacy

Nilak’s departure marks a turning point in the story of the Six Bears. Her decision to abandon the cursed sword to Thumping Waters severs the last tie between her people and Amiri’s past. Yet her influence lingers — in Amiri’s guilt, in the spirit still bound within the blade, and in the quiet question of whether forgiveness might someday thaw even the hardest frost.
