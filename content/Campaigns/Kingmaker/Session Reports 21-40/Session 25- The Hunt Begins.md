### From [Linzi](../NPCs/Linzi.md)’s Journal

*An account of events from 2 Erastus until 13 Arodus, 4711 AR, reconstructed from the testimony of those present*

The bridge of webs proved exactly as trustworthy as it looked. The creatures that had woven it emerged from the caves below, accompanied by an ogre spider large enough to make the chasm seem almost welcoming by comparison. The web lurkers even summoned unicorns into the battle. I have no objection to unicorns as a rule, but being attacked by one on behalf of murderous spiderfolk must be terribly confusing.

The Tubthumpers prevailed and recovered the long-dead dwarf from the far ledge. His softly glowing warhammer was indeed magical, and the party also found a useful talisman and a quantity of coin among the remains of the web lurkers’ previous victims. They spent the journey home reconnoitering more of the countryside and reached [Thumpington](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Thumpington/Thumpington.md) on 8 Erastus.

The following weeks were occupied by the less dramatic but equally necessary work of governing. Diplomatic envoys established friendly relations with Galt, Hymbria, and Outsea, while new trade routes opened with Galt and Outsea. An arcanist’s tower rose in Thumpington, a watchtower was built on [Longtail Island](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Longtail%20Island/Longtail%20Island.md), and [Sootscale Valley](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Sootscale%20Valley/Sootscale%20Valley.md) received a stonemason’s workshop. [Olegton](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Olegton/Olegton.md) and [Tatzlford](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Tatzlford/Tatzlford.md) grew as well, and the next month brought still more construction: a festival hall in the capital, an inn on Longtail Island, a smithy in Olegton, a jail in Tatzlford, and new housing in Sootscale Valley.

Not everyone welcomed that growth. A group of vandals, angry that the kobolds appeared to be receiving better treatment than they were, attempted to burn down Sootscale Valley’s new stonemason’s workshop. The Minister of Justice caught them before they could do serious harm. The republic still lost resources dealing with the damage, but the workshop—and, more importantly, the principle that Sootscale citizens are citizens—survived the night.

The Tubthumpers found time for private business between council meetings. [Fitzroy](../Characters/Fitzroy.md) hunted for income and refined his training. [Matteo](../Characters/Matteo.md) retrained and went searching for poison formulas and a set of doubling rings. Ally purchased a crafter’s eyepiece, improved one of her wands, and exchanged some of her old training for a deeper understanding of religion. [Ant](../Characters/Ant.md) went shopping. [Seamus](../Characters/Seamus.md) left no useful account of his activities. [Mairi](../Characters/Mairi.md) undertook the most ambitious project of all: teaching her owlbear cub to fetch. Reports differ on whether the cub understood the lesson or merely enjoyed making Mairi retrieve the same object repeatedly.

The weeks of administration ended with an invitation from Grand Ranger [Jamel Visser](../NPCs/Jamel%20Visser.md), leader of the Embeth Travelers. Jamel proposed a grand hunt in a monster-infested reach of the Gudrin River, both to make the region safer and to become better acquainted with the rulers of Thumping Waters. The Tubthumpers accepted and arrived at the Travelers’ rebuilt hunting lodge on the evening of 12 Arodus.

Jamel’s other guests were Annamede Belavarah, a celebrated songwriter and social commentator from [Pitax](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Pitax/Pitax.md), and the sisters Edina and Jolia Aldori, formidable swordfighters attached to [Varnhold](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Varnhold/Varnhold.md). The evening was intended for introductions, dinner, and friendly conversation. Naturally, it became a competition before the actual competition had even begun.

Ant impressed Jamel with his knowledge of survival, then turned around and impressed Annamede by discussing songwriting. Mairi recounted the defeat of the Beast and her attempt to raise its orphaned cub, a story that earned Jamel’s enthusiastic attention. Seamus proposed collaborating with Annamede on an anthem and demonstrated his Aldori training for Edina and Jolia. Ally found common ground with the sisters through their shared experience of noble society. Ant, apparently unwilling to leave any conversational field unconquered, also spoke to the Aldoris about strength training.

As dinner ended, Jamel explained the hunt’s true purpose. The Embeth Travelers had found a grievously wounded man named Vath Locher washed upon the bank of the Gudrin. Before [Dying](../../../Rules/Dying.md), Vath told them that a wyvern had slaughtered his family at their cabin upriver. When the Travelers investigated, they discovered signs of two other dangerous beasts in the same region: a hydra and a krooth.

Three monsters were more than Jamel wished to face with a single band of hunters, so he had transformed the danger into a contest. The group returning with proof of the greatest number of kills would receive eighty gold pieces, a returning runestone, and a healing wand. It was a clever way to gather help without admitting that help was needed, which suggests Jamel may understand politics rather better than he likes to claim.

The hunt began before sunrise on 13 Arodus. The competitors traveled upriver aboard the Embeth Travelers’ barge and separated upon reaching the hunting grounds. Edina and Jolia headed north. Annamede went east with her guards, showing rather more interest in observing the wilderness than killing anything in it. The Embeth rangers continued upriver in search of the krooth, while Jamel remained with the barge.

The Tubthumpers began at the ruined Locher family shack. The building held no obvious magic, but old trails of blood led away from it and north into the woods. They followed those grim traces until they found the creature responsible.

The wyvern waited ahead, every bit as large and murderous as Vath’s final warning had promised. The hunters readied their weapons, the beast turned toward them, and the first true contest of Jamel Visser’s grand hunt was about to begin.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
