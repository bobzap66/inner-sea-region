### From [Linzi](../NPCs/Linzi.md)’s Journal

*An account of events from Neth 12 through Kuthona 13, 4711 AR, reconstructed from the testimony of those present*

Defeating Tulvak freed the Golden Grove from the hag’s grasp, but it did not undo years of neglect or months of desecration. [Shelyn](../../../Rules/Deities/Shelyn.md) made her wishes known: the shrine was to be cleansed, restored, and returned to something worthy of the beauty it had been built to honor. [Seamus](../Characters/Seamus.md) agreed to help. [Valerie](../NPCs/Valerie.md), having already endured a duel, exposed a hag, and watched her former teacher discover that he had been made into a fool, felt she had done quite enough for one goddess.

“Stupid goddess,” was her considered theological opinion.

Sir Fredero Sinnet left the [Stolen Lands](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Stolen%20Lands.md) soon afterward. Perhaps he went in search of redemption. Perhaps he merely wanted to find somewhere no one had heard the story. Considering that I am writing it down, the second ambition may prove more difficult.

The Tubthumpers finally received some well-earned time to themselves. Ally devoted much of hers to magical study, learning new spells and inscribing runes upon the company’s equipment. The others rested, trained, retrained, shopped, or attended to the hundred small matters that accumulate whenever adventurers spend too long preventing disasters. [Ant](../Characters/Ant.md) and Seamus each kept three weeks in reserve, which is either admirable foresight or evidence that neither of them finished making a list.

After completing their survey of the southern [Narlmarches](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Greenbelt/Narlmarches.md), the company returned to [Thumpington](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Thumpington/Thumpington.md) on Kuthona 3. They found the kingdom still growing in their absence. A band of Galtan refugees had reached our borders seeking safety and citizenship, and they were welcomed. They also brought word of a valley beyond the mountains, rich in natural resources but overshadowed by a strange and ancient monolith. Whatever stood there had left the travelers with the distinct feeling that it was not merely old, but wrong.

The council concluded the month’s business on Kuthona 10. [Greengripe](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Greengripe/Greengripe.md) gained a town hall, [Olegton](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Olegton/Olegton.md) a marketplace, and Thumpington a cemetery. Roads stretched farther across the realm, the site of the fallen unicorn was claimed, and the Golden Grove was formally brought within our borders and cleared so that its restoration might begin. The goblin backlash continued to trouble the kingdom despite another effort to contain it. Some problems yield to heroism. Others require patient work, fair laws, and a refusal to let resentment become inheritance.

Among the smaller matters awaiting the Tubthumpers was an old locket they had carried for far too long. It had belonged to the hateful hermit they fought months before—a bitter man who, they later learned, had been [Bokken](../NPCs/Bokken.md)’s estranged brother. When Bokken opened the locket, he recognized the woman pictured inside as their mother.

There are treasures whose value cannot be weighed. Bokken nevertheless offered the company either coin or a generous credit from his alchemical stores, because sentiment and sensible business need not be enemies. Whatever quarrels had divided the brothers, one small piece of their family had finally found its way home.

With their affairs settled, the Tubthumpers turned east toward [Varnhold](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Varnhold/Varnhold.md). The farther they traveled, the quieter the settled country became. At an abandoned pig farm they found dead hogs strewn through a muddy pen and four enormous daeodons feeding upon the remains. The great boar-like beasts charged, smashing through the farm’s poor fencing, but the Tubthumpers defeated them.

The nearby farmhouses had not been ruined or emptied by ordinary abandonment. Tools, furniture, and personal belongings remained where their owners had left them, as though everyone had expected to return after a brief errand. At a tannery, several hides had been stretched out for curing. They resembled badly cut horse hides until closer examination revealed the truth: they had come from centaurs, with the humanoid portions removed.

A brewery beside the river offered a less dreadful discovery. Its sign showed a barrel and a smiling dwarf, and its stock of Cheerful Delver Stout remained sealed and drinkable. Beneath a loose floorboard, the company found a lockbox containing 115 silver pieces and the brewery’s recipe. I am told the stout lacked refinement. I am also told this has never prevented adventurers from drinking anything.

To continue toward Varnhold, the party crossed the Kiravoy River at a broad, muddy ford. The water was shallow through most of the crossing, but a deeper pool lay nearby. Two chuuls rose from it—great insectoid horrors with snapping claws and grasping tentacles—and attacked while the company was spread between water and bank.

The fight churned the ford into mud and foam. Steel struck chitin while magic flashed across the river’s surface, and the chuuls tried to seize anyone unfortunate enough to come within reach. Cold water, uncertain footing, and claws large enough to carry away a person made for a thoroughly miserable battlefield, but the Tubthumpers killed both creatures and reached the far bank.

They had survived the river. The road proved less forgiving.

Spriggans had concealed several deep, funnel-shaped pits beneath cloth and dirt, lining their sloping walls with sharpened wooden stakes. [Fitzroy](../Characters/Fitzroy.md) found one in the traditional manner: the ground collapsed beneath him, and he fell twenty feet into it. The spikes did not kill him, but they left him bloodied and in no condition to laugh at anyone else’s misfortune.

Beyond the trap lay Varnhold. No voices carried from its streets, no smoke rose from its chimneys, and no one came to investigate the fighting at the ford. The Tubthumpers had not yet entered the village, but the empty farms, the butchered centaurs, and the traps on the road had already delivered their warning.

Something had happened to Varnhold—and the silence was waiting for them to discover what.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
