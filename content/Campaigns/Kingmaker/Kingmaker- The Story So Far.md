# Kingmaker: The Story So Far

![](../../assets/images/shared-cover-8a1bfbff37cb.png)

### The Story So Far

The adventurers known as the Tubthumpers entered the Stolen Lands with little more than a charter, a collection of dangerous talents, and a remarkable ability to turn straightforward assignments into chaos. After defending Oleg’s Trading Post, defeating the region’s bandits, and overthrowing the Stag Lord, they founded the Republic of Thumping Waters.

  

#### Building a Republic

Founding a nation proved considerably harder than conquering one. The new rulers explored the wilderness, established settlements, negotiated alliances, and survived threats ranging from trolls and cultists to the monstrous Beast of Thumpington. They defeated the Cult of the Bloom, endured political scandals and strange curses, and gradually transformed a vulnerable frontier settlement into a growing republic.

  

#### Varnhold and the Eastern Lands

When the neighboring settlement of Varnhold mysteriously emptied, the Tubthumpers followed the trail into the Valley of the Dead. There they uncovered the work of the ancient cyclops lich Vordakai. His defeat brought Varnhold and new eastern territories into the Republic, but it also drew the attention of more powerful neighbors.

  

#### Blood, Chains, and Broken Kingdoms

The Republic was soon pulled into conflicts involving Fort Drelev, the Tiger Lords, and Armag the Twice-Born. The Tubthumpers liberated Drelev, broke the power of hostile warlords, and confronted dangers that tested both their armies and their ideals. Their victories made Thumping Waters one of the strongest nations in the Stolen Lands—and a threat that King Irovetti of Pitax could no longer ignore.

  

#### The War of the River Kings

Irovetti’s invitation to the Rushlight Tournament concealed preparations for war. Pitax attacked the Republic with mercenaries, giants, mammoth riders, and wyverns. After defending their settlements and dismantling Irovetti’s alliances, the Tubthumpers infiltrated Pitax, encouraged a popular uprising, and fought their way through the House of One Hundred Doors.

Irovetti was defeated, but his fall revealed larger mysteries. The party recovered the artifact sword Briar and learned that the nymph Nyrissa may have influenced many of the enemies who threatened the Stolen Lands. Pitax now faces an uncertain political future as the Republic decides what victory will mean.

  

#### The Western Frontier

Now 16th level, the Tubthumpers have begun a period of consolidation and exploration. Their attention has turned toward the Forest of a Thousand Voices, the mysteries beneath Candlemere, and dangers gathering along the Republic’s western border.

During a Victory Festival in Thumpington, Seamus proposed to Linzi—and she accepted. The celebration was interrupted by evidence that Seamus’s long-missing father, Mikhail, was still alive. The Tubthumpers rescued him from a criminal network that had manipulated Seamus for years and uncovered the truth behind his lifelong enemy: there was never a human named Reyoknar. The name was a mask used by Ilthuliak, an ancient umbral dragon who remains at large.

  

#### The Years Between

The months after Irovetti’s fall brought both celebration and change. Mairi returned from a private journey involving Amiri’s sword, having cleansed the spirit bound within it, and retired from public adventuring. Leroy gave one final performance before his death, and Seamus and Linzi were married in Thumpington.

Research continued at Candlemere while the ministers resumed surveying the Glenebon Uplands. They sighted the legendary wyvern Minognos-Ushad, the Eater of Kings, and defeated deserters from Kob Moleg’s fallen army. Fitzroy later resigned as Minister of Defense and returned to Brevoy; Ekundayo succeeded him in office.

The new year opened beneath a darker warning: fey activity is increasing across the Stolen Lands, and the Wild Hunt may be approaching. The Republic strengthened its defenses while the Tubthumpers turned their attention toward Candlemere and the western frontier.

  

#### Breaking the Seal

On Abadius 18, the Tubthumpers returned to Candlemere and deliberately unmade the ancient Pharasmian spiral seal beneath the ruined tower. The rite opened a shaft into a vast underground complex whose guardians had apparently remained at their posts for thousands of years. Seven Grave Knights were destroyed in two brutal engagements, and the party discovered a star-filled void into which several suits of cursed armor were cast rather than allowed to reform.

The expedition also encountered Korog, an undead warrior who claimed that the seal had contained his brother Foras and a cult called the Thresholders. Their work is tied to Yog-Sothoth and to gates and passages between worlds. Three ancient Morrigna psychopomps later confirmed much of Korog’s warning. After initially mistaking the party for Threshold cultists, the Morrignas granted the expedition temporary sanctuary and directed them toward the Hall of the Reliquaries, where Foras and his followers are believed to remain.

The Oculus of Abaddon has become an immediate danger as well. While the party examined the star-filled void, it compelled Ant to try to shove Elowyn into what he believed was an endless fall. Elowyn resisted the attempt, but the incident demonstrated that the artifact can now force Ant toward the direct murder of a companion rather than merely encourage cruelty or violence.

The Tubthumpers remain beneath Candlemere under Morrigna protection, preparing to continue deeper into the complex. The Republic has closed the island to civilian traffic while Linzi and the council begin researching Foras, the Thresholders, Yog-Sothoth, and the danger released—or exposed—when the ancient seal was broken.
