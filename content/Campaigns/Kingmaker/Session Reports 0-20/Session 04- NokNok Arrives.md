### From [Linzi](../NPCs/Linzi.md)’s Journal

*An account concerning events from approximately 8 through 21 Pharast, 4710 AR. The earliest portions were reconstructed from the accounts of those present.*

The surviving bandits captured at Oleg’s Trading Post had given the party directions to a camp beside the Thorn River. Soon after the attack on the trading post, [Seamus](../Characters/Seamus.md), [Mairi](../Characters/Mairi.md), [Ant](../Characters/Ant.md), and Ally followed those directions south. I was not yet traveling with them, so this first part of the account has been assembled from their recollections. All four agreed that the camp was well hidden, strongly defended, and occupied by considerably more bandits than anyone sensible would wish to encounter in a forest. Their agreement became less reliable when they began explaining whose plan had made the ensuing victory possible.

The bandits had built their camp among the trees beside the river, using elevated platforms and the surrounding woods to watch the approaches. Their leader was a woman named Kressle, who met the party’s attack with a pair of hatchets and no apparent interest in surrender. She was killed in the fighting, and the surviving bandits were either captured or scattered into the [Greenbelt](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Greenbelt/Greenbelt.md). With Kressle dead, the camp ceased to be an immediate threat to Oleg and Svetlana.

The party also learned that Happs and Kressle had served a greater power. A man calling himself the [Stag Lord](../NPCs/Stag%20Lord.md) had united much of the Greenbelt’s banditry beneath his command and ruled from a fortress farther south. The prisoners revealed its location, but the party wisely decided that knowing where an enemy lived was not the same thing as being prepared to knock upon his gate. They returned their attention to exploration, supplies, and several promises made to the Levetons.

One of those promises concerned moon radishes, a delicacy Svetlana needed for Oleg’s favorite soup. The nearest patch had already attracted a group of kobolds who had eaten themselves nearly senseless but remained entirely prepared to defend the remaining roots. The resulting struggle was, by all accounts, less dignified than the battle at Thorn River but no less energetic. The party defeated the kobolds and gathered enough radishes to satisfy Svetlana, proving that the work of future heroes occasionally consists of fighting for vegetables.

On the journey back to Oleg’s, they found a badly wounded ranger named [Ekundayo](../NPCs/Ekundayo.md) with a stray dog keeping close beside him. They tended his injuries and learned that a troll was responsible for his condition. Rather than leave the matter unfinished, they joined Ekundayo and his new companion in hunting the creature down. Together they killed the troll and returned to the trading post, where the party settled in for roughly a week of rest, repairs, [Crafting](../../../Rules/Crafting.md), and preparation.

It was during this interval that I caught up with them at Oleg’s. I had heard enough of their exploits to fill several pages and had accumulated considerably more questions than anyone appeared eager to answer. Kressle was dead, the trading post was safe, a troll had been slain, and Seamus was preparing to lead them beneath an ancient tree occupied by hostile gremlins. I had suspected at Lady Jamandi’s feast that I was looking at future heroes of the realm. Finding them at the frontier discussing bandits, soup, and subterranean mites did nothing to discourage me.

When the party set out for [The Old Sycamore](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Greenbelt/Kamelands/The%20Old%20Sycamore.md), Seamus remained briefly behind to complete the last of the preparations. I accompanied the others and offered what support I could in his absence. The enormous tree was home to a tribe of mites engaged in a vicious little war with the nearby [Sootscale Kobolds](../../../Groups/Sootscale%20Kobolds.md). The party attempted to infiltrate the mites’ warrens beneath the roots, while I did my best to provide suitable encouragement and avoid thinking too carefully about the quantity of earth above our heads.

Seamus caught up with us before we had progressed very far. His arrival was both convenient and, from my perspective, extremely welcome. With the party restored to its full strength, I withdrew from the active expedition and devoted myself to keeping the camp, tending supplies, and recording what I could. This was not cowardice. It was responsible allocation of talent. Seamus was considerably better suited to standing between the others and hostile creatures, and I was considerably better suited to writing about how handsome he looked while doing it.

Even with Seamus restored to them, the first advance beneath the tree ended in a fighting retreat. The party escaped the warrens, tended their wounds, and rested overnight before attempting another descent. During that uneasy pause, they encountered a scarred little goblin who introduced himself as [Nok-Nok](../NPCs/Nok-Nok.md) and announced, without hesitation or supporting evidence, that he was a hero.

Evidence followed shortly thereafter. Nok-Nok joined the party’s next assault upon the mites, fighting with an enthusiasm entirely disproportionate to his size. With his help, they pushed deeper into the warrens and rescued a kobold named Mikmek of the Sootscale tribe. From him they began to understand the conflict beneath the tree and learned that the mites possessed something sacred to the kobolds.

[The Old Sycamore](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Greenbelt/Kamelands/The%20Old%20Sycamore.md) had not yet been cleared, and the war between mites and kobolds remained unresolved. Nevertheless, the party had gained a guide, rescued a prisoner, and acquired a self-appointed goblin hero who seemed determined to prove his title correct. The Greenbelt, I was beginning to realize, had a very peculiar sense of humor.
