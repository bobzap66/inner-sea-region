### From [[Linzi]]’s Journal

*An account of events from 18 Lamashan until 22 Lamashan, 4710 AR, reconstructed from the testimony of those present*

This expedition marked the formal addition of [[Fitzroy]] Fulgrim III to the Tubthumpers. Ally’s cousin arrived with all the confidence one expects from a young paladin bearing a famous name, a polished shield, and the certainty that every moral question has a correct answer. The [[Stolen Lands]] have a talent for testing all three.

The company first travelled to the Iruxi village along the shore of Candlemere. Their reception was wary but peaceful. [[King Vesket]], a scarred and formidable warrior, listened to their words and measured them against the safety of his people. The village had placed its faith in an ancestral spirit called Stisshak, which appeared as the glowing image of an ancient Iruxi king and claimed to offer protection and guidance.

Bresque, troubled by the supposed ancestor’s influence, secretly led the Tubthumpers back into the village after nightfall. Their attempt at quiet investigation did not remain quiet for long. [[Mairi]] alerted the settlement, and armed Iruxi converged upon the spirit hut just in time to witness the truth.

Stisshak was no honored ancestor. The regal illusion split apart in sickly green light, revealing a will-o’-wisp lurking within the image of a floating reptilian skull. It struck with lightning and fed upon the fear it had cultivated among Vesket’s people. The Tubthumpers destroyed it before the assembled tribe, leaving no doubt that their revered protector had been a predator.

King Vesket did not offer an immediate answer regarding the future of his people. He asked for time to consider what had been revealed, and the company respected that request. There are victories won with swords, and others that begin only after the swords are lowered.

[[Kereek]] and [[Prazil]] then guided the company toward a concealed entrance into an ancient dwarven fortress occupied by the trolls. The passage, hidden within the remains of a vast purple worm, was unpleasant even by the generous standards of underground travel. Beyond it waited four savage trollhounds, followed by a cavern troll called Kargadd.

The fighting carried the Tubthumpers deeper into the ruined stronghold until they reached its throne room. There they confronted [[Hargulka]], the troll king who had gathered the tribes against [[Thumpington]], and Gurija, his sorcerous consort. Both were killed, breaking the leadership of the troll host and ending the immediate threat of an organized invasion.

Kereek later explained that events had unfolded precisely according to his plan. Prazil’s expression suggested this was the first he had heard of the plan, but he was loyal enough not to contradict his companion in front of witnesses.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
