### From [[Linzi]]’s Journal

*An account of events from approximately 17 Erastus until 8 Arodus, 4710 AR, reconstructed from the testimony of those present*

Having driven the quickling from the Forgotten Keep, the Tubthumpers paused just long enough to bind their wounds before approaching its central tower. The ancient elven building remained remarkably beautiful beneath the moss and creeping vegetation. [[Mairi]], who has developed an entirely reasonable suspicion of unusually decorative plants, warned everyone that the growth within might be dangerous.

[[Ant]] attempted to find a safe path through the undergrowth. This was a sensible plan, carefully undertaken, and completely unsuccessful.

A thick violet fog billowed through the hall. Everyone immediately held their breath, which would have been helpful had the poison required lungs rather than simply forcing itself into their thoughts. Wits dulled, senses twisted, and friends suddenly appeared to be enemies. [[Tristian]] and [[Connal]] began fighting one another while Mairi struggled to separate them. Ant fled up the stairs, seeking clean air and finding something considerably worse.

A beautiful woman waited in the chamber above, dressed in flowing finery and dancing as though the ruined tower were a royal ballroom. She beckoned to Ant with promises better left unrepeated in a respectable journal. Ant, being devoted to Ally and possessing more sense than many people would credit to a halfling wearing an enormous stag’s skull, rejected her advances.

The woman responded by raking her claws across his face.

Ally and [[Seamus]] heard Ant’s cries and rushed upstairs. They found their injured friend with the Dancing Lady—and promptly fell under her enchantment themselves. The creature drew Ally close and drank her blood, using the stolen vitality to close the wounds Ant had inflicted upon her. Seamus, too, stood captivated. I find it difficult to fault him for being vulnerable to beauty, though I might question his judgment concerning which beautiful women are likely to drink him.

The enchantment eventually loosened its grip, but the violet fog still clouded Ally’s mind. Unable to distinguish friend from foe, she began attacking everyone within reach. By then Mairi had separated Connal and Tristian and joined the struggle upstairs, leaving the others to avoid Ally’s magic while concentrating upon the true enemy.

Tristian finally cleared his thoughts enough to recognize the creature. The Dancing Lady was a baobhan sith, a predatory fey that ensnares its victims through beauty and movement before feeding upon their blood. More importantly, she was vulnerable to cold iron.

Armed with that knowledge, Mairi, Ant, Seamus, and Tristian brought the Dancing Lady down while doing their best not to hurt the still-confused Ally. When the creature died, her enchantments ended, and the Forgotten Keep finally fell silent.

The ruins contained a remarkable collection of elven treasures. Unfortunately, some of the finest pieces—including a life-sized statue of a dancing elven woman and an elaborate filigreed water clock—were far too large to carry home. The company marked them for later recovery. They also determined that the surviving structures could become the foundation of a proper castle if a settlement were ever established there.

Tristian, grateful for the trust the company had shown him, offered his continuing assistance. He volunteered to establish a shrine and an orphanage within settlements chosen by the Council. He also offered to teach Seamus a spell called *Dawnflower’s Light* when Seamus was ready to master it, and to share the secrets of *Blazing Blade* with any suitable divine or primal spellcaster. He has now become a true friend to the republic, which is fortunate, considering how frequently its leaders require someone capable of putting them back together.

The journey home was interrupted two days from [[Thumpington]] by a solitary wandering troll. There was no army, ambush, or grand design behind the encounter—merely an extremely large creature that happened to be travelling in the opposite direction and objected violently to sharing the road. The Tubthumpers defeated it and continued north, although the encounter added one more troll sighting to an increasingly worrying list.

Their return brought them immediately from the dangers of the wilderness to the dangers of government. The Council reorganized several responsibilities, with Seamus continuing his service as Minister of the Interior and [[Ekundayo]] assuming the office of Minister of Defense. Seamus handled the transition exceptionally well. I was not surprised, although I was perhaps more pleased than strict ministerial impartiality requires.

The Council formally claimed the territory surrounding Oleg’s trading post and attempted to establish a settlement there. The work proved far more expensive than planned, but [[Olegton]] was founded nonetheless. Houses followed soon afterward, transforming the lonely trading post into the beginning of a proper community.

Thumpington gained an herbalist, trade continued to improve, and assistance arrived from [[Varnhold]]. The blight that had threatened the republic’s crops also came to an end. An attempt to establish formal diplomatic relations with the local iruxi was less successful, though the exchange left some hope that another approach might fare better.

Loy and [[Latricia Rezbin]] then approached the Council with a proposal of their own. They wished to establish a freehold near the old tatzlwyrm lair. The Council agreed to support them, and [[Amiri]] accompanied the settlers into the wilds. Their new community was named [[Tatzlford]]: another small point of light on the republic’s expanding frontier.

Unfortunately, new roads and settlements were not the only things growing. Rumors spread of a secretive cult operating somewhere within Thumping Waters. Ally attempted to locate its members but found no meeting place, leaders, or reliable witnesses. The failed investigation only fed public anxiety, and unrest increased as citizens began seeing conspirators in every barn and cellar.

On the eighth of Arodus, [[Nok-Nok]] arrived from Olegton with still worse news. [[Survivors]] of the Tanglethorn goblin tribe—previously believed destroyed by a rampaging moose—had returned and were preparing to attack the new settlement.

The republic had acquired another town, a new freehold, a potential castle, and several promising institutions. It had also acquired a hidden cult, hostile goblins, and further evidence of trolls wandering near its borders. This appears to be how nations grow: every wall built creates another wall that someone must defend.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
