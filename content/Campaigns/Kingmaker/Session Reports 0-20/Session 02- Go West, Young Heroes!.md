### From [[Linzi]]’s Journal

*Calistril 24, 4710 AR*

After crossing the Great Hall, we continued into the western portion of the manor in search of Lady Jamandi. The Black Tears had left fires burning in our path, and the party was forced to stop and fight the flames before they could spread farther through the estate. Only after the fires were brought under control could we continue through the smoke, heat, shattered doors, and overturned furniture toward the sound of battle. We finally reached Lady Jamandi where the last of the Black Tears were making their stand.

The woman commanding the remaining assassins carried a wickedly curved greataxe. She struck Seamus once—only once—and very nearly killed him. I had spent much of the evening entertaining the idea that I had recognized a future hero before anyone else. Watching that future nearly end beneath an axe was a sharp reminder that heroism is not protection from death. For one terrible instant, I thought the story I had only just begun recording was already over.

It was not. Seamus survived, and the party helped Lady Jamandi bring down the last of the Black Tears. When the fighting finally stopped, the manor fell into a silence almost as unsettling as the battle. The wounded were tended, the dead were gathered, and Lady Jamandi’s household began the grim work of reclaiming the estate from the ruin left behind.

Lady Jamandi allowed the party to keep the weapons taken from the defeated attackers, along with the equipment they had borrowed and used in defense of her home. Among their spoils was an enchanted mace discovered earlier in the manor. It was a practical reward, if not a ceremonial one: arms placed immediately into the hands of people who had already demonstrated that they knew how to use them.

Later that day, Lady Jamandi gathered the survivors and presented the promised charters. Seamus, Mairi, Ant, and Ally received authority to travel into the [[Locations/Inner Sea Region/River Kingdoms/Stolen Lands/Greenbelt|Greenbelt]], explore the wilderness, and confront the banditry that had prevented lasting settlement. Yesterday they had been guests answering a noblewoman’s call. Today they carried [[Restov]]’s written authority into the [[Stolen Lands]].

![[session-02-go-west-young-heroes-image-01.webp]]

*Calistril 25, 4710 AR*

They departed the following morning. I did not travel with them—not yet. My road and theirs separated outside Lady Jamandi’s manor, although I found myself watching Seamus until distance made it impossible to distinguish his red hair from the rest of the company. I told myself this was because a responsible chronicler should observe her subjects for as long as circumstances permit. A responsible chronicler can apparently be very convincing when speaking to herself.

#### A Note Added Later

When our paths crossed again, I learned that their first journey west had taken them through Nivatka’s Crossing. They collected several local rumors there, but encountered nothing that detained them for long. Their charter, their newly won equipment, and the road into the Stolen Lands waited ahead.
