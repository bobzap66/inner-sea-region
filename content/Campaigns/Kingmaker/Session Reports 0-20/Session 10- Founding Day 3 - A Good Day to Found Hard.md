### From [[Linzi]]’s Journal

*1 Desnus through late Sarenith, 4710 AR*

On the first day of Desnus, we founded a kingdom. That is an astonishing sentence to write, particularly when I remember that only a few months ago we were sleeping under Lady Jamandi’s roof and trying not to be murdered by giants. Now there is a Republic of Thumping Waters, a capital called [[Thumpington]], and an entire government composed largely of people I have personally watched fall into holes.

![[session-10-founding-day-3-a-good-day-to-found-hard-thumping-waters-flag.webp]]

[[Mairi]] was chosen as Prime Minister, which seemed sensible. She has the necessary authority, the necessary determination, and a sufficiently terrifying glare for ending meetings. Ally Grainger became Minister of Magic; [[Amiri]], Minister of Defense; Antson “[[Ant]]” Crazyfoot, Minister of Intelligence; [[Jubilost Narthropple]], Minister of the Treasury; [[Seamus]], Minister of the Interior; [[Ekundayo]], Minister of Justice; and I became Minister of Communication. Mine is an office concerned with announcements, records, correspondence, and ensuring that the people know what their government is doing. In other words, they have made it my official duty to write things down and tell everyone about them. At last, responsible government demonstrates some sense.

We attempted to celebrate the founding with a public holiday. Food was provided. Entertainment was arranged. Speeches were prepared. Unfortunately, most of our new citizens were still living in tents and appeared considerably more interested in roofs, walls, and somewhere to purchase a drink than in listening to us congratulate ourselves. They gathered outside their canvas homes and regarded the festivities with the patient expressions of people waiting for their rulers to remember that shelter is generally considered useful.

“Next year,” Jubilost declared, surveying the celebration’s failure, “we shall endeavor to found the kingdom after constructing it.”

The holiday was not a success. The kingdom, fortunately, was.

During that first month, houses rose throughout Thumpington, lumber and food were purchased, and the [[Groups/Sootscale Kobolds|Sootscale Kobolds]] formally pledged themselves to the republic. Their people began settling into the life of the new realm with considerably less difficulty than some had predicted. Prime Minister Mairi’s government also opened relations with [[Varnhold]], and a promising vein at Crack Rock Cave was claimed and developed into an ore mine. Taxes were collected, investments secured, fish pulled from the rivers, and enough official paperwork created to make me suspect parchment may be our most important imported commodity.

Not all our problems could be solved around a council table. Fishermen had begun reporting an enormous snapping turtle in the waterways—a brute known locally as Old Crackjaw—which was smashing boats and making a menace of itself. I sent Arven to investigate. Yes, wilderness affairs technically belonged to Seamus and the [[Ministry of the Interior]]. I was Minister of Communication, however, the fishermen had communicated the problem to me, and I communicated to Arven that someone ought to do something about it. The administrative process was therefore flawless.

The Tubthumpers went after the beast themselves. On the road they were attacked by bandits, apparently proving that even founding a nation does not prevent people from trying to rob its rulers. The attackers were beaten, and the party continued with Mairi’s faithful hound, [[Connal]], once more beside her.

Old Crackjaw was worse than the fishermen’s stories suggested, and far less considerate than most monsters about choosing a convenient battlefield. The enormous turtle remained in deep water, forcing Mairi and Seamus to swim out and engage it where it was strongest. Ant and Ally stayed on the bank, Ant peppering the creature with arrows while Ally hurled spells across the water. Fighting is difficult enough without having to remember how to float at the same time.

Crackjaw struck with enough force to splinter boats and crush bone. It caught Mairi badly while she was in the water, and for one dreadful moment it appeared Thumping Waters might lose its first Prime Minister before she had completed her second month in office. Seamus fought beside her while Ant and Ally continued their barrage from shore, and together they finally killed the beast. Mairi survived, though rather more narrowly than anyone cared to contemplate.

When they returned, they found a worried man named Edrist Hanvaki waiting for them. His brother, Temin, had departed to arrange a gemstone agreement with Varnhold and had not returned. Edrist described the mother-of-pearl brooch Temin wore and asked the Tubthumpers to watch for any sign of him. In a new kingdom there is apparently no shortage of missing people, dangerous roads, or frightened relatives seeking the only heroes they know by name.

Sarenith brought further efforts to turn our optimistic collection of tents into a functioning nation. Trade with Varnhold expanded to include food, ore, and luxury goods. Diplomatic contact was established with [[Restov]], and preparations began for regular trade between us. A mill was constructed in Thumpington, a stone quarry was established in newly claimed territory, and the kingdom’s flag began to take shape. Our attempts to improve everyone’s standard of living were less successful, but the Treasury assures me that failure becomes much more respectable when entered correctly in a ledger.

Amiri also organized a hunting expedition. It did not proceed as intended. Rather than returning with a neat supply of game, the hunters succeeded in driving a considerable quantity of angry wildlife toward the town. By the time the confusion ended, a rumor had spread that the government had arranged a grand royal hunt for the entertainment of the public. I attempted to correct this. I was informed that the inaccurate version was much more exciting.

The month concluded with the dead rising from unconsecrated burial grounds near the settlement. Thumping Waters’ newly organized militia was called out, and this time our preparations held. The undead were put down swiftly, the townsfolk were protected, and the crisis passed without lasting harm. It was the militia’s first true test and, unlike the holiday, one we could call an unqualified success.

Thus ended the first two months of Thumping Waters: a failed festival, a working mine, new houses, new allies, profitable trade, marauding wildlife, walking corpses, and a turtle that very nearly killed the head of government. It was not an elegant beginning, but it was ours.

Kingdoms, I am learning, are not founded in a single ceremony. They are founded every morning afterward, when everyone wakes to discover the work is still there.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
