### From [[Linzi]]’s Journal

*Calistril 24, 4710 AR*

I had intended my first night at Lady Jamandi’s manor to end with several pages of observations, a little music, and perhaps a few hours of sleep. Instead, sometime before dawn, I awoke to a scream, the crash of something heavy, and the unmistakable sound of people being murdered in the corridor. There are occasions when history announces itself with trumpets. This particular occasion arrived with splintering doors and blood on the floorboards.

The manor had been invaded by killers bearing the mark of the Black Tears. They moved from room to room, cutting down guards and guests before most of us properly understood what was happening. In the confusion, I found myself trapped in a room with an ogre. It was enormous, furious, and positioned inconveniently between me and every sensible means of escape. I managed to strike it blind with a spell, which improved my chances from “none whatsoever” to “possibly, if it trips over the furniture.” I then hid while it blundered around the room, smashing anything it could reach and coming much too close to finding me.

Then the door burst open, and in came [[Seamus]], [[Mairi]], [[Ant]], and Ally.

I had wondered during the feast whether I was looking at future heroes. It appears I may have underestimated how quickly they intended to begin proving me right. They rushed the blinded ogre together and killed it before it could lay its hands on me. I would like the historical record to show that I had the situation largely under control. I would also like it to show that I was extremely pleased to see them—Seamus perhaps a little more than was necessary for a strictly professional account.

They did not fight like four strangers who had happened to share a table. Mairi was Seamus’s aunt, and even amid the confusion she watched him with the particular concern of family. Ant and Ally were married and possessed the quiet familiarity of two people accustomed to accounting for one another without discussion. Ant and Seamus had served together in the [[Brevoy]] Foreign Legion, and when the fighting began, they slipped back into the practiced rhythm of soldiers who had already survived danger side by side. Whatever had brought them to Lady Jamandi’s feast, their company had roots extending well beyond that evening.

There was no time for proper introductions or gratitude. The manor was still under attack, and every corridor seemed to contain another fight. We battled Black Tears assassins through the halls and gathered the surviving guests we could find. [[Amiri]], [[Harrim]], and [[Valerie]] were rescued amid the violence, while [[Jaethal]] and [[Tartuccio]] were found later as we pushed farther through the manor. By sunrise, those of us who had been merely fellow guests were becoming something closer to companions.

Then we encountered a frost giant. For one long, dreadful moment, no one attacked. The giant spoke, or demanded, or perhaps warned us—we could not understand a word of it. We tried to answer, but it understood us no better. Both sides watched the other for some sign of intent, each movement seeming more threatening because it could not be explained. I do not know whether peace was ever possible. I only know that the silence tightened until someone moved, and then there was no room left for questions. The fight was brutal, and the party killed the giant.

The attack was not yet over. Fighting still echoed from elsewhere in the manor, and Lady Jamandi had not been found. We were exhausted, bloodied, and surrounded by the wreckage of what had been a splendid feast only hours earlier. Yet Seamus and his companions kept moving toward the danger.

I followed them.
