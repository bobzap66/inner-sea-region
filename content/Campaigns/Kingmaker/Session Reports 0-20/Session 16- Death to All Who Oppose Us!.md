### From [Linzi](../NPCs/Linzi.md)’s Journal

*An account of events from 1 Rova until approximately 8 Rova, 4710 AR, reconstructed from the testimony of those present*

[Mairi](../Characters/Mairi.md) returned from her diplomatic mission to Nivaka’s Crossing on the first of Rova, just as the council settled in for another long stretch of kingdom business. There were roads to discuss, stores to count, complaints to answer, and—as it happened—a nest of vipers hiding beneath our feet.

The cult troubling [Thumpington](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Thumpington/Thumpington.md) had escaped our first efforts to uncover it, but [Matteo](../Characters/Matteo.md) Auditore, our new Minister of Justice, set his agents to the task. Their work traced the cultists to a lonely barn on the outskirts of the capital, where they gathered on Sunday evenings. On the second of Rova, the Tubthumpers and [Amiri](../NPCs/Amiri.md) went to pay them a visit.

A concealed trapdoor beneath the barn led to a cellar consecrated to Gyronna, the Angry Hag—a goddess of hatred, spite, and the sort of whispered cruelty that turns neighbor against neighbor. Five women waited below, dressed in ragged ritual garments and gathered around an altar crowned by a glittering stone carved like a bloodshot eye. Their leader was Malgorzata Niska, a respected midwife who had concealed a far darker calling behind that trusted face.

The cultists attacked as soon as they were discovered. The battle was quick, close, and merciless. Malgorzata and all four of her followers were killed, bringing the cult’s work in Thumpington to a bloody end.

Behind the altar, the Tubthumpers found a wooden coffer containing a substantial payment and a short note: “In appreciation of our common cause.” It bore no name, only three black teardrops—the mark of the Black Tears, the killers who attacked Lady Jamandi’s manor. Their grudge had followed us south, and this time they had tried to poison our kingdom without ever drawing their own blades.

There was little time to enjoy that victory. On the sixth of Rova, a farmhand named Saki was murdered while gathering starberries after dark. The following night, a shepherd named Beven and all six of his sheep were slaughtered while returning from pasture. Their bodies had been savaged and partly eaten by something resembling a great wolf or warg, but the marks upon them suggested something capable of using both jaws and human hands.

The Tubthumpers followed a set of enormous wolf tracks to a bloodstained copse and eventually traced the killings to Kundal, a trapper who had arrived in the kingdom only a few months earlier. Kundal had been cursed shortly after coming to the [Stolen Lands](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Stolen%20Lands.md). He remembered only dreams of blood and fury, followed by mornings spent waking in the forest in torn clothing, with no understanding of what he had done.

When the moon called to him again, Kundal transformed and attacked. The Tubthumpers fought him beneath the trees, but they did not kill him. They subdued the werewolf and took the man alive, ending the murders before he could claim a third victim. Kundal was later cured of the curse—a mercy that does the Tubthumpers greater credit than another corpse ever could.

Among Kundal’s possessions was a ledger recording the animals he had trapped and delivered. One unusually large order named a bandit called Eirikk in [Hooktongue Slough](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Hooktongue%20Slough/Hooktongue%20Slough.md), who had purchased three brush thylacines, a tiger, and a boar with instructions that they be delivered alive and fit for training. Kundal’s enchanted composite longbow was also recovered from his quarters.

Naturally, [Grigori](../NPCs/Grigori.md) found a way to turn even this into an accusation. He began telling anyone willing to listen that our explorations were drawing monsters toward Thumpington and that our government invited the very dangers from which we claimed to protect the people. A dead cult, two murdered citizens, a captured werewolf, and the mark of old enemies lurking behind it all—and still that man could find room atop the pile to deliver a speech.

The Black Tears wanted hatred to take root here. Gyronna’s servants wanted neighbor to turn against neighbor. Kundal’s curse wanted only blood. None of them got what they wanted—not entirely, at least—but I fear Grigori understands that fear can survive long after the monster responsible for it has been defeated.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
