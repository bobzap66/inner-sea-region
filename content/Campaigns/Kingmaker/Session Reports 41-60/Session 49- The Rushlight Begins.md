> #### A Warning in the Palm
>
> During the festival, Ally submitted to a palm reading by a Varisian vendor calling herself Mistress Aveline. Aveline’s interpretation suggested that movement would be necessary for Ally to preserve her authority, and that leadership would become a shared burden—a crown of thorns rather than a solitary honor.
>
> Most troublingly, she predicted betrayal not by an enemy, but by someone who believed they were acting as Ally’s protector. Divination offered in a festival tent should not be mistaken for verified intelligence. In light of the incident described below, however, the warning merits preservation.
>
> #### The Destroyed Wand
>
> Antson Crazyfoot destroyed a wand intended as a gift for Ally. He reported that a dream had convinced him the wand posed a threat to Ally’s familiar. Rather than consult Ally or another member of the company, he acted alone and described the destruction as necessary for her protection.
>
> This behavior is consistent with the escalating influence of the Oculus of Abaddon. The artifact appears increasingly capable of turning suspicion into certainty and presenting harmful preventative acts as unavoidable necessities. Ant may sincerely believe that he is protecting those around him. That conviction makes the influence more dangerous, not less.
>
> #### The Orange-Haired Gnome
>
> The wand was subsequently replaced by unknown means and delivered to Ally by an unidentified orange-haired gnome. The individual’s name, allegiance, and means of acquiring the replacement remain unknown.
>
> Possible explanations include an extraplanar agent, an interested patron, or an individual already monitoring the party. Until the gnome is identified, the replacement wand should be treated cautiously and the incident kept from public circulation.

## THUMPING WATERS TAKES THE FIRST SHOT

*Antson Crazyfoot wins the Rushlight archery championship and places the republic upon the tournament board.*

*By Tobin Bramblefoot, Games and Society Correspondent*

---

The Rushlight Tournament has begun, and Thumping Waters has already placed its first victory upon the board.

Antson “Ant” Crazyfoot defeated a field drawn from across the River Kingdoms to claim the opening archery championship. Navarathna of Mivon took second place, while Pitax’s reigning champion failed to place upon his own field.

**OPENING RESULTS**

**First Place:** Antson Crazyfoot of Thumping Waters

**Second Place:** Navarathna of Mivon

**Disqualified:** Ilraith Valadhkani of Daggermark

**Tournament Leader:** Thumping Waters

### BEFORE THE ROAD TO RUSHLIGHT

*By Mara Venn, Managing Editor*

Before departing, the Tubthumpers concluded two pieces of unfinished business from their recent expedition. High Marshal Valerie’s longstanding bounty for Speartooth was collected and divided, while Harrick Fenlock agreed to preserve and mount the head of the great lake monster slain in Hooktongue Slough.

If the work survives both the taxidermy and the journey home, the creature will eventually be displayed in the national museum.

The delegation also carried a generous supply of Bixen’s Berry Best blackberry mead. Bixen expects the competitors to drink it before the Midnight Joust, apparently on the theory that victory is improved by poor balance and excellent publicity. The Lantern and Ledger has not endorsed this theory, but will report the results faithfully.

---

### A KING DRESSED FOR ADVENTURE

*By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication*

Delegations arrived at the Rushlight amid a gathering of competitors, performers, and dignitaries from across the River Kingdoms. King Castruccio Irovetti welcomed his guests with all the confidence and theater for which he is known.

His Majesty looked less like a king holding court than an adventurer preparing to raid a ruin. Of particular interest were several pieces of strange equipment adorned with lights and mechanisms said to be of Numerian origin. Whether these objects were weapons, ornaments, or both was not immediately explained.

Nunzio Arpaia was appointed to guide the Thumping Waters delegation through the grounds. He pointed out the contest fields, the menageries, the markets, and—most urgently for several members of the company—the food stalls to the southeast.

### MARKETS OF THE RUSHLIGHT

*By Sella Copperclaw, Civic Reporter*

Merchants from across the River Kingdoms have transformed the festival grounds into a temporary town of silk, canvas, food, curiosities, and ambitious prices.

The Old Beldame is among those who have erected temporary shops. So too is Crazy Akbar, proprietor of Crazy Akbar’s Adventurer’s Supply, whose stall appears to contain nearly every wondrous object a traveler might require and several no sensible traveler should touch.

Akbar announced a generous first-day discount to the Tubthumpers. The precise generosity of this offer decreased each time he remembered it. Shoppers are advised to obtain all discounts in writing, preferably while the merchant is still describing them.

---

### BOATS UPON THE RUSHLIGHT

*By Tobin Bramblefoot, Games and Society Correspondent*

Not every contest at Rushlight awards points. Velka found a quieter one beside the river, where she sat among a group of children and helped them fold paper boats. They launched the little fleet together and watched it race upon the current.

Before leaving, Velka gave the children coins and instructed them to “buy something sweet and argue about it later.” This advice was followed with admirable speed.

She then placed a considerably larger sum upon Thumping Waters to win the tournament outright. The odds were generous. Velka’s confidence was more generous still.

---

## SIX ARROWS FOR GLORY

*By Tobin Bramblefoot, Games and Society Correspondent*

The formal competition opened on Starday, Sarenith 7, with the archery championship. Each archer was given six arrows and six rounds in which to loose them at three increasingly difficult targets.

The nearest blue target stood fifty feet away and offered a single point. A reinforced green owlbear at one hundred and fifty feet offered three. The red iron dragon waited at four hundred and fifty feet and offered five. A bullseye tripled the value of a hit, while finishing early earned additional points. An archer who missed with every shot would be disqualified, although embarrassment would likely have rendered the official ruling unnecessary.

#### Champions and Cheats

The field included some of the finest archers in the River Kingdoms. It also included Villamor Koth, Pitax’s reigning champion, who suffered a disastrous performance and failed to place. There are days when a bow refuses to cooperate with its owner. This was apparently one of them.

Ilraith Valadhkani of Daggermark was disqualified after officials discovered illicit magical enhancement. The decision threatened to become a diplomatic quarrel until Fitzroy intervened, sat the offended archer down over a drink, and persuaded everyone involved that wounded pride was preferable to an international incident.

#### Mivon Takes Second

Navarathna of Mivon delivered a calm and technically excellent performance, earning second place.

Seamus also accepted a friendly exhibition against Florent Mayak of Gralton. Florent won, after which the two men shared an ale and discussed the matter with considerably more cheer than accuracy.

---

## ANTSON CRAZYFOOT TAKES FIRST PLACE

*By Tobin Bramblefoot, Games and Society Correspondent*

The official championship belonged to Antson “Ant” Crazyfoot.

> The targets were large enough. They were simply rather far away.
>
> — Antson Crazyfoot

The halfling archer faced the same distant iron, the same crosswinds, and the same unforgiving time limit as every other competitor. He simply treated them as inconveniences. Arrow after arrow found its mark, and by the conclusion there was no serious question of the result.

Ant took first place and secured the opening tournament points for Thumping Waters. Navarathna claimed second, while the remaining competitors were left to study their scores and reconsider every decision that had brought them within sight of Ant’s bow.

The Rushlight has only begun. Log chopping, boasting, and the Midnight Joust remain ahead, along with whatever surprises King Irovetti has prepared for his guests. The grand prize—eight thousand gold pieces and a valuable magical wand—remains unclaimed.

**AFTER THE FIRST OFFICIAL CONTEST, THUMPING WATERS STANDS UPON THE BOARD.**
