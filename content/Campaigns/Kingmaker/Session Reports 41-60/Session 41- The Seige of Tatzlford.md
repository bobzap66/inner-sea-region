### From [[Linzi]]’s Journal

*An account of events from early Calistril through 12 Calistril, 4714 AR, reconstructed from the testimony of those present*

[[Tatzlford]] still stands.

Yesterday, soldiers of the Republic of Thumping Waters and the people of Tatzlford defeated an invading army commanded by [[Ameon Trask]] in the service of Baron [[Hannis Drelev]]. The attackers included mercenaries, Tiger Lord warriors, and trolls. Their purpose was conquest. Their achievement was to leave a great many bodies in the marsh and give the [[Survivors]] a very long walk home.

The victory came at the end of a difficult beginning to the new year. [[Mairi]] Blackwood, the republic’s first Prime Minister, departed Thumping Waters on a private journey with [[Connal]] at her side. [[Amiri]] likewise traveled north in search of the surviving members of the [[Groups/Six Bears|Six Bears]].

[[Seamus]] has withdrawn from public affairs while grieving the departure of two dear friends. Those close to him have asked that he be allowed the time and privacy he needs.

Their absence forced an immediate restructuring of the council. Ally Grainger reluctantly surrendered the office she loved as Minister of Magic and accepted the heavier burden of Prime Minister. [[Tristian]] succeeded her as Minister of Magic, while [[Nok-Nok]] was appointed Minister of Intelligence.

The council also welcomed [[Velka]], a kobold warpriest of [[Hanspur]] whose divine vision warned of a great threat preventing free travel through [[Locations/Inner Sea Region/River Kingdoms/Stolen Lands/Hooktongue Slough|Hooktongue Slough]]. The Tubthumpers traveled to [[Tok-Nikrat]] intending to investigate when a mud-streaked and exhausted woman arrived with news that changed every plan they had made.

Her name was [[Kisandra Numesti]], daughter of Lord Tarrion Numesti of [[Fort Drelev]]. She reported that Baron Hannis Drelev had allied himself with [[Pitax]] and the [[Groups/Tiger Lords|Tiger Lords]], imprisoned her father for opposing him, and surrendered her sister Tamary to the Tiger Lords as a hostage. Worse still, an army under Ameon Trask was already marching against Tatzlford.

Velka directed the refugee toward the Tubthumpers. “They are the leaders of Thumping Waters,” she explained.

“What are they doing in this swamp?” Kisandra asked.

“Looking for trouble.”

“Well,” Kisandra answered, “they’ve found it.”

The Tubthumpers immediately abandoned their planned expedition and began the hard journey across the Slough. At the hidden temple of [[Shelyn]], they called upon the [[Locations/Inner Sea Region/River Kingdoms/Stolen Lands/Greenbelt/Kamelands/Tuskwater|Tuskwater]] Tailbreakers, who joined the march without hesitation. After five days of mire, cold water, and winter fog, the force reached Tatzlford on the twelfth of Calistril and found the battle already underway.

Antson Crazyfoot called upon the local wildlife to scout the enemy’s approach, allowing the defenders to meet the invasion prepared. Barricades rose across the likely avenues of attack, and firepots were distributed against the trolls.

The Tubthumpers took command of three defending forces. The Narlmarch Hunters fortified the town hall and rained arrows upon the attackers. The Tatzlford Guard met the invaders in the field and was defeated after a brave defense, but its survivors were not wiped out. The Tuskwater Tailbreakers held the line and helped rout Drelev’s army.

While those forces fought for the settlement, the Tubthumpers struck directly at Trask’s command position on a small island in the river. Magic carried some across the surface of the water and others through the air. They found Trask protected by two immense, troll-like bodyguards.

The fighting was brutal. The creatures’ claws and terrifying roars battered the heroes and badly wounded Wapati and Cinder, though both animal companions survived. Velka turned one creature’s fearsome cry back upon it, while the flames of [[Fitzroy]]’s enchanted helmet and Ally’s magic checked their regeneration. The Tubthumpers killed both guardians and burned what remained.

Ameon Trask proved less steadfast than the creatures guarding him. When the Tubthumpers reached his command position near the bridge, he abandoned it almost immediately and fled into the fog. His army, deprived of its commander and battered by Tatzlford’s defenders, soon broke with him.

The Tatzlford Guard suffered grievous losses and will require time, recruits, and aid to rebuild. It was defeated in the field, but it was not destroyed. Its survivors continued fighting beside the people they had sworn to protect until victory was secured.

After the battle, Kisandra renewed her plea. Her father remains imprisoned, her sister remains in the hands of the Tiger Lords, and Fort Drelev suffers beneath heavy taxes, hostages, and a baron who chose surrender and oppression over courage. She did not ask merely for Drelev’s defeat. She asked the Tubthumpers to win the hearts and minds of his people and give them the courage to rise against him.

Fitzroy answered with a champion’s oath: “If what you’re telling us is true, I swear we’ll bring your sister home or die trying.”

Kisandra gave Ally a jade ring identifying the Tubthumpers to [[Satinder Morne]], a priestess of Calistria and Kisandra’s ally within the city.

The defense of Tatzlford is over. The reckoning for Fort Drelev has begun.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
