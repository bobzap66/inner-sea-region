> *Compiled by the Ministry of Communication*
>
> #### Jeffrie and the Wild Hunt
>
> The archer encountered during the journey identified herself as Jeffrie of the First World. She warned that a greater Wild Hunt is approaching the region and described its quarry broadly enough to include the Tubthumpers and potentially the population surrounding them.
>
> Jeffrie supplied neither a date nor a clear explanation for the Hunt’s arrival. Her attack appears to have been a test rather than an attempted execution, but the opening volley nearly killed Antson and her spectral hound permanently blinded Wapiti until Velka restored the elk’s vision.
>
> The public edition acknowledges Jeffrie’s warning without naming the Wild Hunt. Further research into First World hunting hosts, their leaders, traditional quarry, and means of warding or diversion is urgent.
>
> #### The Pitaxian Lure
>
> Gaetane admitted that Irovetti paid him to spread the mass-poisoning rumor and accompany the Wardens assigned to the abbey. The false laboratory, staged footprints, and ambush force were components of a coordinated attempt to isolate and kill the Tubthumpers.
>
> Gaetane stated that Irovetti personally predicted the party would investigate the rumor. Future planning should assume Pitax possesses detailed knowledge of the Tubthumpers’ habits, priorities, and likely responses to threats against civilians.
>
> The Wardens used coordinated charges, flanking, heavy armor, and greatswords. Gaetane employed alchemy and a poisonous cloud capable of controlling much of the battlefield. Velka’s ability to clear the air prevented substantial casualties.
>
> #### Disposition of Prisoners
>
> Gaetane was retained for questioning. An ordinary captured Warden possessed no useful intelligence beyond his orders and was executed by Matteo to prevent him from returning to Pitax.
>
> This execution has been omitted from the public edition. Any official account should state only that the Pitaxian force was defeated and Gaetane captured.
>
> #### The Gardener
>
> The Gardener’s manifested form was destroyed, but the entity was not permanently laid to rest and was expected to reform within approximately one day.
>
> White Rose Abbey should not be declared secure. Access to the flooded cavern should remain restricted until the Gardener’s origin, anchor, and means of permanent destruction are identified.
>
> #### The Water Spirit and the Forgotten Sword
>
> The water spirit freed from the clock once guarded a legendary magical sword. The weapon had already been removed from the cavern before the Tubthumpers arrived.
>
> The spirit stated that whoever possesses her stolen shawl holds the key to locating the sword. Neither the shawl’s current bearer nor the sword’s present location is known.
>
> Public reporting states only that objects were removed from the cavern and remain under investigation. The existence of the sword and the shawl’s importance should remain restricted until recovery operations can begin.
>
> #### Sootscale Valley
>
> Reports of cult activity within Sootscale Valley remain unresolved. The White Rose Abbey mission did not address that threat.
>
> The cult should be investigated before it can exploit wartime uncertainty, kobold grievances, or the republic’s focus upon Pitax.
>
> #### Recommended Actions
>
> Research the Wild Hunt and Jeffrie. Determine how Irovetti predicted the party’s response and identify possible sources within Thumping Waters. Secure White Rose Abbey against the Gardener’s return. Locate the water spirit’s shawl and the forgotten sword. Investigate the Sootscale Valley cult.

## IROVETTI’S POISON PLOT PROVES A TRAP

*False laboratory at White Rose Abbey conceals a Pitaxian ambush—and genuine horrors beneath the ruins.*

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent • Additional reporting by Linzi*

---

A rumor that King Castruccio Irovetti had developed a weapon capable of poisoning entire communities drew the Tubthumpers to the long-abandoned White Rose Abbey. The weapon did not exist. The soldiers waiting to murder them did.

The company uncovered staged tracks and a false alchemical laboratory before six Pitaxian Wardens and a wererat alchemist named Gaetane launched their attack. The ambushers were defeated, Gaetane was captured, and the alleged poison weapon was exposed as a deliberate deception.

The abbey itself proved far more dangerous than Irovetti’s counterfeit threat. Haunts lingered in its bloodstained rooms, and a flooded cavern beneath the ruins held ancient wisps, a murderous ghost, and a water spirit imprisoned within a clock.

### A HUNTER IN THE NIGHT

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent*

Before reaching White Rose Abbey, the Tubthumpers were awakened in camp by a spectral hound and a mysterious archer whose first volley nearly killed Antson Crazyfoot.

The hound surrounded the camp with ghostly fog and savaged Wapiti, [[Fitzroy]]’s elk companion, robbing the animal of its sight. The company answered with spells, arrows, and cold iron until the archer called for a truce.

She named herself Jeffrie of the First World. Her purpose and allegiance remain uncertain, but she left the Tubthumpers with a warning of dangerous forces moving through the region before departing with her hound.

Wapiti survived. [[Velka]] later restored the elk’s vision with magic, and he has resumed the demanding work of carrying Fitzroy toward things no sensible elk would approach voluntarily.

---

### THE ABBEY AMBUSH

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent*

White Rose Abbey stands atop a vine-choked hill, abandoned long enough for its stones to acquire both moss and an extremely poor reputation.

The tracks approaching its entrance had been placed too carefully. Inside, the supposed laboratory lacked the convincing disorder of actual alchemical work. It was scenery arranged to support a rumor, not a place where a weapon had been built.

Six armored Pitaxian Wardens and Gaetane attacked once the Tubthumpers entered the trap. Gaetane filled much of the battlefield with poisonous vapor, which Velka cleared before it could decide the fight. Ally’s fire broke the enemy formation, and [[Seamus]] struck down the first Warden.

The remaining Wardens were defeated and Gaetane taken alive. No mass-poisoning weapon was found because none had ever existed.

### THE DEAD REMEMBER

*By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication*

The ambush had been false. The evil within White Rose Abbey was not.

In the monks’ old dormitory, the memory of slaughter rose around the explorers with such force that several were overwhelmed by supernatural terror. Blood and violence long vanished from the waking world appeared again as the abbey forced its visitors to remember what had happened there.

In the abbot’s study, another manifestation formed as a spectral sickle. Velka recognized the spirit’s anguish and calmed it before the haunting could claim another victim.

![[session-52-the-abbey-of-broken-bells-41.webp]]

*A spectral sickle manifests in the ruined abbot’s study before Velka quiets the troubled spirit.*

A hidden cache yielded medicines and weapons intended for use against incorporeal enemies. The explorers also recovered a volume of astronomical lore written in Aklo and translated it with magic.

---

### BATTLE BELOW THE WELL

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent*

A descent through the abbey well opened into a vast flooded cavern. Clear water filled much of the chamber. Blue magical pools glowed beneath the darkness, old casks rested above the waterline, and a precarious wooden bridge connected the remaining dry ground.

Six ancient wisps and a ghostly figure known as the Gardener attacked the explorers almost immediately. The wisps resisted nearly every spell directed against them, while the Gardener struck across the flooded battlefield with mental and spiritual force.

The fight became a struggle through water and air. Antson was brought down twice. Ally fell unconscious into the pool and nearly drowned before Fitzroy pulled her to the surface. [[Matteo]]’s ghost-charge bombs weakened the Gardener, and Velka’s moonlight finally dispersed its manifested form.

The victory secured the cavern, though officials caution that places haunted for centuries rarely become safe after a single afternoon’s work.

---

### THE PRISONER IN THE CLOCK

*By Sella Copperclaw, Civic Reporter*

After the battle, Velka consulted the spirits of the cavern and discovered a water spirit imprisoned within an elaborate water clock by the Gardener.

The Tubthumpers opened the clock and released its captive. The spirit had endured within the mechanism while the abbey above decayed and generations passed beyond the cavern walls.

![[session-52-the-abbey-of-broken-bells-42.webp]]

*The imprisoned water spirit rises from the opened clock beneath White Rose Abbey.*

Certain details of the spirit’s testimony have been withheld while the government investigates objects removed from the cavern before the Tubthumpers arrived.

---

### FROM THE EDITOR’S DESK

*By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication*

King Irovetti invented a weapon because he believed fear would carry the Tubthumpers exactly where he wanted them. He was right about the fear. He was wrong about what they would do when they arrived.

The poison story was false, but it still sent our leaders into danger. That is what makes a well-made lie useful: it need not survive inspection if it can arrange the battlefield before the truth arrives.

White Rose Abbey offers a harder lesson. Some horrors are fabricated by living enemies. Others are memories the dead have been unable to release. Wisdom requires learning which is which before either closes its hand around you.

We will keep asking who benefits from every rumor. We will also remember that disbelief is not the same thing as safety.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
