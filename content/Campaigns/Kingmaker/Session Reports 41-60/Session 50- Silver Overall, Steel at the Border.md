> ### RESTRICTED INTELLIGENCE REPORT
>
> *For council circulation only — Not for publication*
>
> #### Contacts within Pitax
>
> Matteo’s participation in the festival’s knife-throwing games produced information about a smuggling network operating among Pitax’s dockworkers. The network may provide a means of moving personnel or material through the city without royal scrutiny.
>
> Velka was directed toward Joffrey Vascari of the Riversong Trading House. The Vascari family exercises considerable influence over Pitax’s river trade, and many local sailors are reportedly indebted to him. Contact should be approached carefully but may prove valuable during future operations in the city.
>
> #### Kharne of the Rose Tower
>
> Fitzroy and Matteo sought alchemical assistance for Antson before the Midnight Joust and were directed to Kharne of the Rose Tower, a Pitaxian drug dealer and underworld figure.
>
> Kharne sold the party two moderate and one greater Dead Weight Mutagen. When offered additional money for confidential information, he refused. He explained that men in his profession do not survive long if they become known for speaking too publicly, and that his life and reputation were worth more than the offered gold.
>
> Kharne appears willing to conduct business across political lines, but he places unusual importance upon discretion and the keeping of agreements. This makes him dangerous, but potentially dependable within the exact limits of a bargain.
>
> #### The Death of Mairi
>
> Fitzroy learned from a Mivoni contestant that a rumor has spread beyond our borders claiming Antson Crazyfoot murdered Mairi.
>
> The source of the allegation is unknown. Given the timing, its circulation among foreign delegates, and Pitax’s broader effort to portray Thumping Waters as unstable, deliberate disinformation should be considered likely. The rumor should be monitored and countered carefully without drawing unnecessary attention to the circumstances of Mairi’s departure.
>
> #### Assessment
>
> The propaganda performance, the detailed knowledge displayed by Annamede Belavarah, the rumor concerning Mairi, and the invasion of Willowfen demonstrate a coordinated Pitaxian effort against both the republic’s security and its reputation.
>
> Future operations should assume that Pitax possesses informants familiar with Thumping Waters’ internal troubles. Kharne, Joffrey Vascari, and the dockside smugglers offer possible points of entry into Pitaxian society, but none should be trusted beyond demonstrated self-interest.

## THE RUSHLIGHT ENDS IN WAR

*Willowfen survives Irovetti’s surprise attack as the tournament concludes beneath banners of false friendship.*

*By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication*

---

The Rushlight Tournament concluded beneath a blaze of everburning torches, with Pitax claiming the championship and Thumping Waters departing with one victory, two second-place finishes, and the certain knowledge that King Castruccio Irovetti had used a festival as cover for an invasion.

What began with contests of skill ended with armies in the field. Willowfen still stands. The invaders do not.

**WAR BULLETIN**

Pitaxian soldiers, mercenaries, mammoth-mounted hill giants, and wyverns advanced upon Willowfen while the leaders of Thumping Waters attended the Rushlight as King Irovetti’s invited guests.

**The invasion was defeated. Willowfen remains secure.**

#### Inside This Edition

**Games Beneath the Banners**

Ally Grainger matched her skill against an unstable magical matrix while the delegation sampled the Rushlight’s less official contests.

**Second Place by the Axe**

Velka challenged Pitaxian champion Villamor Koth in the log-chopping field.

**A Boast Worth Hearing**

Seamus placed second after recounting the rise of the Tubthumpers.

#### The Rushlight at a Glance

**Archery:** Antson Crazyfoot — First Place

**Log Chopping:** Velka — Second Place

**Boasting:** Seamus — Second Place

**Midnight Joust:** Eliminated in Stage Two

**Overall Champion:** Pitax

### GAMES BENEATH THE BANNERS

*By Tobin Bramblefoot, Games and Society Correspondent*

#### Ally Beats the Matrix

The evening following Antson Crazyfoot’s victory in the archery championship offered no shortage of expensive ways to demonstrate courage, talent, or questionable judgment.

Ally Grainger wagered five hundred gold upon a challenge called “Test Your Arcane Credentials.” Within a velvet-draped pavilion, five translucent sigils circled an unstable knot of blue-white magic. Ally successfully realigned the entire matrix before it collapsed, earning polite applause from the assembled nobles and a prize of three powerful magical scrolls.

Antson placed a side wager upon her success with Saari Katan, a visiting noble who referred to the representatives of Thumping Waters as “rustics.” He lost that wager. The republic appreciates his contribution to our delegation’s spending money.

#### Boats, Blades, and Stories

Velka entered a children’s driftwood-boat race beside the river. Her vessel was constructed with determination, launched beautifully, struck the first obstacle, and sank. Its career was brief but decisive.

Matteo distinguished himself in an informal knife-throwing contest. As the event awarded neither tournament points nor diplomatic prestige, he enjoyed the unfamiliar luxury of throwing blades without anyone attempting to kill him in return.

Seamus joined a gathering of professional bards and storytellers. His dramatized account of the assault upon the Stag Lord’s fort survived a hostile heckler and several disputed details. The audience was entertained, which is the only result upon which all witnesses agree.

---

### A CROWN WORN SIDEWAYS

*By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication*

The evening’s official entertainment included a Pitaxian shadow play presented as a thoughtful meditation upon republican government. By remarkable coincidence, it concerned a queen who abandoned her throne while carrying a cursed sword southward and a smaller successor who placed the crown crookedly upon her head as wolves, banners, and unrest gathered behind her.

> Art, after all, asks questions that politics cannot.
>
> — Pitaxian master of ceremonies

The master of ceremonies assured the audience that this was art rather than politics. The Lantern and Ledger is delighted to report that Pitax has discovered subtlety and expects it to begin using some at the earliest opportunity.

---

### SECOND PLACE BY THE AXE

*By Tobin Bramblefoot, Games and Society Correspondent*

Velka represented Thumping Waters in the official log-chopping competition. Contestants were required to hew through four heavy logs using the axes provided. Magic could strengthen a competitor or weapon, but could not directly affect the logs or another contestant.

Velka attacked the task with admirable ferocity and finished second behind Pitaxian champion Villamor Koth.

Seamus attempted to encourage her by beginning a chant among the Thumping Waters supporters. Several rival contestants claimed that the performance constituted improper assistance. No disqualification followed, but the incident cost the delegation some goodwill among competitors already eager to suspect the newcomers of cheating.

### A BOAST WORTH HEARING

*By Tobin Bramblefoot, Games and Society Correspondent*

Seamus represented the republic in the official boasting competition, where truth was optional but commanding the crowd was not.

He told the story of the Tubthumpers’ rise from chartered explorers to defenders of a realm, recalling the fall of the Stag Lord and the defeat of Armag the Twice-Born. It was a sweeping account of battles survived, friends lost, and a nation built in territory that had devoured every previous attempt to tame it.

The performance earned second place.

Pitaxian bard Annamede Belavarah followed with a polished and merciless catalogue of Thumping Waters’ political embarrassments. Her knowledge of our difficulties was disturbingly precise, her delivery was excellent, and the crowd awarded her first place. A newspaper may object to the substance of a performance while recognizing the skill with which the knife was applied.

---

## NEWS FROM Willowfen

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent • Additional reporting by Linzi*

Before the tournament reached its conclusion, an urgent message arrived from Thumping Waters. A hostile force had crossed north of Lake Hooktongue and was marching upon Willowfen.

> The festival continued around us. For one more moment, only we knew that the music had become the sound of war.
>
> — Linzi, Editor

The attackers included Pitaxian soldiers and mercenaries, mammoth-mounted hill giants, and a flight of wyverns. This was not banditry, a border misunderstanding, or an overeager patrol. It was an invasion launched while the leaders of Thumping Waters attended King Irovetti’s festival as invited guests.

#### THE DEFENSE

The Tubthumpers could not cross the distance home before the invaders reached Willowfen. Instead, they issued orders to the Tatzlford Guard, the Lizardfolk Defenders, and Nok-Nok’s Irregulars, which moved to intercept the enemy before it could enter the settlement.

The defenders maneuvered around the mammoth riders while goblin bombardiers struck the invading formation. The fighting was fierce, but coordinated attacks shattered the enemy armies.

![[session-50-silver-overall-steel-at-the-border-image-01.webp]]

*High Marshal Valerie, commander of the armed forces of Thumping Waters.*

> Willowfen stands. Our soldiers have answered Pitax’s first attack, and every border command is now preparing for the next.
>
> — High Marshal Valerie

#### THE RESULT

The Pitaxian force was destroyed and Willowfen was saved.

The people of Thumping Waters owe their safety not to rulers standing beneath tournament banners, but to the soldiers who obeyed their orders in the field. Their courage transformed Irovetti’s surprise attack into a declaration of war written in Pitaxian dead.

---

### THE MIDNIGHT JOUST

*By Tobin Bramblefoot, Games and Society Correspondent*

The tournament continued despite the news from Willowfen. At midnight, six champions entered the torchlit Coliseum for the traditional drunken joust. Antson Crazyfoot rode for Thumping Waters after honoring Bixen’s Berry Best blackberry mead and accepting every magical and alchemical advantage permitted by the rules.

In Stage One, Ant faced Damanjot of Tymon and advanced. His small stature proved no impediment once armor, saddle, stubbornness, and extensive preparation were combined.

Villamor Koth, the previous year’s champion, received a bye during Stage Two. Ant instead faced Chantal Urena of Daggermark, an accomplished jouster strengthened by alchemy of her own.

The two riders charged. Ant attempted to drive Chantal from her saddle, made a disastrous error in the collision, and unseated himself. Chantal remained mounted and advanced to the final.

There she faced Villamor Koth, who claimed victory for Pitax. Combined with Pitax’s results in the preceding events, his win secured the overall Rushlight championship, its eight-thousand-gold purse, and the enchanted wand offered as grand prize.

---

### THE BANNERS COME DOWN

*An editorial by Linzi*

Thumping Waters leaves the Rushlight with Antson’s first-place archery victory, Velka’s second-place finish with the axe, and Seamus’s second-place boast.

**Instead, we return to a country at war.**

King Irovetti welcomed our leaders beneath banners of friendship while his armies advanced upon their citizens. His first blow failed. Willowfen stands, our defenders remain in the field, and the people of Thumping Waters now know precisely what Pitax’s hospitality is worth.

**THE FESTIVAL IS OVER. THE RECKONING BEGINS.**
