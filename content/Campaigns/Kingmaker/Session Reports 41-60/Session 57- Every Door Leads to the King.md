> ### Republic Chronicle — Restricted
>
> *Compiled by Linzi, Minister of Communication. Not for public dissemination.*
>
> #### Asmeranda Ilata: Intelligence Provided
>
> Asmeranda was confined within an unlocked palace room for approximately two weeks and compelled to perform for Irovetti, his officers, and guests. She remained observant throughout her confinement.
>
> **Confirmed or reported information:** The throne room lies south of the servant quarters. General Avinash Jurrg was Irovetti’s most dangerous remaining guardian. Villamor Koth maintained chambers nearby. Irovetti’s consort Engelidis has appeared differently on separate occasions, supporting earlier reports that she may be a shapeshifter or capable of sustained magical disguise.
>
> Asmeranda was directed to the cleared servants’ passage and should have reached the Red Crescent Theatre. Confirm her arrival when operational conditions permit.
>
> #### Throne Room Engagement
>
> **Enemy force:** Castruccio Irovetti, General Avinash Jurrg, Villamor Koth, and two Pitax Wardens.
>
> **Outcome:** Jurrg, Koth, and at least one warden killed. Irovetti escaped with Dimension Door after banishing Fitzroy into a magical maze. Exact destination was not immediately known.
>
> Irovetti’s proposal of single combat was fraudulent. He used performance magic to strengthen his allies before committing them to a general engagement. Future offers of parley should be treated as attempts to gain initiative unless he is already disarmed and contained.
>
> #### Razatha
>
> Razatha is an imprisoned remorhaz sovereign held within a frozen, bowl-shaped cavern beneath the palace. Translation magic enabled only limited communication through taps and claw-scratched symbols.
>
> Razatha confirmed hostility toward Irovetti and expressed a desire to deal with him personally. The party agreed to attempt to deliver Irovetti to her. She indicated the agreement by scratching out the image of a crown.
>
> **Operational warning:** Razatha is not an allied citizen or domesticated creature. Any release must be contingent upon clear agreement that she will not harm Pitax or its residents. The promise to deliver Irovetti alive must be weighed against the need to prevent his escape and protect the party.
>
> #### Lower Palace Threats
>
> **Fluffy:** Enormous spider-like aberration with multiple mouths and eyes distributed across its back. Inflicted persistent bleeding, incapacitated Matteo, and was killed by the party. Elowyn delivered the final blow.
>
> **Gargoyle torturer:** Associated with Fluffy and the lower torture complex. Status should be confirmed from field testimony before the lower level is declared secure.
>
> **Fountain-room defenders:** Two trolls, one gargoyle, and Alasen of the Cat’s Paw Marauders. The engagement was extremely costly; Seamus fell unconscious twice. At least one troll and the gargoyle were killed. Confirm the second troll’s status.
>
> **Alasen:** Severely wounded by Ant and withdrew after concluding Irovetti was not worth the fee. She was permitted to leave. She remains a dangerous weretiger mercenary and should not be considered an ally, but her decision may influence other contracted forces.
>
> #### Final Refuge
>
> A hidden staircase leads to Irovetti’s concealed private bedchamber. Irovetti and Engelidis were present when the party entered. The final engagement had not begun when the latest report ended.
>
> **Primary objectives:** Prevent Irovetti from escaping; identify and contain Engelidis; recover Evindra’s stolen shawl and the cursed fey blade; preserve Irovetti alive if possible without increasing risk to civilians or the party; search all escape routes before accepting any surrender.
>
> *Editor’s private note: The public sees a king with one room left. The people entering that room must remember that cornered men are not harmless, and cornered shapeshifters are not necessarily still in the corner.*

## EVERY DOOR LEADS TO THE KING

*Irovetti abandons his defenders as the Tubthumpers free a captive singer, survive the monsters beneath his palace, and discover his final refuge.*

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent • Additional reporting by Linzi and Mara Venn*

---

There are many ways to measure the strength of a ruler. Some count soldiers. Others count gold. In the [River Kingdoms](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/River%20Kingdoms.md), where both have a habit of changing hands with alarming frequency, there may be a simpler measure: count the people willing to stand beside a ruler when things begin to go wrong.

By that measure, King Castruccio Irovetti’s reign is contracting rapidly.

The Tubthumpers have pressed through the House of a Hundred Doors, searching its lavish rooms, servants’ corridors, hidden stairways, prisons, and chambers beneath the city. They freed the missing singer Asmeranda Ilata. They defeated General Avinash Jurrg and Rushlight champion Villamor Koth after Irovetti fled the throne room and left both men behind. They survived the palace’s imprisoned monsters and persuaded one of Irovetti’s mercenary captains that his gold was no longer worth dying for.

The search finally uncovered a hidden staircase leading to a private bedchamber. Irovetti waits there with his mysterious consort, Engelidis.

His circle has narrowed to one room.

### A SINGER WALKS FREE

*By Mara Venn, Managing Editor*

Asmeranda Ilata, the celebrated performer whose disappearance troubled the Red Crescent Theatre and helped expose the nature of Irovetti’s rule, has been found alive inside the palace.

She was not discovered in chains. Her door was not locked. The king had instead created the more polished variety of prison: a comfortable room surrounded by enough danger that leaving it alone would have been suicidal.

For approximately two weeks, Asmeranda was compelled to perform for Irovetti and his court. She used that time to observe the palace and its occupants. When the Tubthumpers found her, she was composed, prepared, and able to offer useful directions through the sprawling complex.

> He might be the most loutish person I have ever encountered.
>
> — Asmeranda Ilata, after two weeks in King Irovetti’s company

Asmeranda departed through the servants’ passage already cleared by the Tubthumpers and intends to return to the Red Crescent Theatre.

A royal court may compel an artist to perform. It cannot compel her to admire the audience.

---

### THE LIBRARY OF A MODEST MAN

*By Tobin Bramblefoot, Games and Society Correspondent*

The House of a Hundred Doors contains a private library remarkable for the narrow breadth of its interests.

Reported titles include *The Military Genius of Castruccio Irovetti*, *Why Everyone Loves Irovetti*, *The Art of Humility*, and *Life and Triumph of Castruccio Irovetti*.

Whether these volumes were commissioned by the king, presented by ambitious courtiers, or simply collected by a reader with very specific tastes remains unknown. The Lantern and Ledger nevertheless congratulates His Majesty upon discovering a subject capable of holding his attention for an entire shelf.

Several secret passages were found near the royal rooms. This may explain why the king’s literary collection contains so little suspense: whenever circumstances become difficult, he prefers an escape route.

---

### AN HONORABLE DUEL, BRIEFLY

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent*

The Tubthumpers eventually entered the throne room and found Irovetti waiting beside General Avinash Jurrg, Villamor Koth, and two [Pitax](../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Pitax/Pitax.md) Wardens.

Irovetti announced an honorable single combat. He then immediately used magic to strengthen his allies and allowed them to attack as a group. The king’s understanding of honor appears to be as flexible as his understanding of borders.

The fight was severe. Jurrg repeatedly used mental magic against [Matteo](../Characters/Matteo.md). Irovetti banished [Fitzroy](../Characters/Fitzroy.md) into an extradimensional maze, leaving the remainder of the company to fight without one of its strongest defenders. Ally answered with Chain Lightning, killing one warden and tearing through the assembled opposition. [Seamus](../Characters/Seamus.md) moved through the enemy line with sword and song, while Elowyn brought the full force of her new office against the king’s defenders.

General Jurrg fell. Villamor Koth, champion of the Rushlight Tournament, succumbed to his wounds and lingering flame.

![](../../../assets/images/screenshots/Session%2057%20-%20Every%20Door%20Leads%20to%20the%20King/session-57-every-door-leads-to-the-king-34.webp)

*King Irovetti vanishes from the throne room, leaving General Jurrg, Villamor Koth, and his wardens to continue the battle.</i]*

*Irovetti did not remain to share their fate. He escaped by magic, leaving Villamor with a final instruction to handle the situation.

Villamor could not. Neither could Jurrg. The king who demanded their loyalty had already gone.

---

### ONE TAP FOR VENGEANCE

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent*

In the deepest levels beneath the palace, the Tubthumpers discovered a prisoner unlike any held in its ordinary cells.

Razatha is a remorhaz sovereign: an enormous, armored creature of cold country, radiating terrible heat from between the plates of her body. Irovetti had captured and confined her within a frost-coated bowl of stone.

The Tubthumpers did not attack. With the aid of translation magic, they established a crude conversation through taps and symbols scratched into the floor.

When asked whether she wished to confront the king responsible for her imprisonment, Razatha tapped once.

Yes.

She then scratched the image of a crown into the stone and drew a line through it.

The exchange offers another measure of Irovetti’s reign. Beneath a palace filled with books praising his genius, one of his prisoners required neither speech nor a printing press to write the clearest review yet published.

---

### FLUFFY IN THE TORTURE CHAMBER

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent*

The palace’s lower chambers also contained a gargoyle torturer and an enormous spider-like creature known as Fluffy. No witness interviewed by this newspaper believes the name was accurate.

The creature possessed too many legs for comfort, several mouths, and eyes scattered across its body. Its attacks opened wounds that resisted treatment, and Matteo was brought down after suffering severe blood loss.

Ally accelerated the entire company with powerful magic while Fitzroy and [Ant](../Characters/Ant.md) maintained pressure upon the creature. Elowyn sustained the wounded through the worst of the fight and ultimately struck the killing blow.

![](../../../assets/images/screenshots/Session%2057%20-%20Every%20Door%20Leads%20to%20the%20King/session-57-every-door-leads-to-the-king-35.webp)

*The palace creature called Fluffy attacks amid the instruments of Irovetti’s torture chamber.</i]*

*The gargoyle’s subsequent complaint that the Tubthumpers had killed Fluffy did not produce the remorse he may have expected.

Palaces reveal the priorities of those who build them. Irovetti’s contains lavish bedrooms above, torture chambers below, and secret stairs connecting the two.

---

### THE PRICE OF A MERCENARY

*By Sella Copperclaw, Trade and Civic Affairs Correspondent*

In a chamber dominated by a golden fountain and a statue of Irovetti, the Tubthumpers encountered two troll guards, a gargoyle, and Alasen, leader of the Cat’s Paw Marauders.

Alasen entered the fight invisibly. She departed it with greater clarity.

The battle nearly killed Seamus twice and left the entire company badly strained. Ally’s lightning killed one troll, Fitzroy repeatedly placed himself between danger and his companions, and Elowyn’s healing kept the front line from collapsing. Ant finally struck Alasen with an arrow so severe that the mercenary reconsidered the remaining value of her contract.

> You think you can kill me?
>
> — Alasen, shortly before receiving a persuasive answer

Alasen withdrew. Matteo offered her the opportunity to stand down; Seamus instructed the others to let her leave.

This newspaper has always maintained that markets respond to changing conditions. Apparently, so do mercenaries.

---

### THE LAST HIDDEN STAIR

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent*

The search crossed the palace repeatedly—up servants’ stairs, down concealed passages, through bedrooms, storerooms, privies, libraries, dungeons, and rooms whose architects appear to have regarded direct travel as an insult.

At last, the Tubthumpers uncovered another hidden staircase descending to a concealed private bedchamber.

Irovetti was waiting inside.

Beside him stood Engelidis, the mysterious consort whose appearance has reportedly changed from one witness to another. Where soldiers, champions, monsters, and mercenaries have fallen or departed, she remains.

The door is open. The king has nowhere left to send anyone in his place.

---

### FROM THE EDITOR’S DESK

*By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication*

The House of a Hundred Doors was built to make one man appear larger than he is.

Its halls are grand. Its passages are confusing. Its hidden rooms contain treasures, prisoners, instruments of pain, and shelf after shelf insisting that Castruccio Irovetti is brilliant, beloved, victorious, and humble.

Yet every room searched has made the king smaller.

Asmeranda walked free. General Jurrg and Villamor Koth died after their king abandoned them. Alasen decided that survival mattered more than his wages. Razatha scratched out a crown. Even the palace’s empty arrow slits and neglected corridors testified that fewer people remained willing to defend what Irovetti had built.

A ruler may purchase obedience for a time. He may frighten artists, flatter champions, imprison monsters, and order other people to demonstrate the loyalty he does not possess himself.

But eventually every passage ends. Every guard looks behind him. Every hired sword calculates the price again.

And every door leads to the king.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
