> #### Pitax Horde: Updated Alignment
>
> **Withdrawn from Pitax:** Red Tusks under Korga Skulltaker; Ash Wolves under Veshka Bloodwind; Thunderfeet under Ol’nyr Mammothbreaker; River Vipers under Yelena Three Spears.
>
> **Remaining hostile:** Black Boulders under Drogon One-Eye; Moon Howls under Sivra Ghost Voice; Broken Fangs under Herrick Ironjaw.
>
> The four defecting tribes prevailed in the intertribal fighting but did not become Republican subjects or formal military formations. Their commitments vary and should not be overstated.
>
> #### Terms and Likely Conduct
>
> **Ash Wolves:** Veshka Bloodwind offered a practical alliance based upon demonstrated mutual aid. She is unlikely to accept centralized command or cultural interference.
>
> **Red Tusks:** Korga Skulltaker respects strength and has indicated that hostile forces will not pass unchallenged through his people’s territory. He rejects soft authority, flattery, and attempts to make his tribe resemble settled citizens.
>
> **Thunderfeet:** Ol’nyr Mammothbreaker seeks freedom of movement and noninterference. His agreement is best understood as peaceful neutrality rather than alliance.
>
> **River Vipers:** Yelena Three Spears remains committed first to her tribe’s survival. She acknowledges the party’s aid but has avoided binding promises.
>
> Recommendation: respect the independence promised during negotiations. Attempts to convert these understandings into compulsory service would validate Irovetti’s warnings and reunite the tribes against us.
>
> #### Loyalist Tribes
>
> The Black Boulders, Moon Howls, and Broken Fangs survived the internal battle but were defeated and dispersed. Their remaining numbers, current leadership condition, and relationship with Pitax require confirmation. They may return to Irovetti, seek revenge independently, or fracture further.
>
> #### Littletown Force
>
> The cinder dragon commanding the local drake formations was killed. The surviving force dispersed after its death. One wounded drake escaped the battle and is expected to spread word of the defeat.
>
> This likely removes the immediate aerial threat around Littletown but does not eliminate the wider wyvern presence. Individual hoards believed to contain Littletown’s stolen property remain scattered throughout the surrounding countryside.
>
> #### Littletown Site Risk
>
> No survivors were found. Approximately three hundred inhabitants are presumed dead. The combination of mass violent death, exposed remains, and prolonged abandonment creates a substantial risk of haunts or undead activity.
>
> Recommendation: dispatch a senior cleric with sufficient assistance to consecrate the ruins, identify remains where possible, and establish a protected memorial site. Recovery expeditions seeking stolen property should not disturb human remains or compromise the future consecration.
>
> *Editor’s private note: Publish the truth of Irovetti’s bargain widely. Withhold the locations and precise commitments of the defecting tribes. Friendship begins badly when announced as ownership.*

## FOUR TRIBES ABANDON IROVETTI

*Red Tusks, Ash Wolves, Thunderfeet, and River Vipers break from [[Pitax]] after a grand parley and bloody struggle within the Horde.*

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent • Additional reporting by Linzi*

---

Four of the seven tribes gathered by King Irovetti have abandoned the Pitaxian war effort after the Tubthumpers entered their encampment carrying the head of Kob Moleg.

The Red Tusks, Ash Wolves, Thunderfeet, and River Vipers chose to leave Pitaxian service following a midday council with the leaders of Thumping Waters. Their decision was immediately challenged by the Black Boulders, Moon Howls, and Broken Fangs, whose warriors attacked the departing tribes the following morning.

The Tubthumpers fought beside the defectors. After a chaotic intertribal battle, the four departing tribes held the field and began separating from the remains of Irovetti’s Horde. None has sworn fealty to Thumping Waters, but all four have withdrawn from Pitax’s cause, and several have offered varying forms of friendship, neutrality, or cooperation.

### SEVEN CHIEFS AT HIGH NOON

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent*

The Pitax Horde was never one people. It was a coalition of seven independent tribes forced into King Irovetti’s service by threats, pressure, and the example made of peoples who resisted him.

The Tubthumpers entered the Horde’s camp knowing that a single appeal would not move seven chiefs for the same reason. Seamus spent the morning learning what each leader valued and feared. [[Ant]] hunted an elk, which Ally prepared for the gathering. Elowyn arranged wildflowers around the meeting place. Ally then created a permanent magical diorama depicting Kob Moleg’s defeat.

At midday, the chiefs assembled: Korga Skulltaker of the Red Tusks, Veshka Bloodwind of the Ash Wolves, Ol’nyr Mammothbreaker of the Thunderfeet, Yelena Three Spears of the River Vipers, Drogon One-Eye of the Black Boulders, Sivra Ghost Voice of the Moon Howls, and Herrick Ironjaw of the Broken Fangs.

Seamus placed Kob Moleg’s head before them and made the matter plain. Irovetti had promised strength and protection. One of his most powerful commanders was dead, his mammoth host was leaderless, and the king had not been present to save either of them.

The speech, the evidence, and the spectacle achieved what force alone could not. Four chiefs decided that their people would no longer fight Irovetti’s war.

> We do not intend to buy frock coats and top hats. We do not intend to become easy to sneak up on.
>
> — Veshka Bloodwind, explaining that alliance need not resemble assimilation

### THE HORDE DIVIDES

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent*

The Red Tusks, Ash Wolves, Thunderfeet, and River Vipers began packing their camps after the council. The Black Boulders, Moon Howls, and Broken Fangs answered their departure with violence at dawn.

The fighting spread rapidly through the encampment. Ritual fires flared, mammoths stampeded between tents, warriors rallied around their chiefs, and families trying to leave found themselves caught between the tribes.

The Tubthumpers intervened on behalf of the defectors. Seamus rallied warriors with horn and voice while slipping among hostile ceremonies to disrupt them. Elowyn called spectral warriors into the battle and struck at tightly packed enemies. Ally broke a Moon Howl ritual with fear magic before turning Force Barrage upon the loyalists. Ant reached the panicked Thunderfoot mammoths and calmed them before they could trample friend and enemy alike.

![[session-55-endeavor-to-persevere-28.webp]]

*The four departing tribes fight through an attack by Irovetti’s remaining allies.*

The loyalists’ morale finally broke. The four defecting tribes survived their attempt to leave and began departing separately under their own chiefs.

Their leaders offered no single treaty. Veshka Bloodwind acknowledged an alliance without surrendering Ash Wolf independence. Korga Skulltaker returned to the hills with respect for the party’s strength and no interest in soft kings. Ol’nyr sought only freedom to ride with his people and mammoths. Yelena Three Spears preserved the River Vipers’ independence while recognizing the aid that had allowed them to escape.

That distinction matters. Thumping Waters has not gained four subject peoples or four new armies. Pitax has lost four tribes it compelled to fight.

### A CHALLENGE ACROSS THE ASHES

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent*

After leaving the tribal encampment, the Tubthumpers traveled by Umbral Journey to Littletown, the Pitaxian settlement reportedly offered to wyverns in exchange for their service.

They found burned buildings, empty roads, scattered bones, and no living voice. The monsters responsible were no longer gathered inside the ruins, but they remained somewhere in the surrounding country.

Vision Elowyn Vask used magic to carry her voice for a mile and issued a direct challenge to the occupying force.

> Wyverns of Littletown! The leaders of Thumping Waters are here, and they wish to challenge your leaders. If we win, you will leave—and if we do not, I suppose you get to eat us.
>
> — Vision Elowyn Vask

The answer arrived on leathery wings.

A huge cinder dragon descended at the head of several coordinated flights of drakes. Its red hide radiated murderous heat, black horns swept back from its skull, and smoke gathered around its jaws. The smaller creatures attacked in formations, washing the party with fire, acid, and poison.

The battle was severe. Simply approaching the dragon meant burning in the heat surrounding it, and the creature could intensify flames already consuming its victims. Ant’s cold ammunition exploited its elemental weakness. Seamus broke a drake formation with his Horn of Blasting and cut through another with a Perfect Finisher. Ally’s Chain Lightning tore across the clustered flights, while Elowyn attacked broad sections of the force and kept wounded companions alive.

The cinder dragon fell. One badly wounded drake escaped, carrying news of the defeat. The surviving Littletown force dispersed and abandoned the region.

---

### NO ONE ANSWERED

*By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication*

After the last wings vanished, the Tubthumpers entered Littletown and called for survivors.

No one answered.

The village once held approximately three hundred people. By the time the Tubthumpers reached it, scavenging animals had picked the victims’ bones clean. Homes and workshops had been burned or torn apart. Anything valuable was gone, likely divided among the scattered hoards of the creatures that destroyed the town.

![[session-55-endeavor-to-persevere-29.webp]]

*A burned child’s doll lies among the ruins of Littletown after the occupying monsters depart.*

Seamus recovered a child’s burned doll and a beam from what may once have been the town hall or a chapel. He intends them for a memorial to the people who lived there.

The ground must also be consecrated. A place containing so much violent death may produce haunts or undead if left untended, but that is not the only reason. Littletown should be marked because its people were not livestock, payment, or an acceptable price for military assistance.

King Irovetti purchased an army by giving monsters permission to consume one of his own villages. Littletown is what that bargain looked like after the king had received his soldiers.

---

### FROM THE EDITOR’S DESK

*By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication*

The leaders of four tribes looked at Kob Moleg’s severed head and reconsidered the promises King Irovetti had made them. The people of Littletown never received that opportunity.

Irovetti treats people as pieces upon a board. Tribes become disposable armies. Villages become payment. A king who calls this strategy may congratulate himself for never seeing the bodies closely enough to learn their names.

This republic must be different in more than the identity of the person giving orders. Our new understandings with the Red Tusks, Ash Wolves, Thunderfeet, and River Vipers are valuable precisely because those peoples remain free to define their own futures. They owe us neither frock coats nor obedience.

The dead of Littletown can no longer choose anything. We can only remember that the choice was stolen from them.

The doll recovered from the ashes will be kept. The place will be consecrated. The bargain that destroyed it will be entered into the record.

No king should be permitted to call three hundred lives a reasonable expense.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
