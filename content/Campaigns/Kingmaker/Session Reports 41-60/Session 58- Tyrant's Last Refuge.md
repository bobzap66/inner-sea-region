> ### Republic Chronicle — Restricted
>
> *Compiled by Linzi, Minister of Communication. Not for public dissemination.*
>
> #### Final Bedchamber Engagement
>
> **Enemy force:** Castruccio Irovetti and Engelidis.
>
> **Outcome:** Irovetti killed after surrendering. Engelidis escaped. Ally petrified and subsequently restored. No other permanent party casualties reported.
>
> Engelidis demonstrated an exceptional range of magical abilities. Confirmed effects included banishment into an extradimensional maze, petrification, lightning, cold, supernatural fear, stunning magic, and illusions capable of attacking a subject’s deepest convictions. She should be regarded as a high-priority hostile spellcaster and probable shapeshifter.
>
> Elowyn resisted an illusion constructed to convince her that Razmir did not exist. Fitzroy escaped the extradimensional maze and returned before the engagement ended. Ally remained petrified until transported to Annamede Belavara.
>
> Irovetti surrendered after being driven past his ability to continue fighting. Seamus, apparently still affected by hostile magic, perceived the kneeling king as Reyoknar and executed him. Whether Engelidis deliberately created the illusion or merely exploited an existing fear has not been established.
>
> **Assessment:** The killing occurred after an explicit surrender and must remain accurately recorded. Public reporting should acknowledge the facts without publishing operational details that would assist Engelidis or invite retaliation against Seamus.
>
> #### Engelidis at Large
>
> Engelidis entered the lagoon inside Irovetti’s bedchamber and used translocation magic to escape. Based upon the apparent limitations of the spell, her first destination was probably within several hundred feet of the chamber, but immediate searches using aquatic scouts, examination of nearby statues, and dispelling magic failed to locate her.
>
> No body has been recovered. No claim of her death should be accepted without physical or magical confirmation.
>
> **Recommended action:** Circulate her known appearances and magical capabilities to trusted officers only. Review unusual arrivals among Pitaxian nobles, refugees, household servants, and spellcasters. Because her appearance is unreliable, identification should be based upon knowledge, behavior, and magical examination rather than resemblance alone.
>
> #### Briar and the Recovered Vision
>
> A submerged crystal grotto beneath the bedchamber contained the cold-iron sword known as *Briar*. Contact with the weapon produced a shared vision.
>
> The vision showed a younger Castruccio Irovetti meeting a pale, green-haired woman in a moonlit garden. She offered him not a kingdom, but the opportunity to take one. When uncertainty troubled him, she touched his brow and removed it.
>
> The vision then invoked several threats that have shaped the history of the Stolen Lands: the Stag Lord, Hargulka and his trolls, Vordakai, and the Tiger Lords. The imagery strongly suggests that the same power encouraged, armed, manipulated, or selected multiple enemies of the Republic.
>
> The final image showed the woman alone in a hall of white thorns with a vessel containing golden sand. The words *What was taken must be returned* accompanied the vision.
>
> #### Identification of Nyrissa
>
> After Evindra’s shawl was returned at the White Rose Abbey, she identified the woman in the vision as Nyrissa.
>
> According to Evindra, Nyrissa is a nymph of extraordinary power who once defeated a Jabberwock and protected her First World domain through promises gathered from a thousand fey. She bears a grievance connected to the Elders following the exile of her lover, Count Ranalc.
>
> Evindra described *Briar* as a dormant artifact of near deific power. If awakened through exposure to suitable First World energies, the weapon may become intelligent and acquire a vorpal edge.
>
> **Current assessment:** Nyrissa may be the common influence behind several otherwise unrelated attempts to claim or destroy the Stolen Lands. Her precise objective remains unknown. Awakening *Briar* may provide both a weapon against her and a means by which she can observe or influence its bearer. Proceed only after further research.
>
> #### Restoration of Ally Grainger
>
> Annamede Belavara possessed the power required to reverse Ally’s petrification. Before performing the restoration, she demanded acknowledgment that she could accomplish what the Tubthumpers could not and a genuine place in Pitax’s future governing structure. Fitzroy accepted the terms under protest.
>
> During the negotiation, the Oculus of Abaddon compelled Antson Crazyfoot to fire upon Ally’s petrified body. The shot caused a hairline crack. Fitzroy partially intercepted the damage through a defensive reaction, probably preventing catastrophic destruction.
>
> Annamede successfully restored Ally despite the damage. Ally emerged without apparent knowledge of the attack or the negotiations conducted while she was stone.
>
> **Assessment:** The Oculus has escalated from cruelty and deception to compelling direct violence against an incapacitated ally. Antson must not be left alone with prisoners, petrified persons, state officials, or other vulnerable targets. Efforts to remove, suppress, or contain the artifact remain urgent.
>
> **Political consequence:** Annamede now possesses a legitimate claim to a seat in Pitax’s government, supported by an agreement made under emergency conditions. Attempting to deny that agreement would create an avoidable enemy and undermine the credibility of all future negotiations.
>
> #### The Offer to Razatha
>
> The Tubthumpers had promised to offer Irovetti to Razatha, the remorhaz sovereign imprisoned beneath his palace. After Irovetti’s death, they brought his remains to her in an effort to honor that agreement.
>
> Razatha declined to eat the body. Irovetti’s ultimate disposition has not been established in this report.
>
> #### Western Battlefield
>
> The undead masses encountered west of Pitax were formed from unburied soldiers and surrounding battlefield debris. Each stood approximately twenty feet tall, projected supernatural fear, trampled creatures beneath it, and animated discarded weapons to strike at extreme range.
>
> Elowyn’s Cloak of Light proved particularly effective, simultaneously healing allies and damaging the undead. The party destroyed both creatures without permanent casualties.
>
> **Operational warning:** Post-engagement examination suggested that anyone killed in close proximity might have been absorbed permanently into the amalgamation. Similar battlefields should be consecrated and cleared before civilian recovery crews enter.
>
> *Editor’s private note: We survived the king. That does not mean we have finished surviving what he did.*

## THREE MONTHS AFTER THE CROWN FELL

*[[Pitax]] rebuilds, Irovetti’s final hours enter the public record, and the dead remind both nations that victory does not bury a war.*

*By Mara Venn, Managing Editor • Additional reporting by Darya Holt and Sella Copperclaw*

---

It has been three and a half months since King Castruccio Irovetti died beneath the House of a Hundred Doors.

For those expecting victory to bring an immediate return to ordinary life, the intervening months have been educational.

> Wars end very quickly when written in histories. The decisive battle is won, the enemy ruler falls, the banners are lowered, and the author moves neatly into the next chapter. In reality, the wounded still require care, burned farms do not replant themselves, and roads remain impassable long after the soldiers who destroyed them have gone home.
>
> — Linzi, Editor

Both Pitax and the Republic of Thumping Waters are still recovering. Yet roads have reopened, displaced families have begun returning home, and the armies that marched beneath Irovetti’s banners no longer threaten our border.

The crown has fallen. The work left beneath it continues.

### THE KING WHO SURRENDERED TOO LATE

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent • From interviews with those present*

The final battle began in the hidden bedchamber where the previous edition of this newspaper left King Irovetti cornered beside his consort, Engelidis.

The chamber concealed a broad lagoon and still more of the king’s accumulated magic. Engelidis proved the more dangerous of the pair. She hurled lightning, cold, terror, and enchantments through the room. [[Fitzroy]] was cast into an extradimensional maze. Ally Grainger was turned to stone. Other members of the company were stunned or assaulted by visions designed to turn conviction into doubt.

Elowyn, confronted by an illusion denying the very existence of [[Razmir]], refused it.

> You cannot shake my faith.
>
> — Elowyn

[[Matteo]] moved invisibly through the bedchamber and repeatedly struck at Irovetti while Antson Crazyfoot, Ally, and [[Seamus]] pressed the attack whenever Engelidis’s magic allowed them to act. Fitzroy eventually found his way out of the maze and returned to the fight.

At last, Irovetti dropped his weapons and surrendered.

He did not survive the surrender.

Witnesses report that Seamus remained under the effects of hostile magic and saw, in the kneeling king, the face of Reyoknar—the man he holds responsible for taking everything from him. Seamus refused to permit that face another escape. He struck Irovetti down with a single terrible blow.

> He looks like the man who took everything from me. That man doesn’t get to surrender.
>
> — Seamus

The Lantern and Ledger will not print that Irovetti died fighting when he did not. Nor will this newspaper pretend that the moment can be separated neatly from the spells, bloodshed, betrayals, and private grief carried into that room.

Irovetti surrendered. Seamus killed him. Both facts belong in the record.

Engelidis escaped during the confusion. She plunged into the lagoon and vanished by magic. Searches of the water, the surrounding palace, and the nearby city did not locate her.

The king is dead. His most dangerous surviving confidante is not.

### A CITY WITHOUT A KING

*By Sella Copperclaw, Trade and Civic Affairs Correspondent*

Pitax has spent the last three months discovering how much of a kingdom can be neglected while its ruler commissions statues, private theatres, hidden chambers, and books about himself.

The answer, according to civic officials, is nearly all of it.

Food stores had been redirected to the army. Roads and bridges suffered from both wartime damage and years of poor maintenance. Soldiers returned to families with little money and fewer prospects. Merchants who had learned to survive by flattering the palace are now attempting to remember how ordinary trade works.

The provisional administration has concentrated upon restoring food distribution, reopening roads, accounting for the missing, and preventing revenge against citizens who had no meaningful part in Irovetti’s crimes. Annamede Belavara has been granted a place in Pitax’s developing government and has already proven difficult to ignore, which may be precisely the qualification she intended to demonstrate.

Relations with Thumping Waters remain cautious but constructive. Supplies and healers have crossed the former battle lines in one direction; refugees and returning prisoners have crossed them in the other.

No final settlement has yet determined Pitax’s long-term relationship with the Republic. For the moment, both governments appear to agree that roofs, bread, and passable roads are more urgent than arguments over titles.

---

### ALLY Grainger RESTORED

*By Mara Venn, Managing Editor*

Ally Grainger, Minister of Magic, has been restored to life and movement after Engelidis transformed her into stone during the final battle beneath the palace.

The Tubthumpers transported the petrified minister with extraordinary care to Annamede Belavara, widely regarded as the most capable surviving spellcaster in Pitax. Belavara agreed to attempt the restoration and succeeded.

Those present describe a difficult negotiation before the spell was cast. The exact terms have not been released, although Pitaxian officials have confirmed that Belavara will hold a meaningful place in the city’s future government.

> On your head be it.
>
> — Annamede Belavara

Minister Grainger emerged from the stone apparently unaware of how much time had passed during her affliction. She has since resumed her duties.

![[session-58-tyrant-s-last-refuge-16.webp]]

*Annamede Belavara restores Ally Grainger after the Minister of Magic was petrified beneath Irovetti’s palace.*

The restoration is being celebrated in Thumpington and Pitax alike. The Ledger gently suggests that anyone wishing to honor the occasion refrain from presenting the minister with decorative statuary.

### THE REPUBLIC REBUILDS

*By Sella Copperclaw, Trade and Civic Affairs Correspondent*

Our own settlements continue repairing the damage left by Irovetti’s armies and the forces he sent across our borders. Families have begun returning to abandoned homes. Trade has resumed along roads that were unsafe only months ago.

Soldiers have been released from service in numbers small enough that the borders remain protected, but large enough that farms, workshops, and fishing boats are no longer missing every able pair of hands.

The work is neither glorious nor quick. Walls must be rebuilt stone by stone. Seed grain must be found before fields can be planted. Widows and orphans require more than speeches. Veterans carry injuries that will outlast the war.

Nevertheless, markets are reopening. Hammers can be heard where siege engines once stood. The first caravans have completed journeys that would have been impossible during the fighting.

Peace has not arrived as a celebration. It has arrived as work—and the work has begun.

### THE WAR WILL NOT STAY BURIED

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent*

After three months devoted to recovery and government, the Tubthumpers resumed exploration west of Pitax.

Their journey brought them to an abandoned battlefield littered with the unburied remains of Pitaxian soldiers. Before the dead could be given proper rites, the corpses drew together into two towering masses of bone, rusted weapons, rotten armor, and grasping limbs.

Each stood nearly twenty feet high. Weapons torn from the battlefield rose and flew around them, striking far beyond the reach of ordinary arms. Their passage inspired supernatural terror, and their immense bodies threatened to crush anyone caught beneath them.

Elowyn surrounded the living with a cloak of radiance that restored her companions while burning the undead. Fitzroy held the creatures’ attention, intercepting blows meant for others, while Antson, Ally, Matteo, and Seamus tore the masses apart piece by piece.

The last of the amalgamated dead collapsed beneath decisive blows from Seamus and Fitzroy. No treasure was found—only bones, ruined armor, and the remains of soldiers whose king had led them to an unnecessary war.

![[session-58-tyrant-s-last-refuge-17.webp]]

*The unburied dead of Irovetti’s army rise together upon a battlefield west of Pitax.*

The battlefield will be marked for recovery and burial. Travelers should avoid the site until clergy and work crews declare it safe.

The war is over. Its dead have not yet received the message.

### FROM THE EDITOR’S DESK

*By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication*

There is a temptation, after a tyrant falls, to give him one final victory by allowing his death to become the most important thing that happened.

Irovetti would have enjoyed that. He arranged his entire kingdom around the belief that every room, performance, tournament, road, and human life became more significant when it pointed toward him.

Three months have made his error increasingly obvious.

Pitax still exists. Its performers sing without royal permission. Its merchants bargain without consulting a portrait of the king. Its people eat, argue, repair roofs, mourn their dead, and discuss what sort of government should follow him.

Thumping Waters still exists. The homes Irovetti tried to burn are being rebuilt. The soldiers he tried to kill have gone back to farms and families. The ministers he sought to humble have returned to desks piled with work.

Even this newspaper has other stories to print.

Castruccio Irovetti wanted every door to lead to the king. Now they lead out of his palace.

Let us see where the people choose to go.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
