# Pitax Hordes Betrayed by Foreign Agitators

![](../../../../assets/images/shared-cover-5d0935703bb5.png)

*Published in The Pitax Gazette, Lamashan 11th, 4714 AR*

### Republic Agents Sow Treachery Among Brave Pitaxian Auxiliaries

Reports from the front confirm that several auxiliary horde formations operating in support of His Majesty King Castruccio Irovetti's campaign have been lost following an unprecedented act of foreign subversion.

Witnesses describe agents of the so-called Republic of Thumping Waters spreading lies, false promises, and seditious propaganda among irregular troops recruited from throughout the River Kingdoms. These efforts culminated in a shameful wave of desertions as several formations abandoned their oaths and fled the field.

![](../../../../assets/images/pitax-hordes-betrayed-by-foreign-agitators-image-01.png)

Even more disturbingly, three remaining horde formations, enraged by the treachery of their former allies, were drawn into a chaotic and bloody confrontation. What should have been a united effort against foreign aggression instead became a tragedy engineered by enemies who lack the courage to face Pitaxian arms directly.

Military officials have condemned the incident as an act of political sabotage rather than a legitimate battlefield victory.

*"King Irovetti's enemies could not defeat these warriors honorably. Instead, they poisoned men's minds and turned comrades against one another."*

Despite these losses, royal commanders emphasize that Pitax's core military strength remains intact. Citizens are reminded that the kingdom has endured far greater hardships in its long history and that loyalty, discipline, and vigilance remain the surest safeguards against foreign manipulation.

Authorities have also urged residents to report suspicious activity, particularly individuals distributing anti-Pitaxian pamphlets, repeating foreign propaganda, or attempting to undermine public confidence in the Crown.

His Majesty has reportedly described the incident as *"a temporary setback brought about by treachery rather than valor,"* and has vowed that those responsible will ultimately answer for their crimes against the people of Pitax.

As always, The Gazette encourages all loyal citizens to remain calm, reject rumors spread by enemy agents, and place their trust in the wisdom and leadership of their rightful king.

*—The Editors of The Pitax Gazette*
