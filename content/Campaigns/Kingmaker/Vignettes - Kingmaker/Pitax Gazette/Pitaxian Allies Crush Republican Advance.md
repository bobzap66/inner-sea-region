# The Pitax Gazette

*Lamashan 4th, 4714 AR — Authorized for Public Distribution by the Office of His Excellency, King Castruccio Irovetti*

# Pitaxian Allies Crush Republican Advance

Reports from the eastern frontier confirm a decisive victory against Republican military forces attempting to interfere with ongoing operations near Fort Drelev. Combined allied forces under the banner of the Tuskriders inflicted severe losses upon the invading army, forcing surviving Republican elements into full retreat.

Witnesses describe the engagement as a complete rout, with disorganized enemy forces collapsing under the strength of the allied assault. Mammoth cavalry shattered Republican formations while seasoned raiders swept aside what little resistance remained. Though the self-proclaimed “Republic” has already begun circulating embellished tales of heroics and strategic victories, the reality observed on the battlefield was far less flattering.

“Their rulers fled the field with the rest,” stated one officer attached to the allied host. “The only difference is they expect someone to write songs pretending otherwise.”

The Gazette has also received credible reports that Republican leaders resorted to reckless personal attacks against forward commanders in a desperate attempt to salvage morale after the collapse of their army. Such theatrics may impress tavern audiences in frontier logging towns, but they do little to conceal the broader military failure now visible to all.

King Castruccio Irovetti, in his customary wisdom, urged citizens to remain calm and confident in Pitax’s continued security and prosperity. “Pitax stands as the beacon of civilization in the River Kingdoms,” His Excellency reportedly remarked during preparations for this season’s festivities. “While lesser nations exhaust themselves playing at war, Pitax continues to thrive through culture, refinement, and proper leadership.”

Celebrations throughout the city are expected to continue as scheduled, including performances, public feasts, and exhibitions commissioned personally by the Crown. Visitors are reminded that applications for the upcoming Rushlight festivities remain open.

Meanwhile, sources within Drelev suggest growing concern among local populations over the Republic’s ability to protect its own borders. Trade caravans have reportedly begun reconsidering routes through Republican territory following news of the defeat.

The Gazette congratulates the brave warriors who carried the day and extends its confidence that further attempts at Republican expansionism will meet a similar fate.
