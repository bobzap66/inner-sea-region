> ### A Lesson in Governance
>
> *Issued by Authority of the Crown of Pitax*
>
> Citizens of Pitax,
>
> You may hear noise from the west—dramatic accusations, wounded pride, theatrical outrage. Such displays are predictable when enthusiasm outpaces experience.
>
> The so-called Republic of Thumping Waters has grown fond of expansion and spectacle. Borders have shifted. Militias have multiplied. Alliances have been proclaimed with impressive confidence. One might even mistake ambition for stability.
>
> The Crown does not make that mistake.
>
> When irregular military activity was observed near contested corridors, Pitax acted with precision. Our forces advanced, assessed, and withdrew in disciplined order. No panic. No collapse. No confusion.
>
> It is curious that those who boast of preparedness object when others demonstrate it.
>
> We are told this occurred during a tournament, as though governance pauses for applause. A ruler capable of hosting a festival and safeguarding a frontier simultaneously is not duplicitous. He is competent.
>
> Let others print broadsides and beat drums. Pitax will continue to secure trade, enforce stability, and remind the River Kingdoms what experienced leadership looks like.
>
> **Strength is not loud. It is steady.**
>
> — By Decree of His Majesty, King Irovetti
>

*A broadside circulated in Thumping Waters after the Rushlight Tournament.*

**Citizens of Thumping Waters—**

Pitax welcomed our rulers with cheers, banners, and wine… while their mercenaries crossed our borders.

They applauded our victories in the arena while they measured the distance to Willowfen. They hosted a tournament while they marshalled hill giants on mammoths and a flight of wyverns. They offered us celebration—so our guard would drop.

***This was not sport. It was strategy.***

They came for our outlying homes first. Not because Willowfen is weak—because they thought it was alone. They wanted panic. They wanted us scattered. They wanted our people to look west and see only smoke.

Instead, they found steel.

High Marshal Valerie held the line. The Tatzlford Guard stood fast. The Lizardfolk Defenders fought like the river itself—cold, patient, and unstoppable. Nok-Nok’s Irregulars brought fire and fury to the field. The invaders broke. The oni commander fled with survivors crawling behind him.

Now hear the truth: **we are at war.**

Pitax will tell their people this was “an unfortunate border incident.” They will call it “misunderstanding.” They will smile and insist they were provoked—because lies are cheaper than armies.

Do not swallow it.

**They came under the cover of a festival. They marched while we were meant to dance.**

So we answer as a republic answers: not with fear, not with silence, and not with bowed heads.

**Sharpen your tools. Check your walls. Report strangers. Shelter your neighbors. Feed our soldiers. Keep the roads clear. And when the call comes—stand.**

Thumping Waters does not belong to kings. It belongs to the people who built it, bled for it, and refused to let bullies decide what “peace” means.

*—Linzi, Minister of Communication*

*Editor’s note: If Pitax wishes to accuse us of hostility, we invite them to point to the moment we crossed their border first. We will wait.*
