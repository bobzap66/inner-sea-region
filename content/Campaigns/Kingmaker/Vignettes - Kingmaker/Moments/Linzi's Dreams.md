# Linzi's Dreams


Linzi leaned back in her chair, tapping her quill against her lips, her eyes sparkling with excitement. The crackling fire in the hearth cast dancing shadows across the walls of her study, but her mind was far away, drifting to the bustling Republic of Thumping Waters.

"There's got to be a way," she whispered to herself, her fingers itching to jot down the grand idea forming in her mind. "A way to get the news to everyone... to let them know what true heroes their rulers are. If only I could make sure they all read it. If they could see, feel, and believe it themselves!"

Her quill hovered over the parchment, then began to move swiftly as inspiration struck.

"Imagine... something like a newsletter! No, something more... magical. A Chronicle of Heroes! Delivered to every home. No one would miss it—not a soul. It would appear in their hands, crisp and fresh, like a letter from the gods themselves."

Linzi grinned, already envisioning it. "Every villager, every commoner, no matter how far they live on the outskirts, could have the stories of their rulers’ brave deeds—right there in front of them. Imagine the look on their faces when they read about the battle at the Lonely Hills or how their baroness saved that caravan from certain doom. They’d talk about it at every market, in every tavern. They’d boast about living in a land ruled by such heroes!"

She could almost hear the murmurs of awe and excitement spreading through the village, the buzz of conversation, the shared pride.

"The Thumping Waters Heroic Herald," she murmured, tasting the name on her tongue. "No, something grander... The Song of Valor, maybe. No, no... The Chronicle of Thumping Waters! Yes, that’s it! A living record of every heroic deed—read by every man, woman, and child in the land."

Her mind danced with visions of the Chronicle spreading far beyond Thumping Waters, a symbol of pride that would unite the people, bind them to their rulers, and remind them every day of the valor that kept their kingdom safe.

Linzi sighed, gazing at the parchment before her. “Now... if only I could figure out how to make that happen…”
