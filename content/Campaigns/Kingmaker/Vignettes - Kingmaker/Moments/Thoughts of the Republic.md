# Thoughts of the Republic


Linzi, as excited as ever, shows up at dinner one night with a sheath of parchment. “I’ve been talking to some of the settlers and some of the folks that already lived in the Stolen Lands, and I’ve been asking them for their thoughts. I put together a random assortment of condensed thoughts from our people, and I’m pretty happy with the kind of folks we have so far! See for yourselves!” With a flourish, she spreads the parchment across the table.
Eadric
"There’s finally hope for real change. If they protect us from the trolls and kobolds, we can finally expand our farms without fear. I just hope the new government is fair and doesn’t burden us with heavy taxes."
Hilda
"The republic’s focus on community could mean more support for my healing practices. Defeating the Stag Lord was just the beginning; we still have many dangers to face. I’m hopeful that this new government will value and support healers like me."
Oswin
"With the Stag Lord defeated, the forest feels a bit safer, but the trolls and kobolds remain a threat. If the republic forms a militia, I can guide them and teach them to navigate these lands. I just hope they respect my knowledge and freedom."
Elfrida
"New settlers mean new customers for my textiles. The republic might bring fairer trade and more demand for local crafts. I’m eager to see what new materials and patterns I can learn from the settlers, but I hope we don’t lose our unique styles."
Godric
"Their soldiers and settlers will need my tools and weapons. With the bandits defeated, business should thrive, but I must be ready for increased demand. I hope the republic supports craftsmen and ensures we aren’t caught in constant conflicts."
Leofric
"These kingdoms have been tried before, a thousand times, probably. Calling it a republic don’t mean anything. We must ensure our voices are heard and our traditions respected. This is a chance to build a safer, more united community, but we must tread carefully."
Aethelred
"A republic means new trade opportunities and hopefully safer routes. I need to establish good connections with the new government to secure favorable trade agreements.
Wulfstan
"The Stag Lord’s defeat is a victory, but we still face dangers from trolls, dragons, and giants. A republic’s focus on collective security could make us stronger. I’ll offer my skills to help train and lead a militia, ensuring our village is protected."
Aelfwynn
"This feels like a new beginning with more freedom and opportunities. I hope the changes bring safety and prosperity to our home. But I’m also wary of how these new structures might change our daily lives."
Hereward
"The republic could mean a more balanced approach to managing the wilderness. With the bandits defeated, there’s room for settlers, but we must ensure they don’t exploit the land recklessly. I’ll work to protect our forests and advocate for sustainable practices."
Alistair
"I came here for a fresh start. If these so-called ‘ministers’ can protect us from the wilderness threats, I’ll help build a strong, thriving community. I’m ready to work hard and contribute to our new home."
Beatrix
"The wilderness is rich with herbs and plants I’ve never seen before. The republic’s focus on community health is encouraging. I look forward to collaborating with local healers like Hilda and learning how to use these new resources."
Cedric
"New buildings and homes will be needed as more settlers arrive. I’m ready to work."
Eleanor
"With the beginnings of civilization established, we have a chance to educate the young and build a literate, informed society. I’m eager to set up a school and help both local children and new settlers understand the importance of knowledge and unity."
Gareth
"The Stag Lord is gone, but the wilderness is still dangerous. As a member of the new militia, I’m committed to protecting our community. The republic’s values of justice and cooperation inspire me to serve with honor and diligence."
Ingrid
"The republic’s founding opens up new trade routes and opportunities. I’ll bring goods from the settlers to the frontier folk and vice versa, fostering mutual prosperity. Safety is still a concern, but I’m hopeful that the republic can maintain order."
Jonas
"There’s potential in these hills for mining. I heard about the gold strike at Crack Rock Cave!"
Livia
"More settlers mean more mouths to feed. I’m ready to build a real bakery and provide for the growing community. I hope the republic supports small businesses like mine and ensures a steady supply of ingredients."
Marcus, the Scout
"The republic will need to map and secure the Stolen Lands. I can use my skills to scout for threats and resources. We must stay vigilant."
Nina
"With more families settling here, a midwife like me will be in high demand. The republic’s focus on community well-being is promising. I’m eager to help build a healthier, safer environment for mothers and children alike."
