# Gershwin's Grattitude


Late at night, Gershwin toiled over his tiny forge, hammering horseshoes into their shapes. He had been making horseshoes and nails and plows for weeks now, a welcome change of pace from weapons of war. He glanced over at his newest prized possession, a faint smile crossing his face. He'd always been interested in ancient examples of fine metalwork, but such things were far out of the price range of a lowly smith such as himself. And yet....
After a few minutes of contemplation, he paused and started digging through his scrap metal, looking for the right substances. Not enough for a blade, but perhaps... With a genuine grin, he turned back to his forge, the glowing coals lighting up the night as he began his newest creation.
