# Oleg's Relief


As the news of the Stag Lord's defeat reaches my ears, a swell of conflicting emotions floods my heart. Relief washes over me like a cool breeze on a sweltering day, yet beneath it lies a current of apprehension, tinged with uncertainty.

For years, Svetlana and I have labored tirelessly to keep our modest trading post afloat amidst the chaos and lawlessness of this unforgiving land. The bandits, with their iron grip on the region, have been a constant thorn in our side, extorting us for protection money and threatening the safety of our livelihood.

But now, with their leader toppled from his throne of tyranny, a glimmer of hope flickers in the darkness. Perhaps, just perhaps, we can finally breathe easy, free from the shadow of fear that has loomed over us for so long.

And yet, even as I allow myself to entertain the possibility of a brighter future, a pang of guilt gnaws at my conscience. The Tubthumpers, the brave adventurers who aided us in our time of need, risked life and limb to rid our land of its blight. Without their courage and selflessness, this victory would never have been possible.

As I turn to share the news with Svetlana, a sense of optimism takes root in my heart. Though the road ahead may still be fraught with challenges, I know that together, we can weather any storm. For Oleg's Trading Post is more than just a place of commerce—it is our home, our sanctuary, and with the bandits vanquished, its future has never looked brighter.
