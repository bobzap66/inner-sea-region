# The Ballad of the Swordlord's Rescue


In a manor draped in opulence and grace,
A female barbarian sought a fearsome chase.
Through halls adorned with tapestries so fine,
She ventured forth, her destiny to intertwine.

Within the manor's walls, an ogre loomed,
A creature dark, in shadows it was groomed.
Yet, with sword in hand, the barbarian stood,
Her gaze unyielding, her spirit like hardwood.

A male bard, with a fife in hand,
Played a tune that echoed through the land.
In the heart of luxury, their fates entwined,
As the ogre stirred, a menace maligned.

The barbarian, fierce and resolute,
Her strikes precise, her strength absolute.
The fife's melody danced through the air,
A harmonious partnership, rare and fair.

The ogre's roars clashed with the fife's sweet song,
A symphony of battle, fierce and strong.
Through marble halls and chambers adorned,
The trio clashed, a tale of fate's thorns.

The barbarian's might and the bard's sweet tune,
A dance within the manor, beneath the moon.
With each swing and note, their victory near,
The ogre's end, a triumphant cheer.

In the echoes of the manor, victory's sigh,
The barbarian and bard, beneath the sky.
The ogre defeated, their triumph bold,
A tale of valor in a manor's hold.
