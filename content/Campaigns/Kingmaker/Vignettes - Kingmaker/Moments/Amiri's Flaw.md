# Amiri's Flaw


As Amiri practices her swings against a post outside the town hall, the giant blade snaps in half.

"NO!" she roars, her voice echoing like thunder. Her weapon, her companion through countless battles, the extension of her very soul—shattered. Her breath comes hard and fast, and for a fleeting moment, a rare flicker of helplessness crosses her face.

But then, the impossible happens. A faint shimmer of magic dances along the fractured edges, subtle at first, then growing brighter. Amiri's anger is replaced by confusion as she watches, wide-eyed, the two halves of the sword begin to move—like living things.

"What the—?" she mutters, taking a cautious step back, her grip still iron-strong on the hilt. The jagged edges align with a smooth precision that defies logic, the glow intensifying until the blade knits itself back together seamlessly.

The light fades, leaving the sword whole once more. Amiri stares at it, mouth slightly agape, her breathing still uneven. She cautiously runs her thumb along where the break had been, finding no trace of the fracture.

Her voice drops to a wary growl. "What in the name of Gorum’s bloody axe just happened?"

Suspicion creeps into her expression, her brow furrowing deeply. Amiri holds the sword up, examining it from hilt to tip. It feels... different. Not heavier, not lighter, but alive, as though the weapon itself had taken a breath.

She mutters to herself, equal parts wary and defiant, "If this is some kind of magic, you’d better not turn on me, blade. I’ve got enough enemies already."

Though shaken, her grip remains steadfast. Whatever this sword had become, it was still hers—and it had better prove itself worthy of her trust.
