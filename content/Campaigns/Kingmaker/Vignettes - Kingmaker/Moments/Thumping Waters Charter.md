# Thumping Waters Charter


Charter for the Exploration, Clearing, and Settlement of the Stolen Lands
Preamble
In the name of the Kingdom of Restov, this charter establishes the legal and operational framework for the exploration, clearing, and settlement of the designated Wilderness along the Kingdom's border. This charter aims to promote safety, prosperity, and the expansion of our community while ensuring responsible stewardship of the land and resources.

Article I: Designation of the Stolen Lands
The designated Wilderness, herein referred to as "Stolen Lands," is located along the southern border of the Kingdom of Restov. The exact boundaries shall be defined and marked by the appointed surveyors.

Article II: Purpose
The primary purposes of this charter are to:

Establish a permanent and thriving settlement within the Stolen Lands.
Ensure the safety of settlers from threats such as trolls, kobolds, bandits, and other dangers.
Promote sustainable development and resource management.
Secure the financial and legal responsibilities of the settlers towards the Kingdom of Restov.
Article III: Responsibilities and Obligations
Exploration and Surveying:

The appointed leaders shall organize and conduct thorough exploration and surveying of the Stolen Lands.
Detailed maps and reports of the land, resources, and threats shall be submitted to the Kingdom's authorities.
Clearing and Settlement:

The appointed leaders shall oversee the clearing of dangerous elements within the territory, including trolls, kobolds, and bandits.
Initial structures for defense, shelter, and essential services shall be erected using supplies provided by the Kingdom or resources found within the territory.
Settlement Governance:

A provisional council shall be established to govern the settlement, consisting of representatives elected by the settlers.
This council shall operate under the laws of the Kingdom of Thumping Waters and report regularly to the Kingdom's authorities.
Financial Responsibilities:

The settlers shall incur financial debt to the Kingdom of Restov for the initial supplies and support provided.
A detailed accounting of all expenditures and investments shall be maintained.
Settlers shall contribute a portion of their resources, production, and income towards repaying this debt, as specified in the financial agreement attached to this charter.
Article IV: Rights and Protections
Land Ownership:

Settlers are granted the right to claim and cultivate plots of land within the Stolen Lands.
Upon successful settlement and repayment of debts, settlers shall receive formal deeds of ownership for their claimed land.
Safety and Security:

The Kingdom shall provide initial military support to assist in clearing threats and establishing the settlement.
Settlers have the right to form militias and defenses to protect their community, under the guidance of the provisional council.
Support and Supplies:

The Kingdom shall supply essential tools, materials, and provisions to support the initial phases of settlement.
Regular supply routes shall be established to ensure the ongoing needs of the settlers are met.
Article V: Sustainable Development
Resource Management:

The provisional council shall implement policies for sustainable resource extraction and land use.
Conservation efforts shall be made to preserve the natural environment and biodiversity of the Stolen Lands.
Infrastructure Development:

Roads, bridges, and other infrastructure shall be developed to facilitate trade and communication with the Kingdom.
Public buildings, such as schools and medical facilities, shall be prioritized to support community well-being.
Article VI: Dispute Resolution
Mediation and Arbitration:

Disputes among settlers or between settlers and the provisional council shall be resolved through mediation and arbitration, following the legal framework of the Kingdom.
The Kingdom shall appoint a neutral arbiter to oversee the resolution process.
Appeals:

Settlers have the right to appeal decisions made by the provisional council to the Kingdom’s authorities.
Article VII: Amendments
This charter may be amended with the approval of a two-thirds majority of the provisional council and the consent of the Kingdom's authorities. Any amendments shall be documented and communicated to all settlers.
