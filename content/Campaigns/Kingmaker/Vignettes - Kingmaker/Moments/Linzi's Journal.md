# Linzi's Journal


"And the Prime Minister of Thumping Waters, the honorable Mairi Blackwood, slashed her mighty bastard sword through the Dancing Lady's neck, ending her pocket kingdom once and for all. Tristian, recovering from his momentary confusion, quickly called upon the power of Sarenrae to heal the Tubthumpers, ensuring they would survive to battle evil once more." Linzi looked up from her journal, her ink-stained fingers absentmindedly brushing her brown hair out of her face. She surveyed the growing settlement of Thumpington, amazed at the construction that had taken place in just a few short weeks. She knew that the rulers of Thumping Waters had been heavily subsidizing the construction out of their own wealth, but she knew that a portion of it came from the two thriving ore mines they had quickly established. She saw more new faces every day, and now, the capitol of the fledgling republic could be safely called a town, if a small one. She knew it would be up to her to educate the republic about the glory of their leaders. She just wasn't quite sure how yet.
