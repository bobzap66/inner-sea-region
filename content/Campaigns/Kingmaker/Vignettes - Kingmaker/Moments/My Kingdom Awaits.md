# My Kingdom Awaits


I’m tired of the dark. I’m tired of the cold stones beneath my feet and the dripping, damp air that clings to our skin like a curse. My people, my trolls, weren’t meant to live like this—hiding away in caves and underground tunnels, scraping by like vermin. We’re stronger than this, better than this. We could be so much more.

I dream of towers stretching to the sky, built by our hands. Of walls made of stone, not to trap us, but to protect us. Markets, bustling with life, where trolls walk freely alongside others—not as beasts or monsters, but as equals. We could build something greater than anything the humans could imagine. But every time I close my eyes and see that future, the same obstacle stands in my way.

The humans call the land above the Stolen Lands, as if they had any right to it. They took it from us. They pushed us underground, forced us to scavenge and fight just to survive. And now, they stand in the way of everything I want for my people. I can’t allow it any longer.

For too long, they’ve ruled the surface, treating us like nothing more than animals to be feared and hunted. But I will change that. I’ve gathered an army of those who share my vision— the creatures the humans consider monsters. We all want the same thing: a place of our own, a place where we can be free.

They’ll call me mad, a tyrant maybe, for what I’m planning. But what do they know of the life we’ve lived? What do they know of hunger, of hiding, of seeing your people die for scraps while others feast in the sun? I’ve seen it. I’ve felt it.

War is coming, and I’ll lead my army into battle with one goal: to reclaim what is rightfully ours. The humans will fall, and when the dust settles, we will build. We will no longer destroy out of anger or hunger. We will create, and my people will thrive.

I see that future as clearly as I see the stones of this cavern around me. And I will stop at nothing to make it a reality. They may call me Hargulka, the troll king, but when the Stolen Lands are ours, they will call me something else—the builder of a new world.
