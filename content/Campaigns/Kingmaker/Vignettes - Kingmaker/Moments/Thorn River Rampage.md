# Thorn River Rampage


The Thorn River Camp... it's been too quiet lately. Not a whisper, not a murmur from those idiots. And that can only mean trouble. Trouble for us.

The Stag Lord, he's not one to tolerate silence. Not when it comes to his outposts. That's the thing about him, he's not just a leader, he's a force. A force wrapped in the shadow of that damned stag skull he wears as a helm. It's like a grim reminder of what he's capable of. And trust me, I've seen what he's capable of. The mere sight of that skull strikes fear into the hearts of even the most hardened men.

But now, with the silence from Thorn River, it's as if the air itself is holding its breath, waiting for the storm to break loose. And when it does... gods help us all.

I can't shake this feeling of dread, gnawing at me from the inside out. What if something's gone wrong? What if Thorn River fell silent because of some unforeseen disaster? Or worse, what if someone's been poking around where they shouldn't be?

No, I can't afford to dwell on the possibilities. I need to focus on what's ahead. If the Stag Lord sends word for us to investigate, we'll have to tread carefully. Whatever lurks in the silence of Thorn River, I pray it's not our undoing. But if it is... well, let's just hope the Stag Lord's wrath falls on someone else's shoulders.
