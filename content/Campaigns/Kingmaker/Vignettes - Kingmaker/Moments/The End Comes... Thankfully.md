# The End Comes... Thankfully...


Harrim, a devout cleric of Groteus, stood before the gathered townspeople of Thumpington, his voice carrying a somber tone as he spoke of the fate awaiting all kingdoms.

"People of Thumpington," he began, his gaze sweeping over the crowd, "you live in the heart of the River Kingdoms, a land that has seen countless rulers rise and fall. These kingdoms, once mighty, now lay in ruins, forgotten by time. The mighty have crumbled to dust, their glory fading like shadows in the night."

He paused, letting the weight of his words settle. "You see, the fate of these fallen realms is not unique. It is the destiny of all kingdoms, of all things. Groteus, the god of the end times, teaches us that nothing lasts forever. Even the strongest empires will one day be reduced to rubble. It is not a question of if, but when."

The townspeople listened, some with fear in their eyes, others with a stoic acceptance. Harrim continued, "Do not be blinded by the fleeting illusion of stability and power. The wheel of fate turns for us all, and one day, this land, this town, will be nothing but a memory, just as the River Kingdoms' fallen thrones are now."

Harrim's voice grew softer, almost a whisper, as he concluded, "Embrace the inevitable, for in doing so, you honor the truth of existence. All things must end, but it is in understanding this that we find our true strength. Prepare yourselves, for the end will come—whether we are ready or not."
