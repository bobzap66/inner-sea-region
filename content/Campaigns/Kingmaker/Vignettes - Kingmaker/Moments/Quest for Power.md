# Quest for Power


Ally Grainger of Thumping Waters drifted into a restless sleep, her mind still alight with the day's arcane residue. But this sleep was different—deeper, stranger. She found herself standing in a place that shimmered between stars and stone, a space that was nowhere at all.

There, waiting in the half-light, was a gnome.

He was small, sharp-eyed, and flame-haired, with a grin that danced just on the edge of kindness and mischief. His amber eyes glowed like molten metal, and when he spoke, his voice echoed as though he were speaking both to her and to something behind her.

“I just want to make it fair,” he said, hands dancing in playful gestures. “You people are interesting to me. I want to see how this plays out. And giving you the power to heal your allies… well, it’ll make the game last longer.”

Ally blinked, caught between awe and alarm. “Who are you?” she asked, though part of her already knew the answer wouldn’t matter.

“Let’s just say I’m someone who enjoys a well-balanced board,” he replied, tapping his temple with a wink. “You’ve got a good story going. I’d hate to see it cut short.”

She wanted to protest. She wanted to ask what he meant by game, and you people, and heal your allies. But more than anything, she wanted to protect her friends. And deep down, she knew she needed more power to do that.

So Ally accepted his offer.

The dream faded as suddenly as it began, and when she woke, her mind tingled with unfamiliar magic—not destructive, but restorative. A gift from a dream. Or a pact with something beyond understanding.

Whatever strings might be attached… they could be dealt with later.

For now, her friends needed her.
