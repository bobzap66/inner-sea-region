# Don't Insult Happs


Happs was confused when he saw the prissy sod standing at the gate. When that cod-piece sniffer accused him of being a bandit, he was right mad. He might've been a bandit, but that didn't give no used wagon salesman the right to call him that to his face. When he came back, he would cut him from stem to stern. He'd burn the whole place down before he would let someone say he didn't deserve his position.
