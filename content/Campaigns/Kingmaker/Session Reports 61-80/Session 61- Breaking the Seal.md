# Session 61: Breaking the Seal

> ### Republic Chronicle — Restricted
>
> *Compiled by Linzi, Minister of Communication, from messages transmitted by the Candlemere expedition. Not for public dissemination.*
>
> **Report date:** Abadius 20, 4716 AR
>
> **Status:** The expedition remains beneath Candlemere Island. The information below is provisional and may be revised when the company returns.
>
> #### The Spiral Was Opened
>
> Lurk previously warned that the Pharasmian spiral seal beneath Candlemere was failing. No independent examination confirmed that claim before the expedition acted.
>
> Elowyn, with magical assistance from Ally, deliberately performed the religious rite that unmade the seal on Abadius 18. The operation did not merely open a physical door. It removed a ward that had prevented physical and dimensional passage into the complex below.
>
> **Assessment:** We must not allow later accounts to confuse suspicion with confirmation. We suspected the seal might be failing. We now know that our own ministers removed it. Whether that action prevented a greater failure or caused one remains unknown.
>
> #### Korog, Foras, and the Thresholders
>
> The expedition encountered an undead warrior identifying himself as Korog. He had become a Grave Knight but claimed to have served the Pharasmian force that sealed the complex thousands of years ago.
>
> Translation magic allowed the party to understand his warning. Korog stated that his brother Foras and a cult called the Thresholders remain below. Their work is connected to Yog-Sothoth, an entity associated with gates, thresholds, and passage between worlds. Korog claimed the spiral had prevented the Thresholders from escaping or completing their work.
>
> Korog accused the expedition of breaking the seal and then killing the guardians charged with containing the danger. The confrontation remained violent, and the party destroyed Korog and the two Grave Knights accompanying him.
>
> **Assessment:** Korog’s undeath made him an abomination to Pharasma, but it does not make his warning false. The later testimony of the Morrignas substantially supports his account. Planning should proceed on the assumption that Foras, the Thresholders, and their connection to Yog-Sothoth are real.
>
> **Recommended action:** Begin immediate research into Yog-Sothoth, the Thresholders, the Hall of the Reliquaries, and any surviving records naming Foras or Korog. Do not circulate these names outside the council and approved researchers until we understand whether speaking, writing, or studying them creates additional risk.
>
> #### The Morrigna Psychopomps
>
> Three ancient Morrignas confronted the expedition after mistaking its members for Threshold cultists. Elowyn persuaded them to delay combat and explained that Korog’s supposed Pharasmian defenders had become Grave Knights.
>
> The Morrignas appear to have entered stasis after reaching a stalemate with Foras’s cult. They did not know that thousands of years had passed. They confirmed that Foras and his followers remain deeper within the complex and directed the party toward the Hall of the Reliquaries.
>
> The psychopomps demanded the destruction of every suit of Grave Knight armor. After the party complied, they granted the expedition three days of protection and healing.
>
> **Assessment:** The Morrignas are presently cooperative, not subordinate. Their priorities concern the proper passage of souls and the destruction of undeath. Their sanctuary should not be interpreted as a general alliance with the Republic.
>
> #### Grave Knight Defenses
>
> Seven Grave Knights were destroyed in two engagements. Their proximity allowed them to counteract vitality-based healing. This nearly caused several deaths and forced Elowyn to rely upon elixirs, temporary vitality, and exemplar abilities that did not carry the vitality trait.
>
> Ant was knocked unconscious after the party broke through the first stairwell and entered the room beyond. A subsequent critical strike brought him to the point of death. Elowyn’s *Fish from the Fall’s Edge* intervened before death became final, and Ally immediately supplied additional healing.
>
> Ally destroyed some of the cursed armor with *Disintegrate*. Other suits were cast into a star-filled void elsewhere in the complex. The armor did not disintegrate, burn, or fall. It remains intact and appears to be drifting indefinitely through the void.
>
> **Recommended action:** Record non-vitality healing options for any future expedition. Assume all recovered Grave Knight armor remains dangerous until completely destroyed or placed beyond any plausible means of return.
>
> #### Oculus Compulsion Directed Against Elowyn
>
> While examining the separate chamber containing the star-filled void, the Oculus of Abaddon compelled Ant to push Elowyn into it.
>
> Ant acted upon the command. He approached Elowyn from behind and attempted to drive her forward by shoving against the backs of her thighs. Elowyn’s footing and physical resistance prevented him from moving her. She initially interpreted the contact as an awkward expression of gratitude toward Razmir. Ally recognized the attempted shove and struck Ant across the face after confronting him.
>
> The immediate fall would apparently have been interrupted after approximately ten feet, although Ant did not know that. From his perspective, he was attempting to cast Elowyn into an endless void. The distinction matters when assessing the Oculus’s intent.
>
> **Assessment:** The Oculus is no longer merely encouraging cruelty, deception, or abstract violence. It can compel Ant to attempt the immediate murder of a companion. The command was opportunistic and used an environmental hazard that Ant believed to be fatal.
>
> **Recommended action:** Ant should not approach pits, portals, exposed heights, prisoners, surrendered enemies, or vulnerable companions without supervision. The Ministry of Magic should investigate methods of suppressing, removing, or isolating the Oculus. Until then, the party must assume that any tactical position visible to Ant may also be visible to the artifact.
>
> #### Current Position
>
> The expedition is resting under Morrigna protection and has prepared to continue toward the Hall of the Reliquaries. The company has reported substantial improvements in readiness following the battles, but the nature and strength of the forces below remain unknown.
>
> **Immediate priorities:**
>
> 1. Maintain regular contact with the expedition while messages remain possible.
>
> 2. Prepare a retrieval or containment response if contact ceases.
>
> 3. Keep Candlemere closed to civilian traffic.
>
> 4. Prevent public disclosure of Yog-Sothoth, Foras, the Thresholders, or the Oculus incident.
>
> 5. Preserve every transmitted account. If the expedition does not return, these messages may be the only record of what was opened beneath the island.
>
> *Private note: I asked the printers to leave space for a later edition announcing that everyone came home. Superstition has no place in government, but neither does tempting the page by pretending I do not care how that sentence ends.*
>
> —Linzi, Minister of Communication

## THE DEAD KEPT WATCH BENEATH CANDLEMERE

*Republic expedition opens the ancient spiral seal, battles undead guardians, and finds unexpected allies in the chambers below the island.*

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent • From official dispatches transmitted from Candlemere*

---

On Abadius 18, five ministers and one newly arrived guardian crossed Lake Candlemere to investigate the ancient ruins at its heart. Messages transmitted from the field confirm that the expedition has entered the sealed complex beneath the island and survived two severe engagements with its undead defenders. They remain below as this edition goes to press.

Those defenders were not wandering corpses. They were disciplined warriors who had remained at their posts for thousands of years, sustained by curses, old obligations, and the conviction that whatever lay below them must never reach the surface.

The expedition has continued deeper.

**THIS EDITION AT A GLANCE**

**Candlemere:** The ancient spiral seal has been opened by a controlled religious rite

**Underground complex:** Previously inaccessible chambers extend far beneath the ruined tower

**Undead defenders:** Seven Grave Knights destroyed in two major engagements

**Republic expedition:** Several members seriously wounded; no deaths reported

**Unexpected allies:** Three ancient servants of Pharasma have offered the expedition temporary sanctuary

**Public warning:** Candlemere Island remains closed to all unauthorized travel

**Household inquiry:** Does keeping a minor elemental in the chimney reduce soot, or merely redistribute it? See Practical Enchantments inside.

---

### THE SPIRAL SEAL OPENS

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent*

On Abadius 18, the expedition departed from the eastern Tuskwater aboard a fishing boat chartered from Arvin, one of the few local boatmen willing to approach Candlemere amid reports of increased supernatural activity. Elowyn assured him that Razmir’s blessings would protect the vessel. Arvin reportedly accepted the blessing while maintaining the expression of a man reconsidering several earlier decisions.

Upon reaching the island, the company entered the ruined tower and descended to the enormous Pharasmian spiral discovered during an earlier expedition. The oppressive dread long associated with Candlemere remained present throughout the ruins.

Elowyn performed the rite required to unmake the seal, with Ally assisting in the identification and control of its ancient magic. The stone spiral vanished without the destructive backlash officials had feared, revealing a shaft approximately one hundred feet deep.

![](../../../assets/images/screenshots/Session%2061%20-%20Breaking%20the%20Seal/session-61-breaking-the-seal-13.webp)

*Elowyn and Ally unmake the ancient spiral seal beneath Candlemere Tower.*

The expedition descended by rope, flight, and transformative magic. Lurk, the strange leshy who recently warned the council of the approaching Wild Hunt, assumed the form of a large bog dragon and flew ahead to examine the darkness below.

At the bottom, the company found a complex of worked stone far older than any modern settlement in the region. Its masonry contains glowing seams, threshold imagery, and architectural features that remain difficult to explain using ordinary measurements.

The Ministry of Magic has declined to speculate publicly upon the complex’s original purpose until the expedition returns with a complete account.

---

### ELOWYN HOLDS THE STAIR

*By Mara Venn, Managing Editor*

The first attack came from four Grave Knights positioned beyond a narrow stairway. Grave Knights are undead warriors bound to their armor by powerful curses. Destroying the body does not end the threat; unless the armor is also destroyed, the warrior may eventually return.

The stairs permitted only a few combatants to engage at once. Elowyn held the bottleneck while the knights battered her with weapons and supernatural cold. Behind her, Ally, Antson Crazyfoot, and Seamus attempted to strike through the restricted space while Lurk’s bog-dragon form found little room to maneuver.

The defenders possessed another dangerous advantage. Their presence disrupted healing magic associated with vitality, forcing Elowyn to rely upon alchemical restoratives and other forms of divine power while under sustained attack.

Once the ministers forced their way through the stairwell and entered the chamber beyond, the battle spread out. Ant was knocked unconscious and then struck again before his companions could remove him from danger. Elowyn intervened at the final instant with a power she calls *Fish from the Fall’s Edge*, preventing his death. Ally followed with additional magic that restored him to the fight.

The Grave Knights were eventually overwhelmed. Ally used *Disintegrate* to reduce several fallen suits of cursed armor to dust before they could reform.

---

### ARMOR AMONG THE STARS

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent*

Deeper within the complex, the expedition discovered an opening into what witnesses described not as a cavern, but as a vast field of darkness and distant stars. Objects cast into it did not fall. They simply continued onward.

The remaining suits of Grave Knight armor were consigned to this void after the battles. They did not burn, shatter, or disappear. They drifted away intact, turning slowly against the distant light until they became too small to distinguish.

![](../../../assets/images/screenshots/Session%2061%20-%20Breaking%20the%20Seal/session-61-breaking-the-seal-14.webp)

*Cursed armor drifts intact into the star-filled void beneath Candlemere.*

Scholars consulted by this newspaper disagree about where the armor is now. Proposed answers include another plane, the darkness between worlds, an enclosed magical projection, and “far enough away to become somebody else’s problem.” The Ministry of Magic has endorsed none of these explanations, although the final proposal has attracted considerable public support.

---

### HE KNEW THE GATE

*By Mara Venn, Managing Editor*

The expedition’s second major engagement involved three additional Grave Knights led by an undead warrior identifying himself as Korog.

With the aid of translation magic, Korog claimed that the ancient defenders had remained below Candlemere to prevent a greater danger from escaping. His continued service through undeath placed him in direct violation of the faith he claimed to defend, but his warning was not dismissed merely because its speaker had become an abomination to Pharasma.

The conversation did not prevent violence. Korog and the other Grave Knights attacked, or continued an engagement already underway, and the resulting battle nearly killed several members of the expedition. Elowyn and Lurk were repeatedly brought down, healing magic continued to fail at critical moments, and the company survived only through careful use of magic that the undead could not suppress.

Korog and his companions were destroyed. The precise content of his warning has been withheld while the council determines what can safely be released. Citizens should understand that this is not an invitation to invent more entertaining versions in taverns.

---

### THE MORRIGNAS PASS JUDGMENT

*By Darya Holt, Traveling Correspondent*

The next chamber contained three Morrignas—powerful psychopomps charged with defending the proper passage of souls and opposing those who defy death’s order. The ancient beings emerged from web-like shrouds and immediately declared the expedition to be cultists deserving death.

Elowyn prevented a third battle.

She persuaded the Morrignas to pause long enough to hear that the supposed Pharasmian guardians had become Grave Knights. When the psychopomps learned how much time had passed and what Korog’s followers had made of themselves, they accepted that the expedition was not allied with the enemy they had entered the complex to oppose.

![](../../../assets/images/screenshots/Session%2061%20-%20Breaking%20the%20Seal/session-61-breaking-the-seal-15.webp)

*Three ancient Morrignas confront the expedition before Elowyn secures a truce.*

The Morrignas have granted the company temporary protection, healing, and a safe place from which to prepare for the chambers ahead. They have also provided directions toward a section of the complex known as the Hall of the Reliquaries.

After resting beneath Candlemere, the expedition intends to continue toward the Hall of the Reliquaries. Further reports will be published as they are received.

---

**PUBLIC NOTICE: CANDLEMERE CLOSURE**

Candlemere Island and the waters immediately surrounding it remain closed to unauthorized travel by order of the council.

This restriction includes fishing, salvage, religious pilgrimage, private magical investigation, treasure hunting, sightseeing, and attempts to determine whether objects thrown into reported spatial anomalies can later be recovered.

Boat owners are asked to report attempted charters to the nearest watch office. No reward is presently offered for proving that the rumors are true.

---

### FROM THE EDITOR’S DESK

*By Linzi, Editor and Minister of Communication*

A locked door invites two questions.

The first is what waits on the other side.

The second is why someone locked it.

The people who built beneath Candlemere left warnings, guardians, prayers, and an island that taught generations of sensible travelers to keep their distance. Those warnings were not enough to tell us whether the lock was failing, whether it needed repair, or whether opening it was the only way to understand the danger approaching our home.

Now the door is open, and the people we sent through it have discovered that even the dead disagree about what should happen next.

There will be time to judge the decision when everyone comes home. Until then, our responsibility is simpler. We keep the island closed. We prepare for what may follow. We refuse to turn uncertainty into panic merely because panic reaches the printer before patience does.

There is light beneath Candlemere.

For once, I wish there were less of it.

—Linzi, Minister of Communication
