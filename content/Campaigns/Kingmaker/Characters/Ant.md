# Ant

![[ant-portrait.png]]

### From the Journal of Linzi

Antson “Ant” Crazyfoot is a mystery wrapped in a green scarf and topped with a pair of antlers. If you ask him where he’s from, he’ll tell you “the woods,” but he says it like the woods themselves raised him—which, honestly, might not be far from the truth. He moves like smoke, talks like he’s half somewhere else, and sometimes I think he is—like his thoughts are in three different conversations and a knife fight all at once.

He’s quiet, sure, but that doesn’t mean he isn’t paying attention. Ant sees everything. The tiniest shift in a shadow, the way someone hesitates before speaking—he notices it all. And when he draws that bow of his, it’s not a weapon so much as an inevitability. You don’t even hear the shot; you just realize whatever he aimed at doesn’t exist anymore.

But underneath the mystery and the menace, there’s a heart beating in there—small, fierce, and surprisingly loyal. He pretends he doesn’t care about anyone, but he’s always the first to move when one of us is in danger. He’s haunted, sure, and the eye of Abaddon gleaming in his skull hasn’t made him any less unnerving—but I think he’s still fighting to stay himself. And if you ask me, that’s what makes him a hero worth writing about.
