# Lorcan

![](../../../../assets/images/lorcan-portrait.png)

After [Thumpington](../../../../Locations/Inner%20Sea%20Region/River%20Kingdoms/Stolen%20Lands/Thumping%20Waters/Settlements/Thumpington/Thumpington.md) was attacked by The Beast, the Tubthumpers tracked it down and avenged their kingdom. In The Beast's cave, they discovered is tiny offspring. [Mairi Blackwood](../Mairi.md) didn't have the heart to kill it with it being so young or leave it to starve to death. She brought it home and has been attempting to train it. It could be an asset to Thumping Waters to have something so potentially fierce on their side should trouble come calling, if it can in fact be trained. Thus far, it has been moderately receptive to training.
