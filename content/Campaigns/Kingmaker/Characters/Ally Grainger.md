# Ally Grainger

![](../../../assets/images/ally-grainger-portrait.jpg)

### From the Journal of Linzi

Oh, Ally! If the Republic of Thumping Waters has a heart, it beats to her rhythm—though she’d probably insist it’s all coincidence and hard work. When I first met her, I thought she was just another scrappy adventurer with a funny cat and too many belts (she does have a lot of belts), but there’s more to her than that. She’s the kind of person who walks into a storm and somehow convinces it to behave itself.

Don’t let her size or her soft voice fool you—she’s a powerhouse of will, sharper than any spell she casts. I’ve watched her stare down kings, monsters, and one very angry dryad without blinking. The people love her because she listens, really listens, and because she’s not afraid to admit when she’s wrong—though that doesn’t happen often, mind you.

Now that she’s Prime Minister, she’s got the weight of a nation on her shoulders, and still she finds time to check in on everyone else. Sometimes I wonder if she ever stops to take care of herself… but then her cat climbs onto her lap, and I remember—maybe that’s how she keeps her balance.
